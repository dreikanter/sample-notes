# March end reflection

Last day of March. Accounting.

The month felt productive in a quieter register than January and February. Those months had the energy of new starts; March had the energy of consolidation — things working, habits established, the croissant not being a crisis anymore.

**Technical**

API gateway implementation is underway. The Go service handles auth and routing; rate limiting next. See [[20260312_2072]] for the architecture decision.

The static site generator has tag pages now — the piece that was missing from the February design. I used it to rebuild my personal site. It's fast; rebuilds in about 80ms for a 40-page site.

**Growing things**

Tomatoes are in 4-inch pots and looking strong. Basil is pinched and bushy. The shishito peppers are small but alive. More seedling updates in [[20260316_2074]].

**Reading**

Finished *GEB*. Currently between books in a way that feels like rest rather than neglect. Reading some long-form online pieces: the Graeber essay on bureaucracy (https://thebaffler.com/salvos/of-flying-cars-and-the-declining-rate-of-profit), an old Borges I'd missed.

**Physical**

Running at 30 miles per week now. Spring miles are easier than winter miles. The body cooperates more. Fixed the bike finally — took it to the shop rather than attempting the derailleur myself. Worth the $40.

**One thing that mattered**

The concert last week (see [[20260319_2076]]). Small room, quiet playing. There are things that don't get replicated anywhere else.

April is all spring.
