# Focaccia recipe notes

I've been working toward a reliable focaccia for three months. This version is now the one I make without consulting anything.

**Formula**

- 500g bread flour
- 425g water (85% hydration — wetter than you think you need)
- 7g instant yeast
- 10g salt
- 50g olive oil (in the dough), plus more for the pan

**Method**

Mix flour, water, yeast, salt into a shaggy mass. Cover, rest 15 minutes. Add 25g of the olive oil, incorporate. Stretch and fold four times over 2 hours. The dough will become smooth and extensible.

Oil a quarter sheet pan generously with the remaining 25g olive oil. Turn dough into pan, cover, refrigerate overnight (8–12 hours). This slow cold ferment develops flavor.

Next day: remove from fridge, let come to room temperature 2 hours. Dimple aggressively with oiled fingers — this isn't gentle, push all the way to the pan. Top: flaky salt (Maldon), rosemary (optional), whatever else you want. Bake at 450°F for 20–25 minutes until deeply golden.

**Why it works**

High hydration dough + oil in the pan = that crispy-bottom, open-crumbed, chewy interior. The overnight retard is not optional for flavor.

**Variations tried**

- Potato slices (par-cooked) and fresh thyme
- Red onion and olive
- Tomato and basil (add tomatoes for the last 10 minutes only)

The base is the thing. Toppings are secondary.
