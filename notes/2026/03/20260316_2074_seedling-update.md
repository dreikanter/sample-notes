# Seedling update – week two

Two weeks since seeding (see [[20260301_2066]]). Status check.

**Tomatoes (Brandywine)**

Six cells, six germinated. All have true leaves now — the first two "seed leaves" (cotyledons) are present plus one set of true leaves emerging. Good color, upright, not leggy. The light is doing its job.

Thinned to one per cell (removed the weaker seedling by snipping, not pulling, to avoid disturbing roots). This is harder emotionally than it sounds.

**Tomatoes (Sun Gold)**

Same status. Sun Gold seedlings look slightly more vigorous than Brandywine at this stage, which matches what I've read — cherry types tend to be faster germinators and early growers.

**Basil**

Germinated densely, as expected. Didn't thin yet — will wait until the seedlings are an inch tall and then thin to the strongest ones, about 1 inch apart in the flat.

**Shishito pepper**

Seven of twelve cells germinated. The other five: planted two more seeds per cell as insurance.

**Environmental notes**

Heat mat has been at 70°F since germination. Light is on 15 hours, off 9 hours. Watering from the bottom every 2–3 days — the tray gets about half an inch of water and the seedlings draw up what they need.

**First transplant**

Tomatoes will need to go to 4-inch pots in about two weeks when they've developed their second set of true leaves. Sun Gold first.

Seedling care: https://extension.umn.edu/planting-and-growing-guides/starting-seeds-indoors
