# Seed starting log – March 2026

Started seeds indoors on March 1. Six weeks before last frost date (approximately April 15 for this zone).

**Started today**

- *Tomatoes*: 6 cells of Brandywine, 6 cells of Sun Gold cherry. Sowed 2 seeds per cell, will thin to one.
- *Basil*: Sweet basil, half a packet. Dense sowing in a flat, will transplant in batches.
- *Pepper*: One variety, Shishito. Peppers are slow germinators; starting now is barely early enough.

**Setup**

72-cell seed tray under a full-spectrum grow light on a 16-hour cycle. Tray on a heat mat set to 75°F — bottom heat speeds germination significantly. No light until germination; seedlings need warmth, not light, until they break soil.

Soil: seed-starting mix, not regular potting soil. Seed starting mix is finer, drains faster, and doesn't compact around tender roots.

**Expectations**

Tomatoes should germinate in 5–7 days. Basil faster — 3–5 days. Peppers slower — 10–14 days.

After germination: lower the heat mat to 70°F, keep the light on 14–16 hours. Water from the bottom (fill the tray, not from above) to encourage roots to grow down.

**What I'm not starting**

Cucumbers, squash, beans — direct sow after last frost. They transplant poorly.
