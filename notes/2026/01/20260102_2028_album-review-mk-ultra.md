# Album review: Mk.gee – Two Star & the Dream Police

I came to this record six months late, pointed toward it by two different people in the same week. That kind of convergence usually means something.

*Two Star & the Dream Police* (2024) is Mk.gee's debut album — Michael Gordon, who plays guitar in a way that suggests he's heard every possible note that guitar has produced and has opinions about all of them. The album sounds like 1978 AOR and 1998 shoegaze and some third thing that doesn't have a name yet, all at once. This is not an accident.

Opener "Candy" establishes the template: a languorous tempo, guitar tones that bloom and recede like something heard through a wall, vocals that stay flat in the mix because the flatness is the point. "I feel a little love / I feel a little more" — the lyric is simple enough that you can load it with whatever you need.

The production is the main event. Everything has a patina, a slightly degraded warmth that recalls a cassette left in a car. But it's not lo-fi fetishism; the mix is actually precise. There's a lot happening if you listen on headphones.

The album falls off slightly in the back half, where a few tracks coast on atmosphere without the melodic anchor that the best moments have. But "Are We Gonna Be Alright" sticks around in the head for days.
