# Matcha latte at home

Been making matcha at home more seriously since January started. The setup I've landed on is pretty simple once you stop overthinking the water temperature.

The main thing I got wrong for months was using boiling water. The recommended range is 70–80°C, and anything hotter makes the matcha taste bitter and grassy in an unpleasant way. I now just boil the kettle and leave it for about four minutes before pouring — rough but it works.

My current process: sift about 1.5 grams of matcha into the bowl, add around 50ml of the cooled water, whisk in a W motion until frothy with no lumps, then add 150ml of steamed oat milk. The brand I've been using is Ippodo's Ummon, which I order from their site (https://global.ippodo-tea.co.jp). It's not cheap but the tin lasts a while if you're having one a day.

Equipment-wise I bought a bamboo chasen (whisk) off a Japanese supplier on Etsy — the 80-strand version, not the 100-strand, which is apparently more for thicker ceremonial matcha. The chasen sits in a ceramic holder to keep the tines curved.

A few things I still want to try: cold matcha with sparkling water and a drop of citrus, and a matcha syrup for baking. Saw a recipe for matcha shortbread that looked solid.
