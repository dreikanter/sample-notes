# Indoor plant winter care notes

January check-in on the apartment plants. We're at minimum light levels — the southwest window gets about four hours of direct sun on clear days, which are rare in January.

**Status by plant**

*Monstera deliciosa* — fine. These things are indestructible. New leaf unfurling slowly, which is normal. Watered once every twelve days roughly.

*Fiddle leaf fig* — struggling. Three leaves yellowed and dropped in December. This is probably a combination of lower light, my moving it to dust it (they hate being moved), and possibly root-bound stress. Considering upsizing the pot in March.

*Pothos (golden)* — thriving regardless. Long trailing vines. No complaints.

*Bird of paradise* — dormant-ish. Not growing, not dying. Just waiting for March. Watering once every three weeks.

*Snake plant (sansevieria)* — also fine, also indestructible.

*Peace lily* — flagging. Soil moisture seems right but the leaves are drooping more than normal. May be a humidity issue — the apartment gets dry in winter with forced-air heat.

**Changes made**

Added a humidifier near the peace lily. Moved the fiddle leaf fig two feet closer to the window. Stopped fertilizing everything for winter — they're not growing and feeding them now just builds up salts in the soil.

**Spring prep**

March: repot the fiddle leaf, top-dress others with fresh compost.
