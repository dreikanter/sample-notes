# Café review: Corner Espresso

Finally went to Corner Espresso, which opened in October and which three different people have mentioned to me. The space is on the ground floor of what used to be a print shop; they kept the wide windows and the exposed brick wall. Good decision.

**Coffee**: They're using a rotating single origin on the espresso, currently a washed Ethiopian from a producer in Yirgacheffe. The espresso was pulled long — closer to a lungo than a traditional pull, I think intentionally. Bright, almost tea-like, stone fruit in the mid-palate. Not for people who want a dark, bitter espresso. For people who like to actually taste the coffee, it's excellent.

The oat milk flat white was well-textured, good latte art (this shouldn't matter but does, aesthetically). The oat milk was the barista's own house blend of two commercial brands, which he explained unprompted. I respect the specificity.

**Space**: Six seats at the bar, four two-tops, one larger communal table near the back. Loud on a Saturday morning — the ceiling is high and there's nothing soft to absorb sound. Acceptable on a weekday.

**Wi-Fi and working**: Single power strip along one wall, a few seats near it. Not set up as a work café but you could manage.

**Price**: Espresso $4.50, flat white $6.50. Reasonable for the quality.

Would return. Particularly on a weekday morning when it's quieter.

Their bean sourcing: https://www.intelligentsiacoffee.com (they're an Intelligentsia partner)
