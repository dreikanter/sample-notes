# January first intentions

The word "resolution" has been poisoned by its own failure rate. Everyone knows that by February, most resolutions have collapsed under the weight of the gap between the self you imagine on January 1 and the actual person who has to get up at 7 AM in the cold.

So I'm calling these intentions instead. Looser. More honest about what they are.

**Write more by hand.** I've been almost entirely screen-based for years and I miss the slowness of ink. Not journaling necessarily — just capturing. A pocket notebook, cheap, no attachment to it.

**Cook something I find intimidating each month.** January: croissants (mentioned in [[20251231_2024]]). February: fresh pasta from scratch. The goal is to stop being precious about difficult things in the kitchen.

**Talk to the people I keep meaning to talk to.** This one is embarrassingly simple. I have a list of six people I've let too many months go by without contacting. The barrier is zero; I've just let inertia win.

**Read before looking at my phone in the morning.** Not always possible, but most mornings. Even fifteen minutes.

These are modest. That's intentional. The goal is to end the year able to say honestly that I did them, not to post about them in February and forget by March.
