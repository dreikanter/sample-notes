# Ricotta from scratch

Made ricotta today. It took 30 minutes and the result was better than anything from the grocery store.

**Ingredients**

- 1 gallon whole milk (not ultra-pasteurized — UHT won't curdle properly)
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 3 tablespoons white wine vinegar (or lemon juice — vinegar gives a cleaner flavor)

**Method**

Combine milk, cream, and salt in a pot. Heat over medium, stirring occasionally to prevent scorching, to 200°F (just below boiling — small bubbles appear at the edges). Remove from heat. Add the vinegar and stir once or twice. Don't stir again. Let sit undisturbed 5 minutes.

The curd forms visibly — white flecks separating from yellow whey. Line a colander with cheesecloth and pour through. Let drain 15 minutes for moist ricotta; 45+ minutes for firmer, spreadable ricotta.

**Result**

Moist, rich, slightly sweet with a clean finish. The texture is nothing like commercial ricotta — grain is finer, no stabilizers, it collapses onto toast rather than sitting in a blob.

**Uses**

- On toasted bread with honey and thyme (immediate consumption)
- In fresh pasta filling with spinach and nutmeg
- As a base for pizza bianca
- Whipped with olive oil as a dip

**Whey**

Don't discard the whey. It's liquid gold: use it in bread dough instead of water, in soups instead of stock, or drink it (honestly not bad cold).
