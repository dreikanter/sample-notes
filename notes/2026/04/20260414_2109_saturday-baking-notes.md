# Saturday baking notes

Raining today. Good conditions for staying inside and making things.

**Morning: focaccia**

Third time making the high-hydration focaccia (see [[20260314_2073]]). This time: potato and rosemary topping. Par-cooked the potatoes (sliced thin, simmered 5 minutes until tender but not falling apart, cooled). Laid them overlapping on the dimpled dough before baking. Added fresh rosemary and flaky salt.

The potatoes crisp on the edges and stay soft in the center where they overlap with the dough. Good. This is a variation worth keeping.

**Afternoon: chocolate chip cookies**

Made the serious Serious Eats version (https://www.seriouseats.com/the-food-lab-the-best-chocolate-chip-cookies) which calls for browned butter and two rest periods: 30 minutes in the freezer after forming, then a 48-hour fridge rest. I only did the freezer rest (circumstances), and they were still excellent — crisp edges, soft center, the browned butter comes through clearly.

Notes: used a mix of milk chocolate chips and bittersweet chocolate chopped from a bar. The chopped chocolate creates irregular pockets and shards rather than uniform chips. Better.

**Evening: sourdough**

Fed the starter for a bake tomorrow. Same formula as the December loaf (see [[20251229_2021]]) — 80% hydration, cold retard overnight.

**On rainy Saturdays and baking**

There's a specific pleasure to a day that is entirely about making things. Not productive in the career sense, not urgent, not self-improving. Just: the kitchen smells like butter and yeast, the counter is dusted with flour, there's something good for dinner.

This is the kind of day I need more of.
