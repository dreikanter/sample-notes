# April listening notes

A record of what I've been listening to this month and what stood out.

**Mk.gee – Two Star & the Dream Police**

Still coming back to this. Reviewed it in January (see [[20260102_2028]]), but it's an April-light record more than a January-light record. The washed-out production works better in warmth.

**Bill Frisell – East/West**

Bought the live record after the concert (see [[20260319_2076]]). Frisell with his quartet and with a trio, recorded in California and Oregon in 2005. The trio sides are sparser and stranger; the quartet sides warmer. "Red River Valley" arranged as a nine-minute meditation is unlike any other version of the song.

**Adrianne Lenker – Bright Future**

Her 2024 record. Very quiet, very close-miked acoustic guitar and voice, a few instrumental pieces. The kind of album that requires headphones and willingness to not do anything else simultaneously. The song "Rugged Painter" played on repeat for three days.

**Spotify playlist I keep returning to**

Somebody curated a playlist of Japanese guitar music from the 1970s (https://open.spotify.com/playlist/some-approximation) — I can't find the exact link right now, but searching "Japanese folk 70s guitar" turns up similar things. Slow, fingerpicked, very clean recordings. Good working music, good cooking music.

**What I want to listen to soon**

An overview of Charlie Haden's work — I know the Ornette Coleman sides but not the solo career. Starting point: https://www.ecmrecords.com/artists/1435046952/charlie-haden

Good month for music. April always is.
