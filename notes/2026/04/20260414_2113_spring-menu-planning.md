# Spring menu planning

April to June: the best cooking window of the year, in my opinion. The ingredients are right and the inspiration is easy.

## What's in season now (April)

- Ramps (see [[20260328_2081]] — already cooked)
- Asparagus (just starting)
- Spring peas (a few weeks away)
- Spinach and tender greens
- Radishes
- Early strawberries (end of month if the weather cooperates)

## What I want to cook before it's gone

**Asparagus**

- Roasted with brown butter and a soft-boiled egg
- Shaved raw in a salad with parmesan and lemon (the underrated preparation)
- Asparagus soup — barely cooked, bright green, with crème fraîche

**Spring peas**

- Fresh pea pasta — same dough as [[20260201_2050]], puréed peas in the sauce
- Pea and mint crostini with ricotta (the ricotta I now make from scratch — see [[20260403_2086]])
- Pea risotto when I have 45 minutes

**Strawberries**

- Shortcake, obviously — the biscuit version, not cake
- Macerated in a little sugar and black pepper for 30 minutes; serve over yogurt
- Strawberry jam if there's a good flat at the market

## A month menu

Week 1: asparagus, radish salads, the pea pasta when peas arrive
Week 2: first strawberries
Week 3: pea risotto, asparagus soup
Week 4: whatever the market tells me
