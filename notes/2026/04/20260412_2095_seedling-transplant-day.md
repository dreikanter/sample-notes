# Seedling transplant day

After five weeks indoors and ten days of hardening off (see [[20260406_2089]]), the tomatoes went into the ground today.

**Conditions**

April 12. Clear sky, 62°F, no frost expected through the next two weeks. Soil temperature at 6 inches: 52°F — slightly cool for tomatoes (they prefer 60°F+) but acceptable. Last frost date for this zone is approximately April 15; close enough with the forecast.

**What went in**

- 4 Brandywine tomatoes, staked with 6-foot bamboo at planting
- 4 Sun Gold cherry tomatoes, staked
- 2 Shishito peppers (still small; going to the warmest spot)

**Planting deep**

Tomatoes are planted deep — stems buried to just below the lowest leaves. They root along buried stems. The deeper they go, the more roots, the more resilient the plant. These went in 8–10 inches deep.

**Soil amendment**

Mixed compost into each hole, plus a tablespoon of balanced slow-release fertilizer. The beds were top-dressed with compost in October; the soil is in good shape.

**After planting**

Watered deeply (not a gentle shower — water all the way to root depth, about 2 gallons per plant). Will let the soil partially dry before next watering. Tomatoes don't want constantly wet soil.

**Expectations**

Sun Golds will start producing by mid-July. Brandywines more like late July/early August. That feels far away.
