# April Sunday

The kind of day that April occasionally produces: 65°F, clear sky, a slight breeze from the south that is warm rather than cold. The city becomes demonstrably nicer. People sit on stoops. Dogs are walked at a slower pace. The trees on the avenue are fully leafed now, that fresh yellow-green that will deepen into proper summer green by May.

I made coffee slowly — pour-over, the weekend method, the kind that takes fifteen minutes if you do it correctly (see [[20260222_2062]]). Stood at the kitchen window while it brewed. The fiddle leaf fig that was struggling in January is putting out two new leaves simultaneously; I take this as an apology.

Afternoon: walked to the park. The cherry blossoms are done, but the crab apples that bloom later are at peak. The park was full in that specific spring-Sunday way — not crowded exactly, but alive. Two people threw a frisbee. A woman read a thick book on a blanket. Someone's child was trying to feed a duck that was not interested in being fed.

I sat on a bench for an hour and did approximately nothing. This is harder than it sounds and more valuable than most things I do that are not nothing.

Dinner: pasta (fresh, from [[20260201_2050]]) with the preserved lemons (from [[20260306_2069]]) in a quick butter-herb sauce. The preserved lemon with pasta is new and obviously right. Will repeat.

The tomatoes went in yesterday (see [[20260412_2095]]). They'll be there in the morning.

A good day. Not every day is a project.
