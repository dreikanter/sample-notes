# Spring run route notes

Updated my main running route for spring — the winter version avoided the park loop because the path was often icy. Now the park is back.

**The route**

Start at home, two blocks to the park entrance. Counterclockwise loop around the main path (2.3 miles). Exit at the north gate, down the hill along the avenue, through the residential blocks, back home. Total: 5.8 miles. Elevation: negligible.

**What's good about it now**

The park in April is peak photosynthesis — the trees are leafing out, still small and fresh, that specific yellow-green of new growth that lasts about three weeks before it deepens. The cherry trees near the south entrance were at full bloom last Saturday. By this weekend they'll be dropping petals onto the path, which is either beautiful or just messy depending on your frame.

The route passes a bakery at mile 4.5. I don't stop, but the smell is motivating in both directions.

**What I've noticed this spring**

More people on the path than even two weeks ago. The conversion to outdoor exercise that happens each spring is abrupt. Also: more cyclists. The city added protected lane segments over the winter; there's less overlap between bikes and runners now.

**Current pace**

7:45/mile on this route, comfortable conversation pace. Not trying to race anything; the goal is the miles and the morning.

**Next new route**

The long trail to the next park (out and back, ~10 miles) — planning for late April when I've built back to 35 mpw.
