# April morning light

6:10 AM. The alarm goes off and the room is not quite dark — there's already blue-grey light at the edges of the curtain. This is new in the last two weeks. In January the same time was fully dark.

I don't check my phone. This is the habit I've been building since January (see [[20260101_2026]]) — reading instead of scrolling. This morning: the Borges, ten pages, "Tlön, Uqbar, Orbis Tertius." A story in which a fictional world gradually replaces the real one because enough people have believed in it long enough.

By the time I finish the section, the light in the room is properly light. The curtains glow. The pothos on the bookshelf is outlined against the window.

I make coffee. The pour-over process is a kind of meditation, if you're willing to let it be — the grinding, the bloom, the slow circles, the counting. I don't count. But I do stand at the window while it brews, watching the street.

At 7:30, a run. The air is cold in a spring way — not winter cold, which demands respect, but a fresh cold that will be gone by noon. The trees on the park path are in full leaf. Two dog walkers, one cyclist, a woman walking fast with earbuds in, completely somewhere else.

The morning has a quality I'm reluctant to name because naming it might dispel it. Just: this is what it's for.
