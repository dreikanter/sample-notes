# April garden notes

First proper day in the garden this year — temperature reached 14°C by mid-afternoon and stayed there. Spent about three hours on and off doing overdue work.

## What's come up

The tulip bulbs I planted in November are all showing now. The red ones near the fence are already at about 20cm; the pale yellow ones in the pot are further behind. Last year they were about a week earlier.

The rosemary came through winter looking rough but has new growth on the lower stems. I've cut the dead wood back and it looks like it'll recover. The thyme is fine — it never seems bothered by anything.

The mint is spreading again. I need to sink a physical barrier around it properly this year, or just accept that the bed is mint now and work with that.

## What didn't make it

The lemon verbena I overwintered in the shed has not come back. I kept it frost-free but I think the light was insufficient. Next time either bring it fully indoors or replace it annually.

One of the rose canes snapped in the February storms — didn't notice until today. Cut it cleanly at the base. The rest of the plant looks healthy.

## Tasks still to do

- Turn the compost heap (it's been sitting undisturbed since November)
- Top-dress the raised bed with fresh compost before planting the courgettes
- Source slug pellets — iron phosphate, not metaldehyde; the latter's banned now anyway
- Decide what to do with the back corner that gets afternoon shade (hostas, probably)

The RHS has a decent planting calendar at https://www.rhs.org.uk/advice/planting-calendar if I need to cross-check timings.
