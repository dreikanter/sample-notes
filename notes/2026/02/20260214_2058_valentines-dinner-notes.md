# Valentine's dinner notes

Cooked at home instead of trying to get a restaurant reservation in February. Correct decision.

**Menu**

- Appetizer: burrata with roasted beets, blood orange segments, toasted pistachios, a drizzle of good olive oil
- Main: pan-seared duck breast with cherry pan sauce, roasted fingerling potatoes, wilted bitter greens
- Dessert: chocolate pot de crème made the day before

**On the duck**

Duck breast is unfairly intimidating. The key is that it's largely self-basting — score the fat layer in a crosshatch, start it fat-side down in a cold pan, render the fat slowly over medium-low heat (10–12 minutes) before flipping. The fat bastes the meat as it cooks. Finish to 130°F internal for medium, rest 5 minutes. It's almost more like cooking bacon than steak.

The cherry pan sauce: deglaze the duck fat (most of it poured off) with a splash of cognac, add cherry jam and chicken stock, reduce until glossy. Add a tablespoon of butter at the end, off heat.

**The pot de crème**

Made these the night before. 70% chocolate, heavy cream, egg yolks, a little sugar, a pinch of salt, a few drops of vanilla. Bake in a water bath at 325°F for 30–35 minutes. They should barely jiggle in the center when done. Refrigerate overnight. Top with lightly sweetened cream.

No reservations, no wait time, wine of our choosing, music of our choosing. Worth the effort.
