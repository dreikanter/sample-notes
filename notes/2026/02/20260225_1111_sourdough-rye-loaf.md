# Sourdough rye loaf — second attempt

Better result than the first try in December. The crumb is still slightly too dense but no longer gummy in the middle, which was the main problem before.

## What changed

I increased the hydration from 72% to 75% and extended the bulk ferment by an hour (total 5 hours at around 22°C). Also switched from a loaf tin to a banneton with a Dutch oven bake — 25 minutes covered at 250°C, then 20 minutes uncovered at 220°C.

The rye proportion is 30% whole rye, 70% strong white. Going higher than that without a longer ferment seems to cause the gumminess.

## Process notes

- Levain built the night before: 20g starter, 60g water, 60g 50/50 rye and white flour
- Autolyse: 30 min before adding levain and salt
- Folds: 4 sets of stretch-and-fold at 30-minute intervals
- Shaped and cold proofed overnight in the fridge (about 14 hours)
- Score: single curved slash, about 2cm deep

## Taste

Mild sourness, which is what I wanted. The crust has a good crackle. The caraway seeds on top were a good call — added about a teaspoon scattered before baking.

## Next adjustments

Try 35% rye and see what happens to the ferment timing. Also want to experiment with a longer autolyse (up to 60 minutes) to develop more gluten before adding the levain.
