# Garden — tomato blight prevention plan

Following up on the August preview note. Going to be systematic about blight this year.

## What I know

Late blight (*Phytophthora infestans*) spreads in warm, humid conditions — exactly what British August delivers. Last year I saw the first spots on August 18th and by August 25th the plants were unsalvageable. The spores are airborne and ubiquitous once present in a neighbourhood.

Infected: remove affected leaves immediately, ideally into a sealed bag, not the compost.

## Prevention strategy for this year

**Copper fungicide sprays:**
Bordeaux mixture or copper hydroxide applied every 7-10 days from the first week of August. First spray done today. Not a guarantee but creates a hostile surface. Only works as a preventive — once blight is present it's too late.

**Physical barriers:**
The polytunnel plants (two Sungolds) should be lower risk because rain doesn't directly hit the foliage. The outdoor plants (Tigerella, Black Krim) are higher risk. Considering a simple plastic roof cover for the outdoor bed.

**Harvest strategy:**
Start picking fruits at first blush rather than waiting for full colour. Ripen on a windowsill. This worked for me in 2021.

**Variety notes:**
Sungold is supposedly more blight-tolerant than most heritage varieties. The Black Krim is likely to be most vulnerable.

Good resource on blight identification: [RHS blight guide](https://www.rhs.org.uk/disease/potato-and-tomato-blight)
