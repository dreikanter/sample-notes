# Retrospective meeting notes — sprint 14

**Date:** August 5, 2024
**Attendees:** Full team (8)
**Format:** Start/Stop/Continue

## Start doing

- Pre-reading documents for planning sessions (Maya's suggestion — brought up consistently for three retros)
- "Definition of done" checklist on tickets before they move to review — too many PRs being approved with missing tests
- A weekly team thread for "things I learned this week" — low pressure, opted-in

## Stop doing

- Mid-sprint scope changes without a full team conversation
- Merging PRs on Friday afternoons (has caused two weekend incidents)
- Calling something "quick" in a meeting without checking if anyone agrees

## Continue doing

- The async standup format is working — team consensus to keep it
- Code review rotations have improved knowledge sharing
- The decision log (Priya's initiative from sprint 12) is being used

## Key tensions

The "Friday afternoon merge" rule was contentious. Leon pointed out that some PRs are genuinely ready and blocking them creates work pileups on Monday. Agreed compromise: merges fine if you've already reviewed the metrics and have explicitly agreed to monitor over the weekend. Self-selection by commitment level.

## My action item

Write the "definition of done" template by next Wednesday and circulate for comment before the sprint 15 planning.
