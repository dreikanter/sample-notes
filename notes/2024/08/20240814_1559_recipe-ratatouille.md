# Recipe — ratatouille (the slow version)

Not the quick version. The slow version that takes two hours and tastes like a different dish.

## What makes it different

The standard recipe throws everything in together and cooks until soft. This version cooks each vegetable separately first, then combines. The difference in flavour concentration is significant.

## Ingredients

- 2 courgettes
- 2 aubergines
- 4 tomatoes (or 400g tin, but fresh in August)
- 2 red peppers
- 1 large onion
- 4 garlic cloves
- Olive oil, generous
- Thyme, bay leaves
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Cut all vegetables into similar-sized pieces. Roughly 2cm cubes.

2. Salt the aubergine pieces, let drain 20 minutes, pat dry.

3. In a wide heavy pan, heat oil over medium-high. Cook each vegetable separately in batches until golden at the edges — aubergine first (absorbs most oil), then courgette, then peppers. Remove each to a bowl as done.

4. In the same pan, soften onion over medium heat, 10 minutes. Add garlic, 2 minutes.

5. Add tomatoes, herbs. Simmer 10 minutes.

6. Return all vegetables. Fold gently to combine. Simmer on lowest heat for 30-40 minutes until everything is very tender and the liquid has concentrated.

7. Season. The dish improves overnight.

Best served at room temperature. Excellent on toast with a fried egg, or alongside roast lamb.
