# Garden harvest notes — August

The allotment has been producing steadily. No blight yet — the preventive spray regime [[20240803_1553]] seems to be working, or I've been lucky, or both.

## This week's harvest

- Courgettes: 3 large, 2 medium. The large ones escaped notice for a few days. One became ratatouille [[20240814_1559]]. One is still in the fridge.
- Climbing beans: about 400g. Blanched, ate cold with olive oil and cherry tomatoes. Perfect.
- Sungold tomatoes: first proper picking — a full colander. Ate most straight off the vine. They don't improve with cooking.
- Basil: cutting regularly to prevent bolting. Making pesto every 10 days or so.
- Beetroot: dug six. Roasted four with vinegar and goat's cheese. Two pickled in apple cider vinegar with fennel seeds.

## Upcoming

The Black Krim is just starting to show colour at the bottom of the fruit. The characteristic dark shoulder should appear in a week or two. I'm excited and nervous — this variety is supposedly exceptional when grown in good conditions and merely fine otherwise.

The climbing beans are starting to slow down. The pods are getting faster from flower to ready. Should have another two or three weeks of production.

## First signs of trouble

One courgette plant has powdery mildew on its older leaves. Standard late-summer development. Removing affected leaves; the plant will probably produce for another month before declining.

Seed saving: letting two bean pods go fully to dry.
