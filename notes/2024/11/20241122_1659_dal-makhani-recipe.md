# Dal makhani — slow-cooked version

This is a weekend recipe — the total time is long but the active work is minimal. Based on several attempts and notes from [Meera Sodha's dal makhani recipe](https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2019/jan/04/meera-sodha-vegan-recipe-makhani-dal).

## Ingredients (serves 4–6)

- 200g whole black lentils (urad dal), soaked overnight
- 50g kidney beans, soaked overnight (or half a tin, added late)
- 2 tbsp neutral oil or ghee
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 2cm ginger, grated
- 2 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp kashmiri chilli (for colour and gentle heat)
- 400g tin chopped tomatoes
- 200ml water or light stock
- 4 tbsp single cream or cashew cream
- Salt to taste

## Method

1. Pressure cook the soaked lentils and beans for 20–25 minutes, or simmer for 1.5 hours until very soft. They should be starting to break down.

2. Fry onions in oil over medium heat for 15 minutes — properly caramelised, not just softened. Add garlic, ginger, and cumin seeds; cook 2 minutes.

3. Add dry spices, stir 30 seconds, then add tomatoes. Cook down for 10 minutes until the oil separates from the tomato paste (important step — this is the base becoming properly fried rather than stewed).

4. Add the cooked lentils with their liquid, season with salt. Simmer uncovered for 30–45 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thick and rich.

5. Stir in cream. Taste and adjust. Serve with rice or flatbread.

## Notes

The long simmer is where the flavour develops — don't rush it. The dal should be almost black and very thick. If it's brown and soupy, keep going.
