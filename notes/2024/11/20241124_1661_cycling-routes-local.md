# Cycling routes from home — notes

Cataloguing rides I've done or am planning. The area has reasonable cycling infrastructure for an English town, plus some good quiet roads once you get past the ring road.

## Regular routes

**Canal towpath loop (22km, 90m elevation)**  
Out along the towpath for 9km, cross at the old stone bridge, return on the B-road parallel. Mostly flat. The towpath is rough in places — narrower tyres struggle. Good for early morning; the canal is calm and foggy in October/November. No traffic until the B-road stretch.

**Reservoir circuit (36km, 280m elevation)**  
Longer ride with a proper climb before the reservoir. The descent back down is fast and clean. Café at the reservoir open on weekends — good cake, passable coffee. The climb has an 8% section that always catches me out after the flat approach.

**Greenway to the market town (48km, 350m elevation)**  
There's a disused railway line converted to cycle path that runs about 18km out. Beyond that it's quiet lanes. The market town at the end has a Saturday market and a good chippy. This is the one I do when I want a destination rather than a loop.

## What I'm on

A 2018 Kona Rove gravel bike — steel frame, comfortable geometry, runs 40mm tyres. Perfect for the mixed-surface routes here. The [Kona Rove review on CyclingAbout](https://www.cyclingabout.com/kona-rove-review/) matches my experience — reliable, comfortable, nothing flashy.

## Winter notes

The canal towpath becomes genuinely difficult after sustained rain. The lanes near the reservoir accumulate mud from farm traffic. Route choice in November/December requires checking the forecast.
