# Country loaf — first bake with revived starter

Following up on the starter revival notes from [[20241015_1620]]. The bake happened on the 17th October and I'm writing it up now.

## What I did

Used the recipe I outlined in that note: 450g bread flour, 50g whole wheat, 375g water (77% hydration), 100g levain at peak, 10g salt.

Autolyse 45 minutes. Added levain and salt, incorporated with stretch-and-fold. Bulk fermented at 21°C for about 4 hours, with four sets of stretch-and-folds in the first two hours. Shaped into a batard, into a proofing basket, overnight in the fridge (about 10 hours).

Baked at 250°C in a Dutch oven: lid on for 20 minutes, lid off for another 20 minutes. Internal temp at 97°C when I pulled it.

## Result

Better than expected given the starter had been dormant. The crumb was reasonably open — not the dramatic open crumb you see on Instagram, but tender and with some decent irregular holes. The crust was good: crackly and deeply coloured.

The flavour was pleasantly tangy without being sharp. The whole wheat adds a slight nuttiness. I'll increase it to 100g next time.

## What to change

- More tension in shaping — the loaf spread slightly
- Let bulk fermentation run a bit longer; the crumb was slightly denser than ideal
- Score more decisively; the ear was present but modest

The [Full Proof Baking YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@FullProofBaking) has excellent slow-motion scoring technique videos. Worth rewatching before next bake.
