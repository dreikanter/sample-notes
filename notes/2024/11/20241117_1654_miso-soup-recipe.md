# Miso soup — quick weekday version

Not a dashi-from-scratch recipe — this is the ten-minute version for weekday mornings or a quick lunch. Based on notes from reading [Just One Cookbook's miso soup guide](https://www.justonecookbook.com/homemade-miso-soup/).

## What I use

- 600ml water
- 1 tsp instant dashi powder (Ajinomoto brand from the Japanese grocery)
- 2 tbsp white miso (shiro miso — lighter and sweeter than red)
- 150g silken tofu, cubed
- 2 tbsp dried wakame seaweed (rehydrates in the hot liquid)
- 2 spring onions, sliced thin

## Method

Bring the water to just below a boil. Add dashi powder and stir to dissolve. Add tofu cubes and wakame. Drop to a gentle simmer for 2 minutes — the wakame will expand.

Take the pan off the heat. Put miso into a small ladle or sieve and dissolve it into the soup by stirring against the ladle. This keeps the miso from boiling, which would damage the live cultures and dull the flavour.

Add spring onions. Serve immediately.

## Notes on miso

White miso (shiro) is sweeter and milder — good for this soup and for salad dressings. Red miso (aka) is stronger, saltier, better for heartier soups and stews. Mixed (awase) splits the difference and is the most versatile default.

Miso stores for months in the fridge once opened. Don't worry about it going off; the fermentation is a preservative.

## Additions

On days when I want more substance: add soba noodles (cook separately), shimeji mushrooms, or a soft-boiled egg. The base recipe is a template.
