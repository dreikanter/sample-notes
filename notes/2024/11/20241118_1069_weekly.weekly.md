# Week of 18 November 2024

## What happened

Work was mostly heads-down writing. Finished a draft of the architecture document that's been sitting half-done for three weeks. Not especially proud of the prose but the structure finally holds together. Will need one more editing pass before sharing.

Went for three runs, which is the most I've managed in a month. The shorter days make it harder — I'm reluctant to run after dark on unlit paths, so morning is the only real window, and mornings have been cold enough that I keep finding reasons to skip. Solved this (provisionally) by laying out running clothes the night before.

Had a long conversation with a colleague about distributed tracing and whether the overhead is worth it for services that see low traffic. No firm conclusions but I left with a list of things to actually test rather than theorise about.
