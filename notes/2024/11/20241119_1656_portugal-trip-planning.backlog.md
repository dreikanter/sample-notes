# Portugal trip — planning backlog

Thinking about a week in Portugal in late January or February when flights are cheap and the country is quiet. Starting to gather ideas; nothing booked.

## Destination options

**Lisbon + Sintra**: obvious choice but genuinely excellent. Sintra palaces, Belém, the food, the hills. Could spend a week in Lisbon alone without seeing everything. Disadvantage: everyone else knows this too, even in winter.

**Porto**: smaller, more navigable, excellent food scene, the Douro is beautiful in winter light. Day trips to the Douro Valley wine country. The [Porto wine tourism board](https://www.portoandnorth.travel/en-GB) has useful orientation.

**Alentejo**: completely different character — flat, dry landscape, cork oaks, medieval walled towns (Évora, Monsaraz). Slower, quieter, less infrastructure for tourism. Interesting food: migas, black pork, bread culture.

**Coast**: off-season the Algarve is genuinely pleasant rather than crowded. The western coast (Sagres, Aljezur, Vila do Bispo) has wilder beaches and a more interesting food culture than the eastern package-holiday strip.
