# Rhubarb compote notes

Used the rhubarb from the market (see [[20240328_1455]]) — 600g, sliced to 3cm pieces.

**Method:**
- 600g rhubarb
- 80g sugar (adjusted downward from first attempt — this variety is less acidic than summer rhubarb)
- Juice of half an orange
- 1 vanilla bean, split

Combined everything in a saucepan, no water — rhubarb releases a lot. Medium heat, covered for 5 minutes, then uncovered. Stirred occasionally. Done in about 12 minutes: the rhubarb had collapsed completely but wasn't a mash, there was a bright pink-red syrupy liquid around it.

**Result:** Tastes like rhubarb should — tart, a little vegetal, sweet without being candy. The vanilla adds a rounded quality without being identifiable as vanilla. The orange juice brightens the color and adds a subtle citrus background.

**Uses:**
- On yogurt in the morning (primary use, got through it in 5 days)
- On steel-cut oats (excellent — the tartness cuts through oat richness)
- Stirred into sparkling water (unexpected, this worked well — 2 tbsp per glass)
- Spooned over vanilla ice cream with candied ginger (tried this once, indulgent)

**Next time:** Try with ginger instead of vanilla. Also want to try preserving some with more sugar to keep through summer.

The color after refrigerating overnight was a deep magenta. Rhubarb color intensifies with heat rather than fading, unlike most red fruit.

Reference for rhubarb varieties: https://extension.usu.edu/yardandhome/research/rhubarb-in-the-garden
