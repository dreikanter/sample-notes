# Red lentil and smoked paprika soup

This came together from a few different recipes I'd been circling around. It's fast — under 40 minutes start to finish — and the leftovers are noticeably better after a night in the fridge.

**Serves 4.**

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1.5 tsp smoked paprika (sweet, not hot)
- 0.5 tsp ground coriander
- 300 g red lentils, rinsed
- 1 can (400 g) crushed tomatoes
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- Juice of half a lemon
- Salt and pepper
- Fresh parsley or cilantro to finish

## Method

Soften the onion in oil over medium heat, about 8 minutes. Add garlic and spices, stir for a minute until fragrant. Add lentils, tomatoes, and stock. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a steady simmer. Cook uncovered for 20–22 minutes until lentils have completely dissolved into the soup.

Use an immersion blender to blend about a third of the soup for body while keeping texture. Season with lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Adjust paprika if it needs more depth.

Finish with a drizzle of olive oil and torn herbs.

## Notes

Smoked paprika quality varies a lot by brand. La Chinata is reliable. Smitten Kitchen has a good discussion of the flavor differences in [her lentil soup post](https://smittenkitchen.com/2010/01/lentil-soup/).

Works well with a piece of crusty bread or a flatbread on the side.
