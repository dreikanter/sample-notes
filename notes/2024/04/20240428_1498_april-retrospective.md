# April retrospective

The month felt longer than March in the good way — more happened and the longer daylight hours created space for things outside work.

**Work:** The mobile API performance fix shipped. The geocoding call is now asynchronous; the p99 latency on affected endpoints went from 2.3s to 340ms. This is the kind of fix that is embarrassing in its simplicity once you see it. The Prometheus monitoring stack is up and the dashboards are showing the service in a way that's useful rather than decorative.

**Running:** Best month since last autumn. 127km total. One tempo run each week starting week three. The knee held without problems. The plan for May: add a second quality session per week (one tempo, one intervals).

**Reading:** Finished *Babel* (see [[20240418_1488]]) and got halfway through *The Master and Margarita*, which is one of the strangest books I've read in years. Started *The Rings of Saturn*, set it aside twice, now genuinely absorbed.

**Garden:** Beds prepared, seedlings growing, tomatoes potted up. Garlic is now 25cm tall. Planted radishes and spinach outside directly — both are frost-tolerant and I want the practice.

**Cooking:** Risotto. Stir-fry technique improved. Kimchi is excellent. Made bread twice with better scoring.

**One thing I want to carry forward:** The early morning writing. It happened more consistently in the second half of the month. The quality of those notes is different from notes written in the evening.

May intentions: more outside time, continue the running progression, actually finish *The Master and Margarita*.
