# Rain day observation

It's been raining since before I woke up — a slow persistent April rain that isn't dramatic but gets into everything. The gutters are clogged (see [[20240309_1419]], still not done) and there's a narrow waterfall off the east corner of the roof where the water can't exit properly.

Rain days at home have a different quality than rain days when you're supposed to be somewhere. Staying in is the obvious choice and doesn't feel like deprivation. The sound of rain on the window while working is productive — the ambient acoustic texture that noise-canceling headphones try to approximate and can't.

Spent the morning with the architecture magazine from Tokyo (see [[20240407_1472]]). Without being able to read the text, you're limited to the visual material and whatever the captions say, which turns out to be sufficient for a lot of it. A long feature on a house in the mountains outside Matsumoto — the photographs showed the relationship between the heavy timber structure and the surrounding forest in a way that was clear without language. The house appeared to be designed to eventually become invisible as the vegetation grows around it.

Made rice and a soft-cooked egg and kimchi for lunch, which is the rain-day food. The kimchi batch three (see [[20240320_1440]]) is now at about three weeks in the fridge and has developed beautifully — more sour, more complex, the heat better integrated.

Afternoon: actually going to read rather than look at my phone. Accountability: started *The Master and Margarita*, which has been on the list for years.
