# Seedling transplant notes

Potted up the tomatoes from cells to 10cm individual pots today. Seven Roma seedlings and four of the beefsteak saved-seed type (the remaining three didn't make it, probably damping off — there was a slightly musty smell from two cells and the stems were thin at the base).

**Roma seedlings:** Excellent root development, roots starting to spiral at the bottom of the cells. This is the right time. The true leaves are the third set and the plants are 12-15cm tall. Buried them deep — tomatoes form roots along the buried stem, so I covered up to the first two leaf nodes. This is always slightly alarming but works.

**Beefsteak seedlings:** Slightly leggy compared to the Romas. This is probably the saved seed genetics or slightly lower germination energy. Buried even deeper proportionally.

**Potting mix:** Commercial seedling mix plus about 20% perlite for drainage. The perlite addition is something I started two years ago after some root rot problems — the commercial mix holds too much water in the heavier pots.

**Basil:** Too crowded, thinned to one plant per cell. The removed seedlings were too small to transplant but I ate them — young basil with a little olive oil is extremely pleasant.

**Parsley:** Now at 5 of 8 cells germinated. Will thin next week.

**Timeline:** These go outside after last frost, which is typically mid-May here. Still a month away. Will harden off starting early May when outdoor temps are more reliable.
