# Pasta from scratch — attempt two

First attempt was six months ago and produced pasta that was too thick and had a gluey interior texture. This time I was more careful about hydration and rest time.

**Dough recipe (serves 3):**
- 200g 00 flour
- 2 whole eggs + 1 yolk
- Pinch of salt
- No water added initially

Made a well in the flour, added eggs, worked from center outward. The dough came together at about 5 minutes of mixing but was quite stiff. Added 1 tsp water, kneaded another 5 min. The finished dough was smooth, slightly tacky, and sprang back slowly when pressed — the right texture.

**Rest:** 30 minutes wrapped at room temperature. This is critical — the gluten needs to relax or rolling tears the dough.

**Rolling:** Used the pasta machine, going from setting 1 down to setting 6 (very thin). Kept the sheet floured. Cut to pappardelle width with a knife.

**Result:** Better than attempt one. The thickness was even and appropriate — just translucent when held to light. Cooked in 2 minutes from boiling salted water. The texture was silkier than dried pasta and significantly more toothsome.

**What still went wrong:** A few spots where the dough tore during rolling, producing holes in the pappardelle. This might be from not resting long enough locally after stretching. Also, the pasta stuck together while waiting to cook — should dust more aggressively with semolina.

**Sauce:** Brown butter, crispy sage, a little pasta water to emulsify. Simple enough to not compete with the pasta.

Reference for egg pasta ratios: https://www.seriouseats.com/fresh-pasta-without-a-machine
