# April, late

Spent most of the weekend in the garden. The raised beds are finally turned over and the compost from winter is dug in — darker and richer than expected, which was encouraging. The pH test came back at 6.3, close enough that I skipped the lime this time.

Transplanted the tomato and pepper seedlings that had been sitting under the grow light since late March. They were getting a little leggy, so it was time. Used the same spacing as last year — 45 cm between tomatoes — but moved the peppers to a sunnier corner after they underperformed in the shadier spot I gave them in 2023.

The drip line is fixed. One emitter had calcified completely; I replaced two. Running time adjusted to 25 minutes in the morning given current temperatures.

Noticed the first aphids on the new bean seedlings. Not a disaster yet. Going to try a strong water spray before reaching for anything else. Last year I waited too long and lost two plants to the resulting ants.

The cold frame is holding steady. Lettuces and arugula in it are doing well — the arugula is already past the first cut stage. Had some in a salad tonight with a sharp cheddar and some walnuts. Good enough that it didn't need much dressing.

The neighborhood pair of crows is back and interested in the newly turned beds. They haven't caused real damage yet, just structural concern. Going to put some netting over the bean starts.
