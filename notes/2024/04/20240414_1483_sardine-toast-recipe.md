# Sardine toast recipe

Sardines have a reputation problem. The smell is real (open the tin near a window) but the flavor is deeply savory in a way that pairs well with acid and fat. This recipe takes ten minutes and costs almost nothing.

## Ingredients (one serving)

- 1 tin good sardines in olive oil (Portuguese brands are best — Matiz, Conservas de Cambados, or similar)
- 2 thick slices sourdough or country bread
- 1 small ripe tomato, or 3-4 cherry tomatoes
- 1 clove garlic
- Handful of arugula or watercress
- Lemon
- Olive oil, salt, black pepper
- Optional: capers, Calabrian chili, fresh parsley

## Method

1. Toast the bread until properly golden. This is important — you want structural resistance against the sardines and tomato.
2. While warm, rub each slice with the cut side of the garlic clove. One pass is enough.
3. Slice or halve the tomatoes, season with salt, let them sit for two minutes.
4. Drizzle toast with olive oil. Layer the dressed greens, then tomato, then sardines (break into large pieces or leave whole — both work).
5. Squeeze lemon generously. Black pepper. Eat immediately.

## Variations

- **Spanish style:** Add sliced Piquillo peppers, omit arugula, finish with sherry vinegar.
- **More substantial:** Add a soft-boiled egg on top.
- **Winter version:** Roast canned tomatoes instead of fresh, add smoked paprika.
