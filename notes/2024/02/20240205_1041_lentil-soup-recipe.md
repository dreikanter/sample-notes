# Red lentil and smoked paprika soup

This has become the default when I need something fast, filling, and requiring minimal attention. The smoked paprika carries most of the flavour work so the ingredient list stays short.

## Ingredients (serves 4)

- 300g red lentils, rinsed
- 1 large onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced thin
- 2 tsp smoked paprika (sweet, not hot — though hot works if you want it)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 400g tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 litre vegetable stock (good quality — this matters more than people think)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper, lemon juice to finish

## Method

Heat oil in a heavy pot over medium heat. Add onion with a pinch of salt and cook until soft and starting to colour, about 12 minutes — don't rush this. Add garlic, paprika, and cumin, stir for 90 seconds.

Add tomatoes and cook down for five minutes. Add lentils and stock. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, simmer uncovered for 25 minutes until lentils are fully collapsed.

Taste. Add salt. Squeeze half a lemon in, taste again. Finish with a swirl of olive oil in each bowl.

## Notes

Keeps for four days in the fridge. The flavour deepens significantly overnight. Freezes well for up to two months.
