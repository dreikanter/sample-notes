# Seed starting log — February

Seeds are in. The grow light shelf is set up and running.

## What's started (Feb 5–6)

**Tomatoes (Feb 5):**
- Brandywine (6 cells) — large beefsteak type, 80 days, notorious for flavor and difficulty
- Cherokee Purple (6 cells) — heirloom, slightly earlier than Brandywine
- Midnight Roma (4 cells) — new to me, paste type with dark coloring

All seeded at 2 per cell, will thin to one after germination.

**Peppers (Feb 6):**
- Jimmy Nardello (4 cells) — sweet frying pepper, prolific producer
- Shishito (4 cells) — mild Japanese pepper, have grown these before, reliable

Peppers need more heat to germinate — put them on a heat mat at 27°C. Tomatoes germinate fine without it at room temperature.

## Setup

Grow lights: two 4-foot LED bars on a timer, 16 hours on / 8 hours off. The lights are about 4 inches above the soil surface and will raise as seedlings grow.

Potting mix: seed-starting mix (not regular potting soil — too dense, poor drainage for delicate seedlings).

Watering: bottom-watering only once established. Mist the surface until germination.

## Timeline

- Germination expected: tomatoes 5–10 days, peppers 10–14 days
- First true leaves: 2–3 weeks after germination
- Transplant to larger pots: when 2 sets of true leaves
- Harden off outdoors: mid-March, 2 weeks
- Final outdoor transplant: after last frost date (mid-May in this zone)

Reference: [High Mowing Seeds germination guide](https://www.highmowingseeds.com/pages/seed-starting) for temperature and timing charts.
