# Sourdough troubleshooting guide

Building this as I encounter problems. Not comprehensive — just what I've actually fixed.

## Dense crumb with no holes

**Most likely cause:** Under-proofed dough, under-developed gluten, or both.

**Diagnostics:**
- Did the bulk ferment reach 50–75% rise? If not, extend it.
- Does the dough pass the windowpane test? Stretch a small piece thin — if it tears immediately, gluten needs more work.
- Is the starter active? Float test: drop a teaspoon of starter in water. If it floats, it's ready.

## Gummy interior

**Most likely cause:** Under-baked. Looks done outside but isn't.

The internal temperature should reach 96–98°C. Use an instant-read thermometer. Don't trust the tap test alone.

Also possible: too much steam too long. Removing the Dutch oven lid after 20 minutes lets the crust set properly. If you leave the lid on too long, the crust stays soft and the interior bakes unevenly.

## Flat loaf that spreads sideways

**Most likely cause:** Over-proofed, or shaped without sufficient tension.

Over-proofing: the dough rises, then starts collapsing its gluten structure. It will be sticky, gas-y, and unable to hold shape. Reduce bulk ferment time or lower temperature.

Shaping tension: the outer skin of the loaf should feel taut, like a drum. If it doesn't, the dough will spread under oven heat rather than rising up.

## Thick, pale crust

**Most likely cause:** Oven not hot enough, or Dutch oven not properly preheated.

Preheat the Dutch oven in the oven for 45–60 minutes before baking. The vessel needs to be fully saturated with heat.
