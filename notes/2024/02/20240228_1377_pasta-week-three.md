# Fresh pasta log — week three, first attempt at ravioli

Attempted ricotta and spinach ravioli. The filling was fine; the sealing was the challenge.

## Filling

- 250g whole milk ricotta, drained overnight (this step matters — excess liquid makes the filling escape during cooking)
- 100g fresh spinach, cooked, squeezed completely dry, chopped fine
- 60g Parmigiano-Reggiano, finely grated
- 1 egg yolk
- Nutmeg, salt, white pepper

Mix cold. Taste before it goes into the pasta — this is the one opportunity to fix the seasoning.

## The pasta and shaping

Used the same egg dough as before (100g flour + 1 egg + 1 yolk per serving). The key difference from flat pasta: roll thinner. Setting 7 of 9, borderline translucent. Too thick and the seal is harder to press and the cooked result is doughy at the edges.

Dot filling (about 1 tsp per raviolo) at 5cm intervals. Fold sheet over, press air out working from the filling outward, cut with a pasta wheel or cutter.

The air removal is the critical step. Two of mine had air pockets that caused them to split during cooking. A damp finger run around the filling before folding helps the seal.

## Cooking

3–4 minutes in heavily salted boiling water. They float when done but I give them another minute.

## Result

Eight ravioli, six were intact, two split. The intact ones were genuinely excellent — the filling is mild and clean, lets the fresh pasta register. Brown butter, sage, and extra Parmigiano is the right treatment.

Reference: [Rosetta Costantino's *My Calabria*](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/) for Southern Italian pasta traditions; [Evan Funke's technique videos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=) for Northern Italian pasta.
