# February close — month summary

A short month that felt long because of the offsite and the infrastructure work.

**Read replica:** In production, monitored, stable. This was the main bet and it paid out. The billing run happened last week with no replication lag issues above 3 seconds. Declared done.

**On-call runbook:** Draft exists. Not reviewed by the team yet. February incomplete, carrying to March.

**Offsite facilitation:** See [[20240207_1368]]. Mixed result. The technical direction session worked; the escape room didn't.

**Reading:** Finished *Seeing Like a State*. Re-read *Piranesi* (see [[20240226_1376]]). Did not start *Blood Meridian* — the appetite for it still isn't there. This is fine.

**Pasta:** Three weeks of fresh pasta. Ravioli attempt (see [[20240228_1377]]) was partially successful.

**Running:** The 5k training is going. Currently at 4.2km comfortable. April 7 is achievable.

**Cold frame:** Built (see [[20240221_1374]]). Tomato seedlings look healthy under grow lights.

## What surprised me

The offsite's best output wasn't the agenda items — it was the conversation between Devlin and Marcus on day two that wasn't on any agenda. Planning for serendipity is a paradox but I want to keep more unscheduled time in future offsites.

## Leap day observation

An extra day this year. Used it for a long walk and finished the February reading rather than working. A reasonable use of a day that doesn't normally exist.
