# Bread country loaf — refined method

After the experiments documented in [[20240309_1418]], here's what I've settled on. This produces consistent results.

## Formula (for one 900g loaf)

- 420g bread flour
- 80g whole wheat flour
- 370g water (74% hydration)
- 9g salt
- 100g active starter (100% hydration)

## Timeline

**Day 1 evening (6pm):**
Feed starter 1:5:5 ratio. It should peak in 8-10 hours at 21°C.

**Day 2 morning (7am — when starter is active):**
1. Autolyse: mix flour and 340g water, rest 40 min
2. Add starter and salt dissolved in remaining 30g water, incorporate by stretching and folding until homogeneous
3. Bulk fermentation: 4 hours at 21°C with four sets of stretch-and-folds in the first 2 hours (every 30 min)
4. Shape: pre-shape, bench rest 20 min, final shape into banneton
5. Cold retard: refrigerate 12-16 hours

**Day 2 evening or Day 3 morning:**
1. Preheat Dutch oven 1 hour at 250°C
2. Score and bake covered 20 min at 250°C
3. Remove lid, reduce to 230°C, bake 20-25 min until deep mahogany
4. Cool completely on rack — minimum 1 hour, ideally 2

## What changed from earlier attempts

- Reduced hydration from 76% to 74%: slightly easier shaping, similar crumb
- Extended cold proof: 12-16 hours produces more flavor than 10
- Added autolyse: better gluten development with less effort
