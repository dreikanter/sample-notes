# Garlic scape recipes

The garlic scapes are here — the curling stems that hardneck varieties send up before bulbing. You cut them off to direct energy into the bulb. This also gives you a delicious ingredient that's available for only about three weeks.

**What scapes taste like:** Milder than garlic cloves, slightly grassy, with the garlic flavor front-loaded rather than lasting. They can be used like a spring onion but with more complexity.

## Scape pesto

The classic use and genuinely better than basil pesto for some applications.

- 150g scapes, cut into 2cm pieces
- 40g pine nuts or walnuts (walnuts more economical and slightly less sweet)
- 50g Parmigiano
- 100ml olive oil
- Juice of half a lemon
- Salt

Blend scapes first until roughly chopped, add nuts and pulse, add cheese and oil, adjust seasoning. Looser than basil pesto. On pasta, on toast with ricotta, on grilled fish.

## Grilled scapes

Toss whole scapes in olive oil and salt. Grill over high heat 3-4 minutes turning once. They blister and char at the tips, become slightly sweet. Serve with a squeeze of lemon. Eat as a vegetable side or snack.

## Scape compound butter

Blanch scapes 1 min, shock in ice water, dry completely, mince. Fold into softened butter with salt and lemon zest. Roll in parchment and freeze. Use to finish a steak or melt over roasted vegetables.

## Pickled scapes

Pack whole scapes (coiled) into a jar. Cover with brine (1 cup water, 1 cup white wine vinegar, 1 tbsp salt, 1 tsp sugar). Process or refrigerate. Ready in 3 days.
