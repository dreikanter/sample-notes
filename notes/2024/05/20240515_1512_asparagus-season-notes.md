# Asparagus season notes

The asparagus season runs roughly six weeks here. This is week two. After June it's gone and won't be worth eating again until next spring.

**What I've made so far:**

**Roasted with brown butter:** The simplest preparation. Toss with olive oil and salt, roast at 220°C 12-15 minutes until the tips char. Make brown butter in a pan (butter → foamy → golden → nutty smell), pour over asparagus. A squeeze of lemon. This works on thin or thick asparagus equally.

**Shaved raw in salad:** The very thin spears can be eaten raw, shaved on a mandoline or with a vegetable peeler. Dressed with good olive oil, lemon, Parmesan shavings, and toasted almonds. The raw texture is different — grassy and slightly crunchy. Better with thin asparagus (the thick ones are slightly stringy raw).

**With eggs:** Several variations. Fried eggs with asparagus is one of those ingredient pairings that's essentially perfect — both are mild, both are rich in their different ways, the runny yolk acts as a sauce. Also: soft-boiled eggs with blanched asparagus for dipping (use spears as soldiers).

**In a simple pasta:** Sauté garlic in olive oil, add blanched asparagus and reserved pasta water, toss with linguine, finish with lemon and pecorino. Twenty-minute dinner.

**What I haven't done yet:**
- Asparagus soup (will try this week — blended with crème fraîche)
- With prosciutto and burrata (the restaurant presentation, worth trying at home)
