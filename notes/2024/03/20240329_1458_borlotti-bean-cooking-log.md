# Borlotti bean cooking log

Cooking the dried Borlotti beans from the market (see [[20240328_1455]]).

**Soaking:** 200g dried beans, covered by 8cm water, soaked overnight (~14 hours). They doubled in size and turned from speckled red-brown to a more uniform pink. Discarded the soaking water.

**Cooking method:** Covered with fresh water, brought to a boil, skimmed the foam, reduced to a gentle simmer. Added two whole garlic cloves, a small sprig of rosemary, and a tablespoon of olive oil. Did NOT add salt until near the end — the salt-toughens-beans debate is largely settled (it doesn't, but the skins do toughen from acid, so I avoid adding tomatoes until fully cooked).

**Timing:** After 45 minutes, tested — still chalky at center. After 75 minutes, tested again — tender through, with a creamy texture and the skin mostly intact. A few had burst.

**Finished dish:** Drained most of the cooking liquid (kept some), added salt, a generous pour of olive oil, black pepper, a splash of red wine vinegar. Ate a bowl as-is with bread.

Also made a second portion: sautéed some shallots and pancetta, added the beans and a cup of cooking liquid, let it reduce to saucy. Added wilted spinach from the same market visit. This was the better of the two preparations — the pancetta fat integrates with the bean cooking liquid into something rich and porky.

Kept remainder in the fridge in cooking liquid, will use in soup over the next three days.

Dried bean sourcing and varieties: https://ranchogordo.com/blogs/recipes
