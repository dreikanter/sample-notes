# March retrospective

End of the month. Some accounting.

**Work:** Better month than February. The infrastructure migration finished; the Q2 planning is done and the scope feels realistic; the async standup experiment seems to be working. The deadline compression at the end of the month (see [[20240330_1459]]) was stressful but didn't produce bad outcomes.

**Running:** Back to consistent after the knee interruption. Ended the month at 28km total, which is low for a full month but correct for returning from rest. No pain in the last two weeks.

**Reading:** Finished Stoner (see [[20240320_1441]]) and most of the essay collection. Got through two Berger essays and the Sontag collection. Less than I'd have liked — the distraction problem is real.

**Cooking:** Kimchi batch is excellent, probably the best yet. The borlotti bean cook was worth the effort. Bread experiments continuing.

**Garden:** Seeds in — germination mostly good. Garden cleanup still not complete outside.

**What I want more of in April:**
- Morning writing before the workday starts — this happened three times in March, should be the default
- Less phone in the evening — the pattern of picking it up from habit rather than intention has gotten worse
- Longer single walks rather than short ones — the three-hour Sunday walk produced more thinking than any number of 45-minute loops

**What I'm carrying forward:**
- The async standup evaluation (ends first week of April)
- Tomato potting up
- Finish the essay collection
