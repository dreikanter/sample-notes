# Direct sow peas — March log

Planted sugar snap peas on March 4, which is probably seven to ten days earlier than recommended for this zone. The gamble: mild forecast through mid-March, and peas can handle light frost once established. If I wait for the safe window, I lose two weeks of growing season.

## Varieties planted

- Sugar Ann (bush type, determinate, 56 days) — reliable producer, no staking needed
- Mammoth Melting Sugar (pole type, indeterminate, 72 days) — needs a trellis, but the pods are the best-tasting I've grown

## Planting notes

Soaked seeds overnight in water before planting — reduces germination time slightly.

Planted 1 inch deep, 2 inches apart in the row, rows 18 inches apart. Inoculated with rhizobium powder (nitrogen-fixing bacteria that helps legumes establish). Not sure how much this helps with peas that have been grown in the same beds before, but costs nothing to do.

Covered the planted area with a single layer of frost cloth attached to wire hoops. The cloth creates a microclimate that might be 2–3°C warmer than ambient. Should be enough to protect against light frost.

## Expected timeline

- Germination: 7–14 days if soil temp is above 7°C
- First flowers: early May
- First harvest: mid-May for the bush type, early June for the pole type

## Notes

The bed where these are planted also had peas last year — moved them to a different bed than where I grew them in 2023 but still within the same raised garden area. Rotation is imperfect in a small garden.
