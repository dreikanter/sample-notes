# Coffee tasting log — Ethiopia Yirgacheffe

**Roaster:** Passenger Coffee (Lancaster, PA)
**Process:** Washed
**Roast date:** Feb 28
**Brew date:** March 12 (12 days post-roast — probably at peak)

**Brew method:** V60, 15g coffee, 250g water at 93°C, 3:00 total brew time

**Grind:** Medium-fine, 18 clicks on Comandante

**Tasting notes:**
- Dry aroma: bergamot, dried lemon peel, something faintly floral
- Wet aroma after bloom: jasmine becomes more prominent, some stone fruit
- First sip (hot): bright acidity, medium body, citrus peel finish that lingers
- Cooled (10 min): sweetness increases, almost like iced tea, lemon curd
- Cooled (20 min): slight funkiness appears, still pleasant, more complex

**Score (informal):** 8.5/10

**Notes:** Washed Yirgacheffes are predictably bright but this one has more body than I expected for a light roast. The floral notes are present but subtle — this isn't the perfume-bomb some Ethiopian naturals become. Would be excellent as a cold brew but that would hide the complexity.

**Compare to:** The Kenya Kirinyaga from the same roaster last month was more savory and less sweet, with a tomato-adjacent quality that was interesting but less immediately likeable.
