# Lentil soup recipe

A version I've refined over many iterations. The tempered spice finish at the end makes the difference between good and memorable.

## Ingredients (serves 4-5)

- 300g red lentils, rinsed until water runs clear
- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 large carrot, diced small
- 400g can whole tomatoes, crushed by hand
- 1.5L vegetable or chicken stock
- 1.5 tsp cumin (whole for tempering, ground for the base)
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- Salt, olive oil
- Lemon juice

**For tempering:**
- 3 tbsp good olive oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1/2 tsp Aleppo pepper (or paprika + pinch of cayenne)
- 2 cloves garlic, thin-sliced

## Method

1. Sweat onion in olive oil until soft and beginning to color, ~12 min over medium. Don't rush this.
2. Add garlic and carrot, cook 3 min. Add ground cumin, turmeric, paprika — stir into the oil, cook 1 min until fragrant.
3. Add lentils, tomatoes, stock. Bring to simmer, cook uncovered 25 min until lentils have dissolved completely.
4. Blend about a third of the soup for body while keeping texture. Season aggressively with salt and lemon juice.
5. **Tempering:** heat olive oil in small pan until shimmering. Add cumin seeds (they'll pop immediately), then garlic slices until golden. Remove from heat, add Aleppo pepper. Pour over soup just before serving.

Serve with good bread and more lemon on the side.
