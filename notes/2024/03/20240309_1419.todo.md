# Home maintenance spring checklist

Tasks to complete before April. Some of these rolled over from fall when I ran out of weekend.

## Outdoor

- [ ] Clean gutters — last done October, definitely full of winter debris
- [ ] Inspect roof flashing around chimney (noticed slight discoloration on ceiling, probably fine but check)
- [ ] Test outdoor hose bibs — turn on slowly, watch for pipe movement
- [ ] Clear dead plant material from beds, add 2" compost layer
- [ ] Check fence posts for frost heave, particularly the northeast corner post

## HVAC and indoor

- [ ] Replace furnace filter — 90-day type, last changed December 2
- [ ] Schedule AC tune-up before June (last year waited too long, no availability until July)
- [ ] Test smoke and CO detectors, replace batteries in hall unit
- [ ] Drain water heater sediment — haven't done this in two years, probably overdue
- [ ] Clean dryer vent duct — clogged vents are a fire risk

## Tools and equipment

- [ ] Sharpen lawn mower blade — has a nick from the hidden rock incident last August
- [ ] Replace spark plug while the mower is apart anyway
- [ ] Inventory garden tools, buy new trowel (old one is bent past usefulness)
