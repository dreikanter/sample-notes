# March cooking plan

Seasonal shift in what I want to cook. The heavy braises of December feel wrong now; the lighter, more vegetable-forward spring food feels right.

## The one new thing

**Khachapuri** — Georgian cheese bread, specifically the Adjarian boat-shaped version filled with egg and butter. I've been thinking about this since seeing it in a cooking video three months ago. The recipe looks manageable. The special equipment needed is minimal — just the right shaping.

## Repeats worth making again

- Ghormeh sabzi (see [[20240205_1367]]) — improve the lime preparation this time
- Dal (see [[20231216_1398]]) — the weekly staple, needs no improvement, just making
- Fresh pasta at least twice — maybe try cacio e pepe the pasta from scratch

## Seasonal ingredients to use

- First garden spinach from the cold frame (already harvesting small amounts)
- Blood oranges are still available — want to use them before the season ends
- Leeks are good right now — roasted leeks with vinaigrette and a soft egg is an underused meal

## What I'm not making

No more heavy braises until fall. The short rib season is over. The slow oven belongs to another month.

## The cooking-without-a-recipe experiment

One Saturday this month: cook a meal entirely without a recipe, just from what's in the refrigerator. Not improvising within a known structure — genuinely no plan. I'm curious what the baseline actually is after a year of more deliberate cooking.

Reference: [Samin Nosrat's *Salt Fat Acid Heat*](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/234726/salt-fat-acid-heat-by-samin-nosrat/) for the underlying principles when cooking without a recipe.
