# Khachapuri — first attempt

Made the Adjarian khachapuri (acharuli khachapuri — the boat-shaped version) on Sunday. More achievable than I expected.

## Dough

Simple yeasted dough: 300g flour, 7g yeast, 175ml warm water, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp salt, 2 tbsp vegetable oil. Mix, knead 8 minutes, rest 1 hour. The dough is straightforward and forgiving.

## Filling

The traditional filling is sulguni cheese, which I couldn't find. Used a mixture of low-moisture mozzarella (for the pull) and feta (for the salt and tang). Some recipes add halloumi. The critical requirement: the cheese should be salty and melty, not mild.

Grate or crumble 300g total cheese, mix with one beaten egg.

## Shaping

Roll each dough piece into an oval. Add filling down the center. Roll the two long edges toward the center to form the boat walls. Pinch the ends to form a sealed point. The boat should hold the filling without leaking.

Bake 220°C for 12–15 minutes until the dough is golden and the cheese is fully melted and beginning to bubble.

Immediately crack one egg into the center of each boat, return to oven for 2 minutes — just until the white is barely set but the yolk is still completely runny.

Finish with a generous piece of cold butter placed in the egg, and eat immediately by stirring the butter and egg together into the cheese and tearing off pieces of the bread to dip.

## Notes

The result was excellent. The egg-butter-cheese combination is rich beyond reason but works because the bread provides structure and mild contrast.

Reference: [Darra Goldstein's *The Georgian Feast*](https://www.ucpress.edu/book/9780520219397/) for the traditional recipe and context.
