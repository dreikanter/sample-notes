# Library visit

Went specifically to return two books I've been renewing for six weeks and not reading. The renewal limit is three times; I was on renewal three. Returning them without finishing felt like defeat but keeping them felt worse — I wasn't going to read them before the renewal ran out and I had real things I wanted to read.

While there, browsed the new non-fiction section for twenty minutes. The library acquires new books well — a good mix of the obvious things (whatever is being reviewed everywhere) and books I've never heard of that turn out to be interesting. Pulled three books to examine:

1. A history of the North American grid that seemed promising but was poorly written in the first chapter — returned to shelf.
2. A collection of essays by a Senegalese novelist I hadn't heard of — took this one.
3. A book about the science of flavour pairing in cooking — also took this, even though I own several cooking science books. The approach looked different enough.

The library has reorganized the magazine section since my last visit. The periodicals are now arranged by interest area rather than alphabetically, which I think is worse. I had to ask where the literary magazines were. They've added more large-format art books to a display near the entrance which is good — these are expensive enough that they belong in a library.

Sat in the reading room for an hour with the essay collection. The room was three-quarters full on a Tuesday afternoon. Mostly older adults, a few university-age people. Everyone doing the thing the internet says nobody does anymore.

Library catalog for those who want it: https://www.overdrive.com/
