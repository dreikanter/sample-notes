# Garden tasks — March

The spring work list. This is the busiest garden month because you're both cleaning up from winter and preparing for the growing season.

## Immediate

- **Turn under the cover crop rye** — week of March 4. The plants are about 8 inches tall, still easy to incorporate. Any later and they set seed and become weeds.
- **Harvest remaining cold frame spinach** — getting bitter, past its best, but still usable
- **Move garlic mulch** — the straw is now keeping the soil too cold, slowing emergence. Pull back to 2 inches.

## Week of March 11

- **Start pepper and basil seeds indoors** — four weeks before tomato transplant date
- **Order any remaining seeds** — missed the cool-season lettuce in the January order
- **Check irrigation system** — the drip timer battery dies every winter, needs replacement

## Week of March 18

- **Direct sow peas** — soil temp should be 45°F by mid-March, peas can go in
- **Turn compost pile** — it's been sitting all winter, needs air
- **Begin hardening off tomato seedlings** — bring outside for a few hours daily, increasing exposure

## Late March

- **Prep beds** — incorporate compost into the tomato and pepper beds
- **Plant cool-season transplants** (broccoli, cabbage) if I can find them at the nursery

## Notes on the garlic

All six rows have emerged and look healthy. The Music variety is showing broader leaves than the German Red, which I've read is typical — Music is a hard-neck variety with larger cloves and more vigorous early growth.
