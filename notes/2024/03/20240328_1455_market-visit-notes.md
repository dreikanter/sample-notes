# Market visit notes — late March

The Saturday market runs year-round but this is the point in the spring where it starts feeling alive again rather than dutiful. Vendors who were offering root vegetables and storage apples since October are beginning to have something different.

**What was available:**
- Greenhouse tomatoes from the grower at the south end — cherry size, decent flavor for this time of year, nothing like summer but better than supermarket
- Early rhubarb from two vendors, the stems much paler than summer rhubarb and slightly more tender
- Overwintered spinach from a farm about forty kilometers north — spent the winter under row cover, intense flavor, small leaves
- Dried beans in eight varieties from the legume-specialist stall, which is always there and I never buy from enough
- Greenhouse herbs: parsley, cilantro, dill
- Several vendors still on last year's apple storage — Honeycrisp getting mealy, but a Newtown Pippin from one vendor was still firm and tart

**Bought:**
- Rhubarb (600g) — plan to make a compote for yogurt through the week
- Spinach — will wilt with garlic and serve with eggs
- A bag of Borlotti beans from the dried bean stall, which I intend to actually cook this time instead of admiring and forgetting

The coffee vendor was doing a pour-over bar that had a line of ten people, which seemed surprising for 8:30am at an outdoor market in March. But the temperature was nearly 8°C and sunny — practically tropical after February.
