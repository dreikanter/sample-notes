# Seed starting update — March 26

Two weeks since seeding (see [[20240311_1423]] for initial entry).

**Germination rates:**
- Tomatoes (Roma type): 7 of 8 cells germinated, all looking healthy, first true leaves beginning
- Tomatoes (beefsteak saved seed): 5 of 8 germinated, slightly lower rate than commercial seed which is normal for saved seed
- Basil: 9 of 10 cells, dense and lush, probably need thinning this week
- Parsley: still only 3 of 8, but parsley is famously slow and I planted fresh seed so it should continue. Two of the three are looking pale — may be light-deficient
- Peppers (added a week after the others): not yet germinating, which is expected

**Issues:**
- The basil closest to the grow light edge is leggy — the light spread isn't even at the periphery. Rotated the tray 180°.
- One tomato seedling has a very slight purple tinge on the undersides of the leaves, which indicates phosphorus deficiency in cold soil. The porch temperature has been running 10-11°C nights, which locks out phosphorus uptake even when it's present. Will move the tray inside at night.

**Next steps:**
- Thin basil to one plant per cell
- Pot up tomatoes when the second set of true leaves is fully open (probably a week from now)
- Continue hardening off in stages once outdoor temperatures are consistently above 5°C overnight

Phosphorus deficiency in seedlings: https://extension.usu.edu/garden/research/phosphorus-deficiency-tomato
