# Early spring birds

Running list since March 1, adding as I see new arrivals. Migration timing is earlier than the historical average here — consistent with what the breeding bird survey data shows nationally.

**Confirmed since March 1:**
- Red-winged blackbirb — first heard March 3 from the reservoir, now heard everywhere
- Common grackle — arrived in a big mixed flock with the starlings March 9
- Eastern phoebe — March 14, calling from the old nest site under the shed eave
- American woodcock — heard peenting from the back field on March 17 at dusk. Didn't see it in the low light but unmistakable
- Tree swallow — March 19, two birds checking the nest boxes, a week earlier than last year
- Killdeer — March 20, two in the empty lot on Elm Street, running their broken-wing act at the dog walkers

**Still waiting for:**
- Wood thrush (usually mid-April, confirmed late migrant)
- Baltimore oriole (usually first week of May here)
- Warblers in general — a few yellow-rumped expected mid-April

**Year-round residents that suddenly seem louder:**
- Black-capped chickadee — singing the fee-bee song constantly now, breeding territory established
- White-breasted nuthatch — more vocal, saw one carrying nesting material

The phoebe at the shed is the most reliable marker I have — it has used that nest for at least six years running. Same individual or offspring using the same spot, impossible to know.
