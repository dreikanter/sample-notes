# Recipe — cold brew coffee

After years of buying cold brew at absurd prices, I've been making my own. This ratio works reliably.

## Ingredients

- 100g coarsely ground coffee (medium-dark roast works best)
- 900ml filtered water
- Fine mesh sieve + paper filter or cheesecloth for straining

## Method

1. Combine coffee and water in a large jar or container. Stir to ensure no dry grounds.
2. Cover loosely and leave at room temperature for 12-18 hours. 12 is the minimum; 24 makes it slightly more bitter and I don't prefer it.
3. Strain through a fine mesh sieve lined with a paper filter. Don't squeeze or press — let it drain by gravity. This takes about 20 minutes.
4. Transfer to a clean bottle. Refrigerate.

## Notes

- This produces a concentrate. Dilute 1:1 with water or milk to serve.
- Keeps in the fridge for up to two weeks without noticeable degradation.
- Cheaper coffees work fine for cold brew — the cold extraction is forgiving and the brewing process smooths over some harshness.
- I've tried the [Toddy system](https://toddycafe.com/) and find the setup too fussy for no meaningful improvement over a mason jar and a sieve.

## Variations tried

- Adding a cinnamon stick during steeping: nice, subtle warmth
- Vanilla bean: too sweet for everyday use but good as a treat
- Cardamom: didn't work, got bitter
