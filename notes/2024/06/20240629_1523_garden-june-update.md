# Garden June update

The allotment is in full early-summer mode. Some successes, some disasters, and one complete mystery.

**Doing well:**
- Courgettes are already flowering. Two plants this year — last year's four was too many by July.
- The climbing beans have reached the top of their canes. Purple-podded variety from a small seed company: [Real Seeds](https://www.realseeds.co.uk/). These are performers.
- Basil in the polytunnel is lush. Keeps threatening to bolt but hasn't yet.

**Struggling:**
- Beetroot is patchy — probably didn't thin enough. Some beauties, some marble-sized disappointments. Will harvest the big ones next week.
- Chard got hit by slugs after last week's rain. Put down organic pellets but feeling guilty about it.

**The mystery:**
Something is eating my parsley at night. Not a slug — the leaves are cleanly cut rather than rasped. Possibly a caterpillar I haven't found yet. Will check in the evenings with a torch.

**Upcoming:**
Tomatoes are in flower. Planted a heritage mix this year — Tigerella, Black Krim, and Sungold. Fingers crossed the British summer cooperates. Last year the blight got everything in August.

Need to net the brassicas this week before the whites find them.
