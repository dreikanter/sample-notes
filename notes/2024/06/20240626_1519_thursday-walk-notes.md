# Thursday walk notes

Took the long route home from the library. Two hours of wandering through the industrial edge of the neighborhood, which I almost never do on foot.

The old printshop on Renard Street still has the ghost sign on the brick — faded but readable: "RELIABLE PRINTING CO. EST. 1923." The building is apartments now, covered in those fake-wood panel balconies that don't suit the original facade at all. I photographed it from three angles.

Further along, a canal path I didn't know existed. Runs parallel to the rail line for maybe a kilometer. The water was dark and still, and someone had left a plastic folding chair at the dead end of the path, facing the water. A very melancholy still life. I sat in the chair for a few minutes and watched a coot move through the reeds.

Found a community garden with a sign listing plot holders. Noticed three names I recognize from the local residents' group. Plot 7 was growing enormous courgettes that had clearly escaped notice and become marrow-sized.

Ended up at the Turkish bakery near the overpass. Got a cheese-filled börek and ate it standing at the counter. The owner was watching a dubbed American thriller on a phone propped behind the till.

Good Thursday. These aimless walks are worth protecting.
