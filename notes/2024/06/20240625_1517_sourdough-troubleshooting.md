# Sourdough troubleshooting log

My starter has been sluggish for the past two weeks. The bread I baked on Sunday came out dense and barely rose during the final proof. Trying to methodically diagnose the problem before the next bake.

**Symptoms:**
- Starter peaks about 10-12 hours after feeding instead of 4-6
- Bread crumb is tight, not open
- Crust is fine but internal texture is gummy near the center

**Possible causes I'm investigating:**

The kitchen temperature dropped significantly when we turned off the central AC. I've been fermenting at what I now realize is around 18°C rather than 24°C. This alone could explain the extended timing.

Also switched flour brands two weeks ago — went from a local stone-ground bread flour to a generic supermarket brand with lower protein content. The label says 11.2% protein vs the previous 13.5%.

**What I'm trying:**
1. Move starter to the oven with just the light on (gets to ~26°C)
2. Go back to the old flour — ordered a 5kg bag from [Shipton Mill](https://www.shipton-mill.com/)
3. Extend bulk fermentation by feel, not by time

Will log results after Friday's bake.
