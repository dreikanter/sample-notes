# Summer reading list

The stack on my nightstand has gotten embarrassing. I counted seven books last week, three of which I started and abandoned somewhere around page forty. This summer I'm being more deliberate about what I pick up and actually finishing before moving on.

Current lineup:

1. *The Dispossessed* by Ursula K. Le Guin — been meaning to read this for years after enjoying *The Left Hand of Darkness*. The anarchist utopia angle is exactly the kind of political speculative fiction I find most interesting.
2. *Dune Messiah* — finished the first book in May and want to continue while it's fresh.
3. *Piranesi* by Susanna Clarke — my sister keeps insisting and I trust her taste.
4. *Thinking, Fast and Slow* — started this twice. Third attempt with better commitment.

I'm tracking my reading on [The StoryGraph](https://www.thestorygraph.com/) this year instead of Goodreads. The mood and pace tracking is surprisingly useful for picking what to read next when I'm tired versus energized.

Goal is four books by end of August. Conservative, but achievable.
