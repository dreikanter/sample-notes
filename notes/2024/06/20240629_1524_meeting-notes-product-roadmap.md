# Meeting notes — product roadmap Q3

**Date:** June 29, 2024
**Attendees:** Priya, Leon, Sam, Dev (me), Fatima (PM)

## Context

Quarterly roadmap review. We had 8 items on the "maybe Q3" list and needed to cut to 4-5 for the sprint capacity we actually have.

## Decisions made

**In for Q3:**
1. Bulk export (invoices, contacts) — customer demand is clear, Sales has three accounts waiting on this
2. API rate limit improvements — engineering needs, not flashy but overdue
3. Dashboard filter persistence — UX fix, relatively small lift

**Deferred to Q4:**

- Mobile app — needs its own planning cycle, too big to slot in

## Tensions surfaced

Fatima's counter: we don't have the design capacity without pulling Yuna off the dashboard work. Unresolved, but mobile is now a Q4 guaranteed item rather than "maybe."
