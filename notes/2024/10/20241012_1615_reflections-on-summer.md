# Reflections on summer 2024

October 12th and the summer is definitively over. The garlic is in the ground, the lido is closed, the coat is back out. Time to look back.

## What the summer actually was

Better than most, honestly. The rate limiting feature shipped and went well. The bulk export feature shipped and went well. The essay got written — for the first time in two years I produced a finished long piece of writing that I'm not embarrassed by.

I swam more than I have in any year since I was a teenager. The lido made this possible and I'm grateful for it. There's something about outdoor swimming that indoor swimming (which I can do year-round) doesn't have — exposure to actual conditions, the light, the temperature, the sense of the season.

Edinburgh was good. Vienna [[20240704_1530]] was the highlight of the year in travel terms.

The allotment produced well and survived the blight season. The fermentation experiments (kimchi [[20240712_1538]], hot sauce [[20240828_1567]]) worked and have become part of the regular kitchen rotation.

## What didn't happen

The Elixir learning. The film photography. Finishing the distributed systems course (five lectures remain, not the six I thought in July). These will roll forward.

## The thing I want to remember

The lido at 7am in October when the water is 16°C and cold enough to make you present. The swim, then the flat white in the sun. That the season is worth marking properly.

[Parliament Hill Lido opens again in May 2025](https://www.cityoflondon.gov.uk/things-to-do/green-spaces/hampstead-heath/where-to-go/swimming-ponds-and-lido). I'll be there on the first morning.
