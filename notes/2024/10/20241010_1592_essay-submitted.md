# Essay submitted — five days late but done

The essay is done. Sent to two people I trust for feedback. If it gets any response other than polite encouragement I'll be surprised and grateful.

Final word count: 1,680 words, down from the first draft's 2,400. The cuts mostly came from section 2, which I rewrote structurally, and from the ending, which I rewrote three times.

The ending I ended up with: a specific example from a software project I read about — a transit app that assumed all users had smartphones with data, deployed in a city where transit-dependent riders were more likely to have limited data plans or feature phones. Not a failure of malice. A failure of imagination — the designers couldn't imagine a user unlike themselves. That specificity grounded the essay's more general argument in a way the previous abstractions didn't.
