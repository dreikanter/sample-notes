# Garlic planting notes

Planted today. Conditions: overcast, soil in good condition after recent rain.

## What I planted

- **Provence Wight** (softneck): 20 cloves. Good for UK conditions, large bulbs, stores well. Source: [Garlic Farm](https://www.thegarlicfarm.co.uk/).
- **Cristo** (hardneck): 12 cloves. First time trying a hardneck variety. Hardneck garlic has more complex flavour but shorter shelf life. If the flavour is as different as advertised, I'll shift more of the plot to hardneck next year.

## Method

- Broke bulbs into individual cloves just before planting (no earlier — exposed cloves dry out faster)
- Planted 10cm deep, 15cm apart in rows 30cm apart
- Pointy end up (slightly more important than people suggest — planted sideways, they'll still find vertical, but it delays emergence)
- Labelled each row clearly — I will forget which variety is which otherwise

## Expected timeline

Shoots should appear by November. Most growth is underground until early spring. Harvest is typically June/July when the lower leaves start to brown.

## Comparison with last year

Last year I planted a single unnamed softneck variety from a supermarket (not recommended — unknown provenance, possible disease). Decent harvest but nothing special. Using actual seed garlic this year with known variety characteristics.

## Also planted

Overwintering onion sets (Radar variety) alongside the garlic. Same depth, same spacing. These are faster — should be ready in May.
