# Garden notes — October 12

First proper cold night last night — dropped to 4°C according to my phone's weather app (felt colder on the plot). The tomato plants are done; pulled the remaining green fruits and composted the plants.

## State of the plot

**Cleared and ready:**
- The main bean and courgette beds are dug over and composted
- The tomato bed has had spent plants removed and compost worked in
- Ready for spring planting

**Still growing:**
- Chard is thriving in the cooler weather. Cut-and-come-again harvest will continue into November
- Purple sprouting broccoli (PSB) is establishing well after the September transplanting. Under net.
- January King cabbage: looking good. Should be ready in January/February.
- Garlic and onion sets: no visible shoots yet — normal for this stage

**Just planted:**
- Overwintering broad beans went in along the south-facing edge of one bed. Variety: Aquadulce Claudia, the traditional overwintering choice. These will establish over winter and get a head start in spring, cropping in May.

## The green tomatoes from yesterday

Made one last batch of chutney from the remaining green fruit. Slightly different to the September batch [[20240926_1582]] — added a tablespoon of fresh ginger and reduced the cayenne. Better balanced.

## Looking ahead

The plot is essentially in maintenance mode now until March. Monthly visits to check the brassica netting, water in very dry spells, and harvest chard and any standing crops.
