# Autumn reading list

Summer list mostly done. Assembling the autumn list now that the nights are longer and there's more time indoors.

## Fiction

1. **Marilynne Robinson — Gilead** — Been on my list for a long time. The follow-up to the summer reading theme of novels with strong moral seriousness.
2. **Olga Tokarczuk — The Books of Jacob** — A big winter novel. 900+ pages about a false messiah in 18th-century Poland. My sister says it's the best novel she's read in five years, which means I'll read it even if it kills me.
3. **Jenny Offill — Dept. of Speculation** — Short. A palate cleanser between the longer books.

## Non-fiction

4. **Ursula Franklin — The Ursula Franklin Reader** — Following on from *The Real World of Technology* [[20240905_1571]]. She wrote across five decades and I want the full range.
5. **Robert Caro — The Power Broker** — Famously long. Starting a chapter-a-day practice rather than treating it as a single reading project.
6. **Oliver Sacks — The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat** — Not sure why I've never read this. Correcting the gap.

## How I'm tracking it

Still on [The StoryGraph](https://www.thestorygraph.com/) — the mood and pace tracking is genuinely useful for choosing what to read next based on current energy.

Target: four books by end of December. *The Books of Jacob* counts as two.
