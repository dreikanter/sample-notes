# Recipe — dal makhani (slow version)

The proper version requires cooking the lentils for a long time. This is not a weeknight dish. Worth knowing anyway.

## Ingredients (serves 4-6)

- 250g whole black lentils (urad dal), soaked overnight
- 60g kidney beans (soaked overnight, or 1 tin)
- 3 tbsp butter + 2 tbsp oil
- 2 onions, finely diced
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp ginger, grated
- 400g tin crushed tomatoes
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp garam masala
- ½ tsp chilli powder
- 100ml double cream
- Salt to taste

## Method

1. Drain and rinse soaked lentils and beans. Cook in fresh water until completely soft, about 1.5 hours (or pressure cooker 30 minutes). Drain, reserving cooking liquid.

2. In a large, heavy pan, heat butter and oil together. Fry cumin seeds until popping. Add onion, cook until deep golden — about 15 minutes over medium heat. Don't rush this step.

3. Add garlic, ginger, fry 2 minutes. Add tomatoes, ground spices. Cook until oil separates, about 12-15 minutes.

4. Add cooked lentils and beans. Add 300ml reserved cooking liquid. Simmer on lowest heat for at least 45 minutes, stirring occasionally. Longer is better — restaurants cook this for 6-8 hours.

5. Stir in cream and garam masala. Season.

The overnight soak, the long onion cooking, and the extended simmer each contribute. Shortcuts produce a different dish.

[Dishoom's recipe in their cookbook](https://dishoom.com/cookbook/) is the restaurant reference point for this dish.
