## One thing I learned

The `RETURNING` clause in PostgreSQL can return any column after an `INSERT` or `UPDATE`, not just the primary key. Saved significant round-trips in the migration script. See [PostgreSQL docs on RETURNING](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/dml-returning.html).
