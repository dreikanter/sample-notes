# Garden October tasks

Getting the beds ready before the first frost. Checking off as I go.

## Immediate

- [ ] Cut back the spent dahlias to about 15cm
- [ ] Dig up dahlia tubers and cure in dry shed — label varieties (Bishop of Llandaff, Park Princess, Cafe au Lait)
- [ ] Net the brassicas — wood pigeons have been eyeing the purple sprouting broccoli
- [ ] Plant garlic cloves in the prepared bed (120 cloves, Solent Wight and Elephant varieties)
- [ ] Sow overwintering broad beans direct — Aquadulce Claudia is supposed to tolerate frost well

## This month

- [ ] Clear and compost spent tomato plants — check for blight before composting
- [ ] Lay cardboard mulch over the squash bed to suppress winter weeds
- [ ] Plant spring bulbs: 200 tulips (Purple Rain, Queen of Night), 150 narcissus (Jetfire, Thalia)
- [ ] Take pelargonium cuttings before frost
- [ ] Move lemon tree into the greenhouse

## Ongoing

- [ ] Weekly check on overwintering greens under fleece
- [ ] Top up bird feeders — suet balls and nyjer seed

The RHS has a useful [October gardening checklist](https://www.rhs.org.uk/advice/in-the-garden/october) that covers some things I always forget, particularly around pond maintenance before winter.
