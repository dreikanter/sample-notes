# Recipe — ribollita

The name means "re-boiled" — a soup that gets better each time it's reheated. A Tuscan peasant dish built around stale bread, beans, and whatever vegetables were at hand. It's October and this is exactly right for October.

## Ingredients (serves 6, keeps well)

- 400g dried cannellini beans, soaked overnight (or 2 tins)
- 200g stale sourdough, roughly torn
- 3 tbsp olive oil, plus more to finish
- 1 large onion, diced
- 3 carrots, diced
- 3 celery stalks, diced
- 5 garlic cloves, sliced
- 400g tin crushed tomatoes
- 400g cavolo nero (lacinato kale), stems removed, leaves chopped
- 1 sprig rosemary
- 2 bay leaves
- Salt, black pepper, chilli flakes
- Parmesan rind (optional but excellent)

## Method

1. If using dried beans: cook until tender, reserve cooking liquid.

2. In a large pot, heat olive oil. Soften onion, carrot, celery over medium heat, 12 minutes. Add garlic, rosemary, chilli, 2 minutes.

3. Add tomatoes, beans, cooking liquid (or water if using tinned), parmesan rind, bay leaves. Simmer 30 minutes.

4. Add cavolo nero. Simmer 20 minutes until very tender.

5. Add bread. Stir it in — it will dissolve and thicken the soup. Simmer 15 more minutes. The consistency should be very thick.

6. Rest overnight. The flavour improves dramatically. Reheat slowly, adding water to adjust consistency.

Finish each bowl with a pour of good olive oil.
