# Halloumi and roasted vegetable traybake

Reliable weeknight dinner. Roughly 40 minutes start to finish, and the oven does most of the work.

## Ingredients (serves 2)

- 225g halloumi, sliced 1cm thick
- 1 medium sweet potato, cubed
- 2 courgettes (or 1 large), sliced into thick rounds
- 1 red pepper, chunked
- 1 red onion, cut into 8 wedges
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt, pepper
- Handful of cherry tomatoes (added in last 10 minutes)
- To serve: flatbreads, Greek yogurt, pomegranate seeds, fresh mint

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 200°C fan.
2. Toss sweet potato, courgettes, pepper, and onion with 2 tbsp oil, paprika, oregano, salt, and pepper. Spread on a large baking tray. Roast 20 minutes.
3. Toss halloumi pieces with remaining 1 tbsp oil. Add to tray along with cherry tomatoes. Return to oven for 15–18 minutes until halloumi is golden and tomatoes are blistered.
4. Serve on warmed flatbreads with yogurt, a scatter of pomegranate seeds, and mint.

## Notes

The pomegranate seeds sound fussy but they're actually essential — the acidity and crunch cut through the richness of the halloumi perfectly. Adapted loosely from [Ottolenghi's vegetable traybake principles](https://ottolenghi.co.uk/blogs/recipes) — high heat, good oil, don't crowd the tray.

Leftovers are excellent cold the next day as a salad.
