# Autumn market visit

Went to the Saturday market on a cold, clear morning. The kind of day where you can see your breath but the sun is fully out and it doesn't feel miserable — just brisk.

The apple selection was the best I've seen this season. A vendor from about sixty kilometres north had Mutsu and Calville Blanc that I've never found at a supermarket. Bought a small bag of each. The Mutsu are enormous, almost alarming. The Calville Blanc have an odd angular shape and a faint wine-like smell that becomes more pronounced once you cut them open.

Also picked up a bunch of dried chiles — anchos and a bag labelled simply "smoked" that the seller said were a local variety grown in the valley. Used three of the smoked ones last night in a bean braise. They dissolved almost completely and left a dark, resinous depth that I haven't managed to get from commercial chipotles.

The market has a small section of second-hand books that people leave on a folding table. Found a 1981 paperback of Patrick Leigh Fermor's *A Time of Gifts* with someone's handwriting in the margins. The notes are brief — underlines and terse question marks, occasionally a name of a place the reader had apparently also visited.
