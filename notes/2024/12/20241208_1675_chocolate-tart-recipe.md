# Dark chocolate tart

Made for a dinner party — this is the kind of dessert that looks impressive but is genuinely not difficult. Serves 8.

## Components

**Shortcrust pastry:**
- 200g plain flour
- 100g cold butter, cubed
- 1 egg yolk
- 2-3 tbsp ice water
- Pinch of salt

**Ganache filling:**
- 250ml double cream
- 200g dark chocolate (70%), finely chopped
- 2 eggs
- 1 egg yolk
- 30g golden caster sugar
- Flaky salt for topping

## Method

**Pastry**: Rub butter into flour until it resembles breadcrumbs. Add egg yolk and ice water gradually, bring together into a disc. Wrap and refrigerate 30 minutes. Roll to 3mm, line a 23cm tart tin, blind bake at 180°C for 20 minutes. Remove beans and bake 5 more minutes until the base is biscuity.

**Ganache**: Heat cream to just below boiling. Pour over chopped chocolate. Let sit 2 minutes, then stir until smooth. Whisk eggs, yolk, and sugar separately, then combine with the chocolate mixture.

Pour filling into blind-baked case. Bake at 160°C for 20–25 minutes — the centre should have a very slight wobble. Finish with a small pinch of flaky salt across the surface.

**Cool completely before slicing** — a warm chocolate tart collapses.

## Notes

The [Nigel Slater chocolate tart recipe](https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2003/jan/12/recipes.food) is a benchmark. The key difference from his: I find the slightly lower oven temperature gives a more set, less fudgy result which holds better when sliced.

Serve at room temperature. A small quenelle of crème fraîche rather than whipped cream — the acidity is better against the dark chocolate.
