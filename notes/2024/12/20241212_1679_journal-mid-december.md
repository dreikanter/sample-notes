# Journal — mid December

The strange suspended quality of the days between the 10th and 22nd, when the year is clearly winding down but Christmas hasn't arrived yet. Everything is either in preparation or in pause.

Work has quieted to a hum. The last real sprint of the year finished on the 6th; what remains is support, documentation, and some preliminary planning for Q1. The relative quietness feels earned after a hard autumn.

I've been cooking more in the evenings — not elaborate things, just more deliberate. Slow-braised things. Stock made from the chicken carcass on Sunday. The miso soup [[20241117_1654]] has become a near-daily habit, which I think of as a small gift to future-me who would otherwise skip breakfast.

Listened to Handel's *Saul* again this week [[20241206_1673]], this time following along with the libretto. The experience of knowing what's being sung changes it. The jealousy that develops in Saul is much clearer in the text than it comes across in the music alone — or rather, the music expresses it in a way that you can hear without necessarily understanding as jealousy unless you know the story.

Reading: *Gilead* by Marilynne Robinson, just started. The voice is immediately absorbing. An old man writing letters to a child who won't read them until the man is dead. The novel knows exactly what it is from the first page and settles into that knowledge without hesitation.

The [Marilynne Robinson profile in The Atlantic](https://www.theatlantic.com/magazine/archive/2020/12/marilynne-robinson-america/616929/) from 2020 is useful context if you haven't read her before.

Cold, dark, still. It's the right kind of December.
