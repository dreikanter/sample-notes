# Christmas Eve and Christmas Day cooking plan

Hosting a group of eight. Keeping it manageable — the point is the company, not proving anything.

## Christmas Eve (Dec 24)

### Make ahead
- [ ] Make stock from the turkey neck and giblets — 3 hours on low
- [ ] Prepare the bread sauce base — can be finished day-of
- [ ] Make the cranberry sauce — will last well in the fridge
- [ ] Prep the vegetables: peel and par-boil the potatoes, trim the sprouts, peel and chunk the parsnips
- [ ] Set the table and sort out seating

### Drinks
- [ ] Make a batch of mulled wine — follows the recipe in [[20241203_1670]]
- [ ] Chill white wine and sparkling water

## Christmas Day (Dec 25)

### Morning
- [ ] 9:00am: Turkey out of the fridge to come to room temperature
- [ ] 10:00am: Prepare the turkey — butter under the skin, season cavity with lemon halves, thyme, bay
- [ ] 11:00am: Turkey into oven (190°C fan, about 2.5 hours for this size)
- [ ] Prepare cocktail snacks for arrival drinks

### Afternoon
- [ ] 1:00pm: Start the roast potatoes — duck fat in the pan, get it smoking
- [ ] 1:15pm: Roast potatoes in
- [ ] 1:30pm: Parsnips and carrots in — honey and mustard glaze
- [ ] 2:00pm: Turkey resting, potatoes finishing, make gravy from the pan drippings
- [ ] Finish bread sauce — add cream and butter at the end
- [ ] Sprouts: halved, roasted with pancetta and chestnuts

### Dessert
- [ ] Christmas pudding pre-steamed — heat in the pudding basin for 2 hours before serving
- [ ] Brandy butter made ahead and in the fridge
- [ ] Dark chocolate tart from [[20241208_1675]] as backup/alternative
