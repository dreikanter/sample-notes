# Second sourdough bake — improvements

Third serious bake (including the first attempt in [[20241103_1640]]). Applied the changes I noted then and experimented further.

## What changed

**Shaping**: Watched the [Full Proof Baking shaping video](https://www.youtube.com/@FullProofBaking) twice before this bake. Applied the envelope fold and then a tight letterbox roll. The pre-shape was better — rounder and with more obvious surface tension.

**Bulk fermentation**: Extended by about 45 minutes. Used the aliquot jar method to track rise — a small portion of dough in a straight-sided jar with an elastic band marker. Allowed it to rise 65% rather than the 50% I'd been aiming for.

**Scoring**: Sharpest blade I could find (a new lame blade rather than my old dull one). Made a decisive single score at 45 degrees rather than tentative parallel cuts. The ear was significantly better.

**Flour**: Replaced 100g of the white bread flour with strong wholemeal. Total: 350g bread flour + 100g wholemeal + 50g whole wheat. Hydration stayed at 75%.

## Result

The best bake yet. Open, irregular crumb — not perfectly "instagrammable" but clearly well-fermented. Ear is prominent. The crust had the crackle that indicates a good steam environment (Dutch oven at 250°C with lid on for 20 minutes).

Flavour: more complex than previous bakes. The wholemeal addition gives a nutty background note that plays well against the mild tang. The crumb was moist and tender.

## Next variation

Try an autolyse of 2 hours rather than 45 minutes. Also considering adding a small amount of rye to the flour mix for more flavour complexity.
