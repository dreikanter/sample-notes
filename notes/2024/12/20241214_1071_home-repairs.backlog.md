# Home repairs backlog — winter 2024

## Urgent

- **Bathroom extractor fan** — runs but barely moves air. Possibly the duct is partially blocked at the bend near the roof. Need to check from the outside; may require someone on a ladder.
- **Kitchen tap drip** — slow but persistent. Probably a worn cartridge. Brand is Hansgrohe Metris so parts should be findable. Have not attempted this yet.
- **Hallway light fitting** — flickering when cold. Likely a loose connection at the ceiling rose rather than the bulb. Switch off at breaker before opening.

## This winter

- Re-seal the gap between the skirting and the floorboards in the back bedroom. Cold air is clearly coming through in the corner near the window.
- Bleed the radiator in the study — it's warm at the bottom and cool at the top, which is the usual symptom.
- Replace two cracked tiles on the back step before frost makes them worse.

## Spring / outdoor

- Repaint the garden gate. The primer coat from two years ago is still mostly intact but the top coat has peeled badly.
- Clear and re-point the raised bed retaining wall — the mortar is friable in a couple of places.
