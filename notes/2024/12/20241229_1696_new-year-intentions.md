# New year intentions — 2025

Not resolutions in the conventional sense. The resolutions framing tends toward binary (kept/broken) in a way that doesn't match how change actually happens. These are intentions — directions to move in, things to pay attention to.

## Health and body

Walk every day — even ten minutes counts. I've already established this partially [[20241109_1646]]; formalising it as a daily intention rather than an occasional practice.

Cook more from whole ingredients and less from packaged shortcuts. Not a purity thing — convenience has its place — but I've noticed that the days I cook properly feel better.

Consistent sleep. The research on sleep is overwhelming and I consistently underinvest in it.

## Work

Go deeper on one or two technologies rather than sampling more. The breadth served me well in earlier career phases; depth is what matters now.

Write more — both internally (documentation, decision records) and externally. Writing is thinking. More writing, better thinking.

Don't optimise for looking busy. The distinction between activity and output has been a useful frame this year; stay with it.

## Reading and learning

Finish the books I start. I abandoned four books this year that I probably would have valued if I'd pushed through the slow section.

Keep the nature observation habit going — birds, fungi, garden. It's a reliable form of attention that costs almost nothing and gives back considerably.

## Relationships

Call rather than text, sometimes. Some people deserve more than a message.

See friends before things become occasions. Spontaneous is often better than scheduled.

## The meta-intention

The [Oliver Burkeman newsletter on time](https://www.oliverburkeman.com/the-impermanence-of-all-things) frames this better than I can: be more present to the things actually happening rather than the version of life I'm preparing for. This is the one.
