# Mulled wine — from-scratch attempt

Advent day 1. Made mulled wine using a recipe based on [Felicity Cloake's perfect mulled wine](https://www.theguardian.com/food/2010/dec/09/how-to-cook-perfect-mulled-wine) rather than a supermarket spice sachet.

## What I used

- 1 bottle of inexpensive but decent red wine (Tempranillo, Spain — about £7)
- 1 orange, sliced in rounds
- 6 cloves
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 3 star anise
- 6 cardamom pods, lightly crushed
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 75g caster sugar (or to taste)
- 100ml brandy (added at the end)
- Approximately 200ml water

## Method

Put everything except the brandy into a pan. Heat very gently — do not boil; you're trying to warm and infuse, not cook. Cloake's key instruction is to hold it at around 70°C for 20–25 minutes, just a gentle steam. Test sweetness and adjust.

Add brandy. Taste again. Serve in warmed glasses.

## Notes on the result

Substantially better than the sachet version. The flavour is brighter because you're controlling the spice balance — the commercial sachets tend to be clove-heavy and one-dimensional. The star anise gives a soft anise note that works with the cinnamon without competing.

The temperature control is genuinely important. Made a second batch at Christmas for a group of six and got distracted — it simmered for a few minutes and was noticeably flatter and more alcohol-forward. The gentle heat preserves something.

Would experiment next time with a slice of fresh ginger and reducing the cloves by one or two.
