# Solstice reflections

A few days after the actual solstice, the light is already measurably different — not longer in any way you'd notice at noon, but the quality of the late afternoon changes. By half past three the sky holds a particular orange band just above the roofline opposite.

This was a quieter end of year than most. Didn't travel anywhere. Cooked a lot. Went through a phase of making stocks — chicken, then a long pork bone stock that simmered for most of a Sunday and left the kitchen smelling of star anise for days. Used the pork stock to braise cabbage, which sounds modest but was one of the best things I ate all month.

Reading stalled somewhat. Started three books in December and finished none of them. One I put down deliberately (not the right moment), one I'm still working through slowly, one turned out not to be what I expected from the jacket description.

Managed to keep up with the evening walk habit through most of the autumn. The routine eventually settled into something I don't have to decide about — which is the point. The stretch along the river at dusk is different every time; see [[20241122_1002]] for a note from November.

Looking ahead without grand intentions. A few concrete things I want to do in the first quarter rather than a list of improvements to my character.
