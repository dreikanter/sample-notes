# Year-end journal — 31 December 2024

Last evening of the year. The house is quiet. Made pasta for dinner, opened a bottle of good red wine that I'd been saving without quite knowing why, and sat down to write.

The year was harder and better than I usually give years credit for being. Some things that seemed difficult turned out to be instructive. Some things I thought I'd handle well I handled poorly. The standard accounting.

What I notice looking back:

The fermentation projects [[20241023_1629]], [[20241108_1645]] were a surprisingly large part of what made the second half of the year feel like something I was actively inhabiting rather than getting through. The slow, physical, low-failure-cost nature of fermentation is a genuine counterweight to work that is fast, abstract, and heavily consequential.

The reading was the best it has been in several years. *Gilead* [[20241216_1683]] and *The Rings of Saturn* [[20241224_1691]] and *The Dispossessed* [[20241014_1618]] all belong to a class of books that reorganise something about how you see. Hard to articulate; real nonetheless.

I moved to a new flat this autumn. The disruption was more significant than I anticipated — not practically but psychologically. A new space requires a new relationship to routine. That's still in progress.

What I will carry into 2025: the morning walk habit [[20241109_1646]], the fermentation projects, the attention to reading, the intention to write more.

The midnight arrival of a new year is a technology for renewing intention. That it's arbitrary doesn't make it useless.

See the [intentions note for 2025 [[20241229_1696]]] for specifics.

Happy new year to whoever reads this.
