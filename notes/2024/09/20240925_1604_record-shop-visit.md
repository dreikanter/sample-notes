# Record shop visit — Saturday finds

Spent an hour in the second-hand section of a record shop on the high street. My rule is: spend no more than £30 and only buy things I'll actually listen to within a week.

## What I bought

**Bill Evans Trio — Waltz for Debby (Riverside, 1962 repress):** Found this in remarkably good condition. The original Village Vanguard recording — piano, bass, drums, with the ambient noise of the club faintly present on quieter passages. Bill Evans' left hand is doing something structural that I can't quite articulate but that the recordings from this period make very clear.

**Stereolab — Dots and Loops:** In the used section for £8. I'd known this record in my late teens and hadn't heard it in fifteen years. The Terry Riley influence, the motorik rhythm influence, and the pop melody instinct all operating simultaneously. It's a strange record.

**Ennio Morricone — The Good, the Bad and the Ugly (soundtrack):** Original Italian pressing of unclear vintage. Worth the gamble at £4.

## What I was looking for and didn't find

The second Chet Baker Blue Note album. Will have to order it properly.

## State of the record

Bill Evans was in perfect condition — quiet, clean, minimal surface noise. Stereolab has one click per revolution on side two that I can live with at the price. The Morricone is slightly scratchy but that suits the material.
