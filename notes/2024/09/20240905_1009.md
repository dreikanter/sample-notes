# Stargazing, quick notes

Clear night, no moon. Drove 40 minutes out of town to get past the
worst of the light dome. Used the 10x50 binoculars and the phone
planisphere.

Picked out:

- Cassiopeia low in the north, easy W shape
- Andromeda galaxy as a smudge in the binoculars, not as big as I
  always expect
- Jupiter plus three of the Galilean moons in a neat little line

The Perseids are long past, so no meteors to speak of. Next time,
bring a red flashlight — the phone screen wrecked my night vision
every time I checked the chart.
