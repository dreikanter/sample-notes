# Green tomato chutney recipe

The plants are slowing. Most remaining tomatoes are green or part-green and won't ripen fully before the nights get too cold. Making chutney.

## Ingredients (makes approximately 4 x 350ml jars)

- 1.5kg green tomatoes, roughly chopped
- 2 large onions, diced
- 3 cooking apples, peeled and diced
- 300g soft brown sugar
- 300ml cider vinegar
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- ½ tsp allspice
- ½ tsp cayenne pepper (or more — this is personal)
- 1½ tsp salt

## Method

1. Combine everything in a large, heavy pan. The volume will look alarming — it reduces significantly.
2. Bring to a boil, stirring to dissolve the sugar.
3. Reduce to a gentle simmer. Cook uncovered for 1.5-2 hours, stirring occasionally, until thick and jammy. The chutney is ready when a channel drawn through it with a spoon holds its shape.
4. Sterilize jars (oven at 120°C for 15 minutes, or dishwasher on hot). Pot while hot.
5. Seal and leave to mature for at least 3-4 weeks before eating. It improves significantly with time.

## Notes

Goes exceptionally well with strong cheese and anything that came off a grill. My aunt makes this every year and I've been working toward her version for several years — this batch is the closest yet.
