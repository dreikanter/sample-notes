# Allotment autumn preparation

September is when summer production starts to slow and winter planning begins. Tasks for the coming weeks.

## Cleared and done

- Climbing beans: plants removed, beds dug over with compost worked in
- Courgette: one plant showing significant powdery mildew; removed both to prevent spread
- Basil: finished for the year; made two large jars of pesto for the freezer

## Still producing

- Tomatoes: outdoor Tigerella and Black Krim still producing but slowing. Green fruit will ripen on a windowsill or become chutney.
- Chard: going strong. Will continue into winter under a cloche.
- Beetroot: a few plants left; will harvest this week.

## Autumn planting

- Garlic going in mid-October (timing matters — needs time to establish roots before ground freezes)
- Overwintering onion sets: same timing
- Winter brassicas: purple sprouting broccoli and January King cabbage seedlings are ready to go into the cleared beds. Net immediately.

## Soil work

The main beds need a top dressing of well-rotted compost. I have two bags of homemade compost that are ready. Ordering mushroom compost for the areas that went through heavy production this year.

The no-dig method I've been following from [Charles Dowding's work](https://charlesdowding.co.uk/) continues to pay off — the weed pressure is minimal compared to what these beds were when I started.

## Seed saving completed

- Climbing bean (purple-podded variety): 12 pods selected, drying on a rack
- Courgette seeds: washed and drying — they're free seeds, may as well try
