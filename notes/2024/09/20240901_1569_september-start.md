# September start

September has a quality of beginning that January doesn't. The light changes — noticeably in the last week of August, confirmed by September's first mornings. There's a cooling quality to it even when the temperature is still warm. Something about the angle.

August ended well. The rate limiting feature is stable. The tomatoes made it through the blight risk period — the preventive sprays worked or I was lucky, but the crop has been excellent. The kimchi and hot sauce have both produced interesting results.

What I'm carrying into September:

The long-form essay is started but not done. The outline is clear and the reading is mostly done. The actual writing is the part I'm circling. This is the familiar pre-writing avoidance. Setting a target: complete a first draft by September 15th, however rough.

The distributed systems course: two lectures left. Paxos remains. I've been putting off Paxos because everything I've read suggests it's harder to reason about than Raft. This is probably why I should do it sooner rather than later.

September also means the allotment season is winding down. The clearing and winterizing work begins — what to grow through winter (chard, certain brassicas), what needs to be composted, what seed saving to do properly.

The swimming lido closes in September. Last swim of the season needs to happen; I'll be deliberate about marking it.
