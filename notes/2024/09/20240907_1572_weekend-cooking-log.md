# Weekend cooking log — September 7

A good cooking weekend. The allotment produced enough to make it fully supply-driven rather than recipe-driven.

## Saturday

**Morning:** Porridge with poached plums. Found the last of the plums at the market — Italian ones, dark and slightly tart. Poached with a splash of red wine and a strip of orange peel. The kitchen smelled incredible.

**Lunch:** Courgette fritters with yogurt and harissa. Used a technique I'd seen on [Ottolenghi's site](https://ottolenghi.co.uk/blogs/recipes) — salt the grated courgette, squeeze aggressively to remove water, mix with egg, feta, and minimal flour. Fried in olive oil. The key is getting the pan hot enough that they form a crust quickly.

**Dinner:** Chicken thighs braised with tomatoes, olives, and capers. Thrown together. Fennel seeds and a bit of white wine in the braising liquid. Served with crusty bread to get the sauce.

## Sunday

**Breakfast:** Shakshuka from the recipe I wrote up in July [[20240707_1533]], using the last of this week's tomatoes.

**Afternoon project:** Attempted sourdough again. Starter is performing properly now after the adjustments in June [[20240625_1517]]. Bulk fermentation ran 4 hours at 23°C. Baked this evening — the crumb is open and the crust shatters correctly. Four weeks ago it would have come out gummy.

**Sunday dinner:** Simple — leftover braised chicken on toast, garden salad with the last of the climbing beans.
