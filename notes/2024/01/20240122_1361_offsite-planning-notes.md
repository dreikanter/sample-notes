# Team offsite planning — February logistics

Three days in February, the whole engineering team (eight people), goal is a mix of alignment work and genuine social time.

## What we're trying to accomplish

1. Get everyone on the same page about Q1 priorities (see [[20240105_1352]])
2. Have the conversations that don't fit in a Zoom — the ones about values and tradeoffs, not ticket status
3. Some unstructured time where ideas happen sideways

## Structure draft

**Day 1 (Monday):**
- Morning: travel and arrival
- Afternoon: current state review — what's working, what isn't, no agenda, just air time
- Evening: dinner, informal

**Day 2 (Tuesday):**
- Morning: technical direction workshop — read replica, reporting layer, future scaling
- Afternoon: team health topics — alert fatigue, documentation debt, on-call load
- Evening: optional activity (escape room is popular but I want to suggest something less structured)

**Day 3 (Wednesday):**
- Morning: Q1 commitments and working agreements — what do we agree to hold each other to?
- Afternoon: travel home, remote

## What I want to avoid

- Death by presentation. Talks with slides limit conversation.
- Treating the offsite as a planning meeting with better coffee. The planning can happen in writing before we arrive.
- Packing every hour. The conversations that happen over breakfast are often the best ones.

## Open questions

- Location: leaning toward a rented house rather than hotel — different social dynamic
- Size of group for activities: should the whole team do everything together, or some parallel tracks?

Reference: [Basecamp's remote team offsite guide](https://basecamp.com/guides) has useful framing even for hybrid teams.
