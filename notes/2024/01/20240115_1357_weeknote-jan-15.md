# Week of 2024-01-15 notes

## Work

Read replica planning meeting on Tuesday. Decided to start with the two heaviest reporting queries rather than trying to route all read traffic at once. Lower risk, faster learning. The replication lag question came up immediately — our reporting queries don't need real-time data, so 5–10 seconds of lag is acceptable. Wrote that assumption down explicitly so we don't have to re-argue it.

The alert audit is done. Reduced from 47 configured alerts to 31 by eliminating duplicates and anything that hadn't triggered an actionable response in the past sixty days. Still needs the team to review before I call it closed.
