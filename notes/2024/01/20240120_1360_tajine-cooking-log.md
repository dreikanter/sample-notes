# Tajine cooking log — chicken and preserved lemon

First time cooking in the tagine I received as a gift last spring and promptly left in the cabinet for eight months. Addressed that today.

## The dish

Chicken with preserved lemon, olives, and saffron — the Moroccan restaurant dish that I've always assumed was harder to make than it is. It isn't hard. It requires patience and good preserved lemons.

## Ingredients (serves 4)

- 1 whole chicken, cut into 8 pieces, or 4 bone-in thighs
- 1 preserved lemon, rind only, rinsed and sliced thin
- 1 cup green olives (Castelvetrano work well)
- 1 large onion, grated or very finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- Good pinch of saffron, bloomed in 3 tbsp warm water
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp black pepper
- Olive oil, salt
- Handful of cilantro and flat-leaf parsley

## Method

Marinate the chicken in oil, ginger, cumin, turmeric, pepper, garlic for at least two hours. Heat tagine base on low with a heat diffuser — don't rush this. Cook onions until translucent. Add chicken, brown lightly. Add saffron water, a splash of plain water. Low heat, covered, 45 minutes. Add preserved lemon rind and olives for the last 15 minutes. Finish with fresh herbs.

## Notes

The heat diffuser is not optional. Direct high heat will crack the tagine.

The preserved lemons are doing most of the work. The salt, funk, and brightness they bring is not replaceable by fresh lemon.

Reference: [Paula Wolfert's Moroccan recipes](https://paulawolfert.com/) remain definitive.
