# Kimchi follow-up — day five tasting

Follow-up to [[20240712_1538]].

Opened the jar after five days at room temperature (averaging 20-22°C in the kitchen). Smells: funky, bright, lactic. Good signs.

**Taste:**
Fermentation is clearly happening — there's real sour depth behind the gochugaru heat. The cabbage has softened but still has structure. The fish sauce / soy hybrid worked; there's umami but it's not fishy in an unpleasant way.

**Verdict:** Ready to move to the fridge. I'll eat from it over the next 2-3 weeks. Kimchi continues fermenting in the fridge, just much more slowly, and many people prefer it aged 2-4 weeks when the sourness deepens.

**What I'd adjust for next time:**
- Double the spring onion — it's the thing that got most lost
- Add a small piece of Asian pear or apple to the paste for sweetness and enzymatic action
- Gochugaru quantity was right; resist the temptation to add more when making the paste

**How I'll use it:**
- Straight from the jar as a side
- In a fried rice with leftover rice and a fried egg
- As a topping for savoury porridge (juk-style)
- Kimchi pancakes (jeon) when it gets more sour in a couple of weeks

This was easier than I expected. Going to make a larger batch next month.
