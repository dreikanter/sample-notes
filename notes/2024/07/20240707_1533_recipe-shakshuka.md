# Recipe — shakshuka

This is the version I've settled on after trying many. Not strictly traditional but works well.

## Ingredients (serves 2-3)

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 red pepper, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp chilli flakes (more to taste)
- 400g tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 tbsp harissa paste
- 4 eggs
- 80g feta, crumbled
- Small bunch flat-leaf parsley

## Method

1. Heat oil in a wide heavy pan. Soften onion and pepper over medium heat, about 8 minutes.
2. Add garlic and spices. Cook 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add harissa, tomatoes, and a pinch of salt. Simmer 10-15 minutes until thick.
4. Make four wells in the sauce. Crack an egg into each.
5. Cover and cook 5-7 minutes. I like the yolks barely set — pull them sooner than you think.
6. Scatter feta and parsley. Eat directly from the pan with good bread.

## Notes

- The harissa is not negotiable. Worth having in the fridge always.
- Adding a handful of spinach at step 3 works well and uses up a bag that might otherwise go sad.
- Yotam Ottolenghi's [Plenty](https://ottolenghi.co.uk/blogs/recipes) has the canonical version if you want to compare.
