# Fermentation log — kimchi attempt one

**Date started:** July 12
**Expected ready:** July 15-17 (three to five days at room temp)

## What I made

Traditional baechu kimchi with napa cabbage. Recipe based loosely on Maangchi's version — her [website and recipes](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/tongbaechu-kimchi) are extremely thorough.

## Process

**Salting (2 hours):**
Cut one head of napa cabbage into quarters, then into 5cm pieces. Tossed with 3 tablespoons coarse sea salt and left for 2 hours, turning every 30 minutes. The cabbage wilted significantly and released a lot of water.

Rinsed three times and tasted — still slightly salty, which is correct.

**Paste:**
- 4 tbsp gochugaru (Korean chilli flakes — bought from a Korean grocery online, not a substitute)
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp soy sauce (would use 2 tbsp fish sauce normally, but testing hybrid)
- 1 tsp sugar
- 4 garlic cloves, grated
- 1 tsp ginger, grated
- 3 spring onions, sliced

Mixed everything, massaged into the cabbage thoroughly. Filled into a 1-litre jar and pushed down to remove air pockets. Left 2cm headspace.

## Observations

- Colour is a deep, vivid red
- Room temperature is 21°C — may need 4-5 days before it tastes properly fermented
- Will taste on day 3

## What I expect to adjust next time

The spring onion amount — add more. The paste felt slightly thicker than online photos suggest.
