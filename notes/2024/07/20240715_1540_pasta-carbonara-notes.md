# Pasta carbonara — technique notes

Carbonara is the recipe that consistently separates people who cook from people who can cook. The ingredient list is short; the technique is unforgiving.

## The ratio that works for me (per person)

- 100g pasta (rigatoni or spaghetti — rigatoni is better for the sauce adhesion)
- 80g guanciale or pancetta (guanciale if you can get it)
- 1 whole egg + 1 yolk
- 30g Pecorino Romano, finely grated
- 10g Parmesan, finely grated
- Black pepper, freshly and generously ground

## The method

1. Get pasta water boiling and salt it properly.
2. Render guanciale over medium heat until the fat runs and the meat is golden but not crisp. Remove from heat.
3. Mix eggs, cheese, and pepper in a bowl. The mixture should be thick.
4. Cook pasta 2 minutes short of packet time. Reserve a cup of pasta water.
5. Drain pasta, add to guanciale pan off the heat. Toss.
6. Add egg mixture. Toss rapidly, adding pasta water a splash at a time until it becomes a glossy sauce. This is the critical moment — pan must not be hot enough to scramble the eggs.

## The two things people get wrong

1. Putting the pan back on the heat after adding eggs. Don't. The residual heat and pasta starch are enough.
2. Using cream. There's no cream in carbonara.

Vincenzo's Plate has [a good comparison video](https://www.vincenzosplate.com/) of guanciale vs pancetta if you want to see the difference.
