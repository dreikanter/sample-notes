# Vienna travel notes

Four days in early July. I'd been once before, briefly, passing through. This was the first time to actually inhabit the city.

## What I didn't expect

The humidity. Vienna in July is genuinely hot — not Mediterranean beach hot but stickily warm in a way that makes the grandiose interior spaces feel absolutely justified. The Kunsthistorisches Museum was partly about the art and partly about the air conditioning.

The coffeehouse culture is real and not a tourist invention. Café Central near the Freyung has been in continuous operation since 1876. You can sit for two hours over a Melange and a newspaper and no one minds. I did this twice.

## Highlights

**Belvedere:** Klimt's *The Kiss* in person is different from every reproduction. The gold genuinely shimmers and the scale is larger than most prints suggest. The upper palace also has a Schiele room that's unexpectedly moving.

**Naschmarkt:** Saturday morning is the right time. Lots of tourists but also genuine shopping happening. Bought hard cheese and a jar of Styrian pumpkin seed oil.

**Prater and the Riesenrad:** The old wooden Ferris wheel from 1897 moves very slowly. The compartments smell of old wood and machine oil. Recommended.

## Logistics

Stayed in the 7th Bezirk, which was walkable to most things and less expensive than the 1st. The U-Bahn is excellent. [Vienna City Card](https://www.wiencitycard.at/) is worth it for 72 hours.
