# Lido morning swim

Finally went. 7:15am, the water temperature board said 19°C which felt optimistic. Let's say 17°C. The shock of it as I went in was the kind that makes you gasp and then immediately feel more awake than you've felt in weeks.

The lido is 50 meters, unheated, outdoors. Eight lanes. At that hour there were maybe thirty people in the water — mostly regulars who knew each other, had their lanes, their rhythms. A woman in lane four was doing butterfly with a metronome efficiency that was very calming to watch. An older man in lane six was doing a slow but impeccable backstroke, not hurrying, stopping to look at the sky between lengths.

I did twelve lengths before the cold caught up with me. My hands went a bit numb. Got out, stood in the sun for a few minutes on the wet concrete. There's a small café that opens at 7:30 and I had the best flat white I've had in months — it might just be the contrast effect, but the coffee genuinely tastes different after cold water.

I'll go on Wednesday. This is the right way to start a morning in summer.

The [Parliament Hill Lido](https://www.cityoflondon.gov.uk/things-to-do/green-spaces/hampstead-heath/where-to-go/swimming-ponds-and-lido) website has opening times and temperatures updated daily.
