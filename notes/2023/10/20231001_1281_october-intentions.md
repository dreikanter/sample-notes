# October intentions

## The season

October is when the year begins to feel like it has a second act. The garden enters its dormant phase, the cooking gets heavier, the evenings compress. It's a good month for sustained indoor work.

## What I want to do less

- Checking the news at night. It doesn't make me better informed; it makes me anxious about things I can't affect.
- Multitasking during meetings. I've slipped back into this.
- Treating rest as something earned rather than something necessary.

## What I want to do more

- Write something that isn't notes or a work document. A longer personal essay, maybe. I've been circling the idea since the equinox [[20230921_1271]].
- Cook things that take time. Long braises, stocks, the bean dishes that need soaking and slow heat. The season makes this natural.
- Walk daily, even when it's cold. Especially when it's cold.
- Sit with the Rust learning [[20230927_1277]] and actually write a small project — not just read about it.

## One specific goal

Get the garlic planted by October 25 [[20230929_1279]].

## The dentist

I know. I wrote it in the calendar for October 3rd at 9am. I will call before the appointment reminder fires.

## Reading

Continue Proust (long game, no deadline). Start something new alongside it — shorter, different register. Maybe Marilynne Robinson's "Gilead."
