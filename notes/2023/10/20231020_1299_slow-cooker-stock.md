# Slow cooker stock — method and notes

Switched from stovetop to slow cooker for stock this autumn and I'm not going back. The results are cleaner and the process is nearly passive.

**Ingredients for 3 liters:**

- 1 chicken carcass (from a roasted bird) plus 500g chicken wings or necks
- 2 medium carrots, roughly chopped
- 2 celery stalks with leaves, roughly chopped
- 1 large onion, halved
- 1 head garlic, halved crosswise
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tsp black peppercorns
- Small handful flat-leaf parsley stems
- Cold water to cover

**Method:**

1. No roasting or blanching — the slow cooker method produces a light, clear stock. If you want a darker roasted stock, roast the bones and vegetables in the oven first at 200°C for 45 minutes.

2. Add everything to the slow cooker. Cover with cold water.

3. Cook on LOW for 12–18 hours (I set it before bed, strain in the morning).

4. Do not stir, do not let it reach a boil. Low, slow, undisturbed — the stock stays clear.

5. Strain through a fine mesh strainer lined with cheesecloth. Let fat rise and skim, or refrigerate overnight and lift off the solidified fat.

**Result:** A stock that is noticeably cleaner and more delicate than stovetop. The clarity is better. The flavor is gentler — excellent for sauces and risotto where you don't want the stock to dominate.
