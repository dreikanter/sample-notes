# Reflection — one year since starting these notes

I started keeping this notes system last October — one year ago now. Worth reviewing what's changed and what's stayed the same.

**Volume:** I've written more notes than I expected. Something about the low friction of the format (no pressure to publish, no audience, just capture) made it easier to maintain the habit than journaling in previous attempts.

**Categories that emerged naturally:**

- Cooking logs and recipes
- Reading notes — this is probably the category I use most
- Technical references and cheatsheets
- Garden observations
- Work meeting notes and design documents
- Journal entries and reflections

I didn't plan these categories; they reflect what I actually think about.

**What's been most useful:**

The cross-references. When I was working on the infrastructure migration this fall, I found myself referencing the post-mortem from August [[20230806_1223]] and the Kubernetes notes I'd written as preparation. Having the trail of thinking available and linked made me more confident in the decisions.

**What I'd change:**

I wish I'd been more consistent about dating observations. Several early notes have approximate dates in the filename but the content doesn't anchor to a specific event. Hard to reconstruct the context.

**One unexpected benefit:**

Writing things down at all has changed how I think about them. There's something in having to articulate an observation — even just for myself — that sharpens it. I notice more because I write about what I notice.
