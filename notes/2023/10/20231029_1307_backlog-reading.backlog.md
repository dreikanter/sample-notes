## Fiction

- "The Recognitions" — William Gaddis. A reader I respect called it one of the great American novels. Long (956 pages) and difficult. Source: https://www.dalkeyarchive.com/
- "The Book of the New Sun" — Gene Wolfe. Recommended twice in the past year by different people with good taste.
- "Middlemarch" — George Eliot. I've been meaning to read this for fifteen years.
- "Pedro Páramo" — Juan Rulfo. Short (90 pages), enormously influential on García Márquez. No excuse for not having read it.
- "A Gentleman in Moscow" — Amor Towles. A friend loved it. I'm skeptical but willing.
