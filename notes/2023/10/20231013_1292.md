# White bean and kale stew with rosemary oil

A recipe that evolved over three or four iterations this autumn. It's the kind of dish that improves with repeated cooking because you develop feel for when to add what. This is the version I'm now happy with.

**Ingredients (serves 4):**

- 300g dried cannellini beans, soaked overnight and cooked until just tender (or 2 cans, drained)
- 1 bunch lacinato kale, ribs removed, leaves torn
- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, sliced thin
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 1 carrot, diced
- 400g canned whole tomatoes, crushed by hand
- 1 litre chicken stock (or vegetable)
- Parmesan rind (if you have it — adds significant depth)
- Salt, black pepper, chili flakes

**Rosemary oil:**
- 4 tbsp good olive oil
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 1 large garlic clove, smashed

**Method:**

1. Make rosemary oil first: gently heat oil with rosemary and garlic until fragrant and just sizzling. Do not let it brown. Strain and reserve.

2. Soften onion, celery, carrot in a little regular olive oil. 10 minutes, low heat.

3. Add sliced garlic, chili flakes. Cook 2 minutes.

4. Add tomatoes. Cook until reduced and darkened, about 12 minutes.

5. Add beans, stock, Parmesan rind. Simmer 20–25 minutes.

6. Add kale, simmer until tender, 5–8 minutes. Adjust seasoning.

7. Serve with a generous spoon of rosemary oil over each bowl.

The rosemary oil transforms the dish. Without it, it's fine. With it, it's excellent.

Reference on bean cookery: https://www.seriouseats.com/how-to-cook-dried-beans
