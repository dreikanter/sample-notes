# Garlic planting day — October 14

Planted a week earlier than planned [[20230827_1246]] because the forecast showed consistent temperatures below 10°C starting next week. Better to get the cloves in the ground now so they establish roots before the cold sets in.

## Bed preparation

The bed that held the tomatoes (cleared in September [[20231252_1252]]) had been sitting under 2 inches of compost. Forked it in this morning and added bone meal at 100g per square meter worked into the top 6 inches. Good, friable soil — the tomatoes left good structure behind.

## Planting

Cracked the heads from Southern Exposure this morning. Yields:
- Chesnok Red: 8 heads → 72 cloves (these had large secondary layers)
- German Red: 6 heads → 39 cloves (true to reputation: large, fewer per head)
- Inchelium Red: 5 heads → 51 cloves

Total: 162 cloves planted.

Row spacing: 12 inches. Within-row spacing: 6 inches. Planted with a dibber, 3 inches deep, pointed side up. This is slightly shallower than the recommended 4 inches but the soil temperature was warmer than I'd like and I don't want to delay root development more than necessary.

Covered the bed with 3 inches of straw mulch after planting. This moderates soil temperature and prevents frost heave.

## Markers

Labeled each variety's section with painted stakes (recycled from the tomatoes). I always think I'll remember and I never do.

Expected emergence: late March, depending on winter. First harvest of scapes: late May.
