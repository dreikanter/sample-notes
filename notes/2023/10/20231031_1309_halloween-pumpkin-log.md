# Halloween pumpkin — carving notes and seed roasting

This is a small tradition I've kept since moving into this apartment: buy a pie pumpkin from the market, carve it, roast the seeds.

## The pumpkin

Small Sugar Pie variety, about 2.5kg. These are actually a better carving size than the large field pumpkins — more manageable, and the flesh is edible unlike the watery field varieties.

## Carving

Did a simple design this year — a stylized owl face with triangle eyes and a round open beak. Took about 45 minutes with a thin serrated knife. Scored the design on paper first and transferred it with a pin.

## Seed roasting

This is the worthwhile part.

**Process:**
1. Separate seeds from pulp — patience, this takes time. A colander and running water helps.
2. Spread on a baking sheet and let dry 1–2 hours (or overnight in a warm oven).
3. Toss with olive oil and salt, and any seasoning.

**Seasoning variations tried:**

*Classic:* just salt, maybe smoked paprika. Best.

*Sweet:* brown sugar, cinnamon, cayenne. Good but I don't want to eat a lot.

*Umami:* tamari + sesame oil + garlic powder. Excellent with beer.

**Bake:** 160°C for 20–25 minutes, stirring halfway. They're done when golden and crisp.

Yield from this pumpkin: 180g of seeds. A surprisingly filling snack throughout the evening.

The carved pumpkin went on the steps until it starts to collapse, which here in this climate is about 10 days.
