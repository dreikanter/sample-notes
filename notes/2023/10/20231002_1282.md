# Braised chicken thighs with olives and preserved lemon

Autumn cooking. This is a reliable weeknight dish that tastes better than the effort justifies. Loosely Moroccan-inflected.

**Ingredients (serves 3):**

- 6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- 1 large onion, thinly sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, sliced thin
- 1/4 preserved lemon (rind only, pulp discarded, finely chopped)
- 100g green olives, pitted and roughly torn
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- Pinch of cinnamon
- 200ml chicken stock
- Handful of fresh cilantro and flat-leaf parsley

**Method:**

1. Season chicken thighs with salt and the spices. Brown well in a wide pan, skin side down, until deeply golden — 10–12 minutes. Don't rush this; the fond is your flavor. Transfer to a plate.

2. Pour off most of the fat, leaving about 1 tablespoon. Soften onion and garlic over medium heat, 8 minutes.

3. Add preserved lemon, olives. Stir 1 minute.

4. Nestle chicken back in, skin side up. Pour stock around (not over) the chicken to keep the skin.

5. Cover and braise on low heat 30 minutes, then uncover and cook another 15 minutes to reduce the liquid and re-crisp the skin somewhat.

6. Finish with fresh herbs.

Serve over couscous or with crusty bread. I made couscous with vegetable stock and a handful of golden raisins.

Reference for preserved lemons: https://www.davidlebovitz.com/preserved-lemons/
