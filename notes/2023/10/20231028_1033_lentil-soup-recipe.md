# Red lentil soup with cumin and lemon

A version that's become the default for cold evenings. Takes about 40 minutes start to finish. Serves four generously.

## Ingredients

- 300g red lentils, rinsed
- 1 large onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- 1 can (400g) crushed tomatoes
- 1.2L vegetable or chicken stock
- Juice of one lemon
- Olive oil, salt, pepper
- Optional: a small dried chilli, a handful of spinach stirred in at the end

## Method

Heat a generous pour of olive oil in a heavy pot over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds and let them sizzle for thirty seconds until fragrant — don't let them burn. Add the onion and cook until soft and lightly golden, about eight minutes. Add garlic and the remaining dry spices, stir for a minute.

Add the rinsed lentils, tomatoes, and stock. Bring to a simmer, partially cover, and cook for 20–25 minutes until the lentils have completely dissolved into the liquid.

Taste for salt. Add lemon juice — start with half, adjust from there. The lemon is essential; without it the soup tastes flat.

For a smoother texture, blend half the pot with an immersion blender and stir back in. I prefer it this way.

**Serve with:** flatbread or crusty bread, a drizzle of good olive oil, and a pinch of paprika on top.
