# Recipe — roasted tomato soup

Made this for Thanksgiving week when the grocery stores were chaos and I had a surplus of canned tomatoes and a half-empty jar of harissa. The roasting step is what separates this from the tinned-tomato-and-basil version that lives in every weeknight repertoire.

## Ingredients (serves 4)

- 2 × 28oz cans whole peeled tomatoes
- 1 large onion, quartered
- 8 garlic cloves, unpeeled
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp harissa paste
- 500ml vegetable or chicken stock
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Salt, black pepper
- Heavy cream or crème fraîche to finish (optional but worth it)

## Method

Preheat oven to 220°C. Drain the tomatoes and spread on a large roasting tray with the onion and garlic. Drizzle with oil, season generously, roast 35–40 minutes until edges are dark and slightly caramelized.

Squeeze garlic from skins. Transfer everything to a pot with the harissa, paprika, and stock. Simmer 15 minutes. Blend until smooth. Adjust seasoning.

Finish with a swirl of cream and cracked black pepper. Serve with good bread.

## Notes

The harissa level can go higher — I used closer to two tablespoons the second time. The smoke from the paprika plus the harissa's heat creates a depth you don't get from plain-roasted tomatoes alone.

Keeping the oven hot enough to get actual char on the tomato edges is essential. Don't rush it.

Reference: [Smitten Kitchen's tomato soup notes](https://smittenkitchen.com/) informed the roasting approach, though my version is spicier.
