# Apartment plants care log — November

## Current inventory

- Monstera deliciosa (large, 4 years old) — southeast window
- Pothos (golden, trailing) — bookshelf, medium light
- Snake plant — bathroom, north-facing
- Fiddle-leaf fig — has been struggling since September
- Two propagations from the pothos (rooting in water)

## This month's notes

The monstera pushed out two new leaves in November, which is unusual for this time of year. I moved it about eighteen inches closer to the window in October and the growth increase is striking. The new leaf unfurling takes about four days from the sheath splitting to full extension.

The fiddle-leaf continues to be a problem. Three leaves yellowed and dropped in early November. I suspect overwatering after reading up on the symptoms — the yellowing pattern (uniform, starting at the base of the leaf) matches root rot more than nutrient deficiency. I've reduced watering to every twelve days and removed it from the saucer it was sitting in. Repotting with fresh well-draining soil is on the list for early December.

The snake plant in the bathroom does nothing visible but also never declines. The most honest plant I own.

Pothos propagations have visible roots after three weeks in a clear vase. Will pot in mid-December.

## Reference

[The Sill's plant care guides](https://www.thesill.com/pages/plant-care) are reliable for the basics. For the fiddle-leaf specifically, the [Fiddle Leaf Fig Resource Center](https://www.fiddleleaffigresource.com/) is obsessively detailed and has been the most useful thing I've read on the subject.
