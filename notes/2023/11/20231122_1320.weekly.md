# Week of 2023-11-20 review

## What happened

Shipped the API rate-limiter refactor on Tuesday — turned out simpler than estimated once I stopped trying to preserve backward compatibility with the old token bucket config. Cut it entirely and the tests still passed. Good reminder that I tend to over-engineer migration paths.

Finished reading *Babel* by R.F. Kuang. Took me longer than expected because the footnotes are dense and genuinely interesting, not decorative. Magic-as-translation is a premise I want to think about more.

The sourdough finally worked (see [[20231121_1317]]). Made a second loaf immediately and gave it to the neighbors. Got unsolicited positive feedback, which I will choose to believe.

## What didn't work
