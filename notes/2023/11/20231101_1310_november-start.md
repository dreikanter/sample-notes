# November — start of the long interior season

The clocks changed last weekend and now it's dark by 5pm. Every year this is a small shock. Every year I adapt within a week and find something I value about it — the forced interiority, the way evening becomes longer and more deliberate.

The garden is as put-to-bed as it gets. Garlic is in the ground [[20231014_1293]], the beds are mulched, the tender plants are in. The spinach and arugula are still producing under a fleece cloche I put over them last week. I'll harvest until they stop.

**Indoor season plans:**

More cooking. The recipes I want to try over the next two months are the long ones — braised short ribs, a proper cassoulet, the Korean braised chicken (dak jjim) I've been meaning to make for a year.

More reading. I've been thinking about what I want to read before the year ends. The backlog [[20231029_1307]] has too many candidates to do all of them, so: finish Proust volume 2, read "Pedro Páramo" (short), and at least start one of the large projects ("Middlemarch" or "The Recognitions").

**Work:**

The analytics service migration to EKS is scheduled for next week. The pilot has been clean [[20231016_1295]]. Cautiously optimistic.

There's something clarifying about November. The year is long enough to see what you actually did versus what you planned. The gap is always instructive.
