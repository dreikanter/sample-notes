# Japan travel planning — research backlog

Not a firm trip yet. Putting everything in one place so when the timing works, the research is done.

## Possible timing

Cherry blossom (late March to mid-April) is the classic. Too crowded, very expensive. Late October / early November for fall foliage is less crowded, still beautiful. November has the advantage of post-holiday pricing.

## Routes under consideration

**Tokyo and Kyoto:** The standard. Works, well-understood. Not ruled out just because it's common — it's common because it covers a lot of ground well.

**Tohoku focus:** Less-traveled northern Honshu. The Sendai coast and the mountain regions. Not as much English infrastructure. More interesting maybe.

**Kyushu:** The southwestern island, warmer, good food culture (ramen from Hakata, the hot spring culture of Beppu). Different from the Honshu tourist circuit.

## Things I want to do

- Stay at a ryokan at least two nights — the onsen, the kaiseki dinner, the full experience
- Take the Shinkansen just to understand the engineering
- Find the neighborhood izakayas rather than destination restaurants
- See a proper noh or kabuki performance even without understanding the language

## Practical research needed

- JR Pass pricing — changes frequently, may or may not be worth it depending on route
- Best months for each route
- IC card vs cash for daily expenses
