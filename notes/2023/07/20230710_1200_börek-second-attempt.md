# Börek — second attempt

Revisiting from [[20230607_1179]]. Added dill and lemon, tried baking one tray alongside frying another.

**Changes**

- Added 1 large bunch fresh dill, roughly chopped
- Added zest of 1 lemon
- Added 1 tbsp fine breadcrumbs to filling (to absorb moisture)
- Squeezed spinach more aggressively (pressed in a tea towel)

**Results — fried tray**

Significantly better than first attempt. The dill is exactly what was missing — it makes the filling taste alive rather than just competent. The lemon zest adds brightness without making it obviously citrusy. The breadcrumbs have eliminated the moisture problem.

**Results — baked tray (200 °C, 20 min, brushed with butter)**

Crispy but differently crispy. Less shatteringly crunchy than fried, more evenly coloured. A good option for making ahead — can be reheated briefly. The fried version has an edge on texture but the baked version is more practical for a crowd.

**Filling verdict**

The feta/spinach/dill combination is now settled. Next variation to try: chicken and walnut (a Turkish classic apparently), or a sweet version with cheese and walnut for breakfast.

**Serving**

Good warm or at room temperature. Ate half the batch over two days. Kept well in the fridge, reheated in a dry pan.
