# Fermented hot sauce — batch three

Third batch. See [[20230423_1131]] and [[20230505_1147]] for batches one and two.

**Changes for batch three**

- Carrot increased to 75 g (from 50 g)
- Added 2 cm fresh ginger
- Reduced bird's eye chillies to 30 g (batch two was uncomfortably hot for some people)
- Added 50 g roasted red pepper for body and natural sweetness

**Ingredient list**

- 250 g cayenne
- 100 g jalapeño
- 30 g bird's eye
- 50 g roasted red pepper (charred, peeled)
- 75 g carrot
- 2 cm ginger, sliced
- 4 garlic cloves
- 2% brine (10 g non-iodised salt in 500 mL water)

**Process notes**

Day 3: Strong activity. The ginger is noticeably present in the aroma — not a given that this is good, will see how it integrates.

Day 7: Pressed and tasted. The carrot and roasted pepper have completely changed the base flavour — rounder, slightly sweet, more complex. Heat is significantly more approachable than batch two.

Day 10: Blended, strained, bottled. pH approximately 3.4.

**Result**

Best batch so far. The ginger is integrated rather than dominant. The roasted pepper adds depth without sweetness. Heat level is medium — accessible but not timid. This will be the standard recipe going forward.

Will give bottles to three people and solicit feedback.
