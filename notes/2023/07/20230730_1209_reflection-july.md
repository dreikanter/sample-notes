# Reflection — end of July

July was a shipping month. The search redesign, the Vite migration, the second blog post. All three were things I'd been working toward for a while, and they all happened in the same four weeks, which gives the month a satisfying completeness.

**What went well**

The two engineering projects. The search redesign in particular — the architecture held, the testing was fast, the ship was clean. The Vite migration was smaller but the day-to-day improvement to the development experience is real.

The coastal trip to Dungeness (long overdue). The visit to the park. I got outside more in July than any month since the Berlin trip.

**The tomatoes**

Three plants producing more than I can eat. This is the fullest expression of what the balcony experiment was supposed to be. Eating a tomato you grew is not dramatically different from eating a good supermarket tomato. It's specifically and concretely better.

**What didn't**

The second blog post underperformed on distribution. I've been thinking about why — I think the first post had a counter-intuitive claim ("your problem isn't what you think it is") that made it shareable. The second post has a more structurally complex argument that requires more investment. Worth factoring into the next one.

**Intention for August**

Slower month at work. Use the space to think about what's next rather than shipping. Read something long and demanding. Dani's visit is next weekend.
