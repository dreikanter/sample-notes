# Notes on Borges

Working through *Ficciones* in translation. A few recurring shapes
across the stories:

- **Infinite or self-containing structures.** Libraries, gardens,
  encyclopedias — systems large enough to be indistinguishable from
  the world they describe.
- **Forgery and authorship.** Who wrote the text, and does it matter
  if the text is identical? "Pierre Menard" is the obvious example
  but the idea recurs.
- **Dreams that imply a dreamer outside the frame.** "The Circular
  Ruins" in particular.

Translation matters. The Andrew Hurley and the older Anthony Kerrigan
renderings produce noticeably different voices.
