# Journal — the heat

Warmest week of the year so far. 31 °C yesterday, which in this country means a kind of low-level national crisis: trains run slower, offices have no air conditioning, everyone looks slightly surprised even though this happens every summer.

The balcony is difficult in this heat. The tomatoes need twice-daily watering or the leaves begin to curl. The basil is actually fine — it's heat-tolerant and seems to be enjoying this. The French beans are flagging slightly in the afternoon; they recover by evening.

Eating has shifted: less cooking, more assembly. Sliced tomatoes with good olive oil, fleur de sel, and fresh basil from the balcony. Cold soba with sesame dressing and cucumber. A bowl of the now-mature kimchi ([[20230529_1170]]) alongside rice and a fried egg. These things take twenty minutes rather than an hour and leave the kitchen cool.

Reading is easier in this heat than working. Finished the Sebald — *The Rings of Saturn* — and felt the usual post-Sebald flatness, as if the world has temporarily lost resolution. Picked up a Penelope Fitzgerald novel (*The Gate of Angels*) to cleanse the palate. Light and funny in a way that Fitzgerald manages to make feel serious.

Slept badly two nights in a row. The flat holds heat. The fan moves the heat around without reducing it. I know this has seasonal solutions but I resist buying an air conditioner on principle and spend the summer regretting it.
