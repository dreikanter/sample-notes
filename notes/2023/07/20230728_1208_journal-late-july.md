# Journal — late July

One of those weeks where nothing exceptional happened and everything was fine.

The second blog post went live Wednesday. Less response than the first, which I'd mentally prepared for — second pieces in a series rarely hit as well as the first unless the argument escalates in an interesting way. I think this one is more nuanced than the first and consequently less shareable. That's probably the right trade.

The Vite migration is done and paying dividends. The dev server speed improvement is the kind of change that seems small until you've had it for a week and then you notice how much more you're willing to experiment when the feedback loop is fast. Five seconds of iteration versus one second of iteration across hundreds of iterations a day adds up.

Tomatoes are coming in quantities now. I'm eating cherry tomatoes every day and still giving them away. The plants show no signs of slowing. Blight watch: nothing visible as of today, though August is the higher-risk month.

Read a short story collection in the evenings this week — Alice Munro, *Dear Life*. She's so good at the elliptical structure — the story that appears to be about one thing and is actually about something that only becomes visible at the end, when you look back at what you read earlier and realise what it was saying all along.

Dani is coming next weekend. First proper visit in about eight months.
