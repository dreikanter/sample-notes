# Cardamom rolls baking log

Made these for the first time this year. The Swedish name is *kardemummabullar* and they're distinct from cinnamon rolls: less sugar, more spice, often twisted rather than rolled into spirals.

## Recipe (makes 12)

**Dough:**
- 500g strong bread flour
- 300ml whole milk, warm
- 80g butter, softened
- 70g sugar
- 7g dried yeast
- 1 tsp ground cardamom
- ½ tsp salt

**Filling:**
- 60g butter, softened
- 40g sugar
- 2 tsp freshly ground cardamom (this is the important part — pre-ground is half the flavor)
- ½ tsp cinnamon

**Topping:** Pearl sugar (pressed on just before baking), egg wash.

## Method

Standard enriched dough process: warm milk activates yeast, add butter and sugar, incorporate flour and spices, knead until smooth and elastic. First proof 1–1.5h. Roll to a rectangle, spread filling, fold in thirds, cut into strips, twist each strip and coil. Second proof 45 min. Egg wash, pearl sugar, bake 190°C for 14–16 minutes.

## Notes

The freshly ground cardamom made a significant difference. Bought green pods, shelled them, ground with a mortar. The aroma before baking was noticeably stronger than any pre-ground version.

The twist shaping takes practice — first three looked awkward, by the sixth I had it.

Reference: [FIKA: The Art of the Swedish Coffee Break](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/246432/fika-by-anna-brones-and-johanna-kindvall/) has beautiful illustrations of the shaping process.
