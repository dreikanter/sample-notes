# Books read in 2023 — full year log

A more complete list than what I'd find in individual notes.

## Fiction

- *Babel* — R.F. Kuang (see [[20231124_1323]]) — 9/10
- *Piranesi* — Susanna Clarke (see [[20231220_1343]]) — 9/10
- *The Night Circus* — Erin Morgenstern (January, revisit from three years ago) — 7/10 on reread, some of the magic has worn off
- *Demon Copperhead* — Barbara Kingsolver (February, Pulitzer winner) — 8/10, Dickens transposed to the opioid crisis, effective

## Nonfiction

- *Thinking in Systems* — Donella Meadows (see [[20231208_1333]]) — 9/10
- *Four Thousand Weeks* — Oliver Burkeman (March, still thinking about it in December) — 9/10
- *The Precipice* — Toby Ord (May, existential risk, dense, useful) — 8/10
- *Range* — David Epstein (July, good central argument, padded execution) — 7/10
- *The Dawn of Everything* — Graeber & Wengrow (started December, finished January 2024) — 8/10

## Started and abandoned

- *Jonathan Strange & Mr Norrell* — Susanna Clarke (also see [[20231220_1343]] for why) — 120 pages, set aside
- *The Making of the Atomic Bomb* — Richard Rhodes — 80 pages, needs a different frame of mind than I had in August

## Notable misses

*Underland* by Robert Macfarlane is still on the stack. Has been for six months. Next year.

Total finished: approximately 14. Below average for me but the year had heavier work demands.
