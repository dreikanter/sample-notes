# Recipe — spiced red lentil dal

Made this weekly through December. The easiest substantial meal I know.

## Ingredients (serves 4)

- 300g red lentils, rinsed
- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 thumb ginger, minced
- 2 tins chopped tomatoes (800g total)
- 600ml water or light vegetable stock
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp garam masala (added at the end)
- ½–1 tsp chili powder (to taste)
- Salt
- Neutral oil or ghee

**Tadka (tempering oil):**
- 2 tbsp ghee or oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 dried red chili
- 4–5 curry leaves (if you can get them)

## Method

Soften onion in oil until golden — this takes longer than you think, maybe fifteen minutes. Add garlic and ginger, cook two minutes. Add dry spices (not garam masala), stir thirty seconds. Add tomatoes, cook five minutes. Add rinsed lentils and liquid, bring to boil, simmer uncovered 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally.

When lentils are completely dissolved, add garam masala. Adjust seasoning.

Make the tadka separately: heat ghee until shimmering, add cumin seeds (they should sputter immediately), dried chili, and curry leaves. Pour over the finished dal.

## Notes

The tadka is not optional — it's half the flavor. The bloomed whole spices on top change the texture and aroma of the whole dish.

Excellent with basmati rice and a dollop of yogurt and something pickled.

Reference: [Madhur Jaffrey's dal techniques](https://www.madhurjaffrey.com/) remain definitive.
