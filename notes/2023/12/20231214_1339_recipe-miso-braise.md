# Recipe — miso-braised short ribs

Made for the solstice dinner. Four hours, worth every minute.

## Ingredients (serves 4–6)

- 2kg bone-in short ribs, cut into individual bones
- 3 tbsp white miso (shiro miso, not the darker variety)
- 2 tbsp rice wine vinegar
- 2 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, sliced
- 500ml beef stock
- Neutral oil for searing
- Scallions and toasted sesame to finish

## Method

Mix miso, vinegar, mirin, soy, and honey into a paste. Rub over ribs and marinate at least two hours, up to overnight.

Scrape off excess marinade (it burns). Sear ribs in batches in a heavy Dutch oven until deeply browned on all sides — takes longer than you expect, maybe twelve minutes per batch.

Add garlic, ginger, stock, and any marinade that didn't go on the ribs. Bring to simmer, cover, and cook in a 150°C oven for 3.5–4 hours until the meat pulls away from the bone.

Rest 20 minutes before serving. Skim fat from braising liquid, reduce by half over high heat, pour over ribs.

## Notes

Serving over plain short-grain rice is essential — the braising liquid needs something to absorb into. Pickled cucumber on the side cuts the richness well.

The white miso is important. Red or brown miso overwhelms rather than deepens.

Reference: [Momofuku's cookbook](https://momofuku.com/) was the influence here, though the recipe has diverged significantly over three iterations.
