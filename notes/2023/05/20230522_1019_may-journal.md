# May journal — third week

The weather has been warmer than usual for the third week in a row. I've started working in the morning before the flat heats up, which means getting things done between 6 and 10 and then the afternoon is a wash. Not ideal but it works.

Finished the Pevear and Volokhonsky translation of *The Brothers Karamazov* this week — took just under two months at my pace. The Grand Inquisitor chapter lands differently on a second reading; less about the argument itself and more about how Ivan presents it and what that performance reveals about his state. I'll probably come back to it in the notes I'm keeping on Dostoevsky. [[20230510_1018]] holds some formatting references I wanted to keep nearby while typing those up.

The sourdough has been unreliable lately. I think the warmer ambient temperature is causing the levain to peak faster than I expect, so by the time I'm shaping it's already tired. Need to either shift the timing or start using colder water. [[20230428_1017]] reminds me to get back to cooking something that isn't bread.

A few things I've been thinking about:

- The gap between "understanding something" and "being able to explain it clearly" is larger than I usually account for. Teaching forces you to find the gaps.
- I've been on the same bus route for three years and only recently noticed there's a small garden behind the station wall. Attention is selective and habituated.
- Keeping a daily word count has made me more consistent at writing, but it's also made me tolerate mediocre output because I need to hit the number.

More later when I have something coherent to say.
