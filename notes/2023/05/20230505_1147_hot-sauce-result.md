# Fermented hot sauce — batch two result

Fermented for 10 days (two days longer than planned — got busy). Result is significantly better than batch one.

**Taste notes**

Initial hit is a deep, rounded heat rather than the raw capsaicin sharpness you get with fresh chillies. The fermentation has produced complexity — sour, umami, and fruit notes underneath the heat. The carrot (50 g added as experiment) has softened the edge as hoped without making it sweet or obvious.

Colour is a deep brick red. Consistency after blending and straining is good — pourable but coats the back of a spoon.

**Heat level**

Significantly hotter than batch one. The 50 g bird's eyes made a real difference. Probably sits around a medium-hot sriracha level, maybe slightly above. Good for cooking but aggressive as a straight table sauce for anyone without a high heat tolerance.

**Modifications for batch three**

- Keep the carrot, increase to 75 g
- Try adding 1 cm ginger during fermentation
- Consider reducing bird's eyes to 30 g if I want it more broadly usable
- Try wrapping the jar in a dark cloth — may improve the environment

**Shelf life**

Bottled in sterilised glass, stored in fridge. The vinegar addition lowers pH further (measured at 3.6 with strips — similar to commercial sriracha). Should keep 6+ months.
