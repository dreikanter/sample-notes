# Kimchi — first attempt

First attempt at kimchi. Using Maangchi's traditional recipe as a base, adjusted for available ingredients.

**Ingredients (for one medium napa cabbage, ~1.2 kg)**

- 1 napa cabbage
- 100 g coarse sea salt (for salting)
- 1 tbsp fish sauce (or soy sauce for vegan)
- 1 tbsp gochugaru paste (or 80 g gochugaru flakes)
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 6 spring onions, cut into 5 cm lengths
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 1 small carrot, julienned

**Process**

Cut cabbage into quarters lengthwise, then into 5 cm pieces. Toss with salt, let sit 2 hours, turning occasionally. Rinse three times in cold water, squeeze out excess. Taste — it should be pleasantly salty, not aggressively so.

Mix remaining ingredients into a paste. Toss cabbage and spring onion with paste, coating thoroughly. Pack tightly into sterilised jars, pressing down to eliminate air pockets. Leave 3 cm headspace (it will expand).

Ferment at room temp for 24–48 hours, then refrigerate. Will be sour and bubbly at 48 hours; flavour develops over weeks.

**Notes**

Day 2: actively bubbling, good sour smell, not unpleasant. Pressed down to keep submerged.

Day 4: transferred to fridge. Sour and crunchy. Not as funky as shop-bought yet — that comes with time.
