# Dialling in V60 for the Yirgacheffe

The Ethiopia Yirgacheffe from the April cupping ([[20230415_1116]]) arrived whole bean (ordered a 250 g bag). Spent this morning dialling it in for V60.

**Target:** 15 g coffee, 250 mL water, brew time 2:30–3:00 min, temp 93 °C.

**Grind size experiments (Timemore C2)**

- Click 18: too fine. Sour, underdeveloped, brew time 3:45. Over-extracted somehow? Or the sour is actually a sign of this being a very acidic coffee.
- Click 22: better. Balanced, clean, slight blueberry finish. Brew time 2:50.
- Click 24: thin. Too coarse, watery, all top notes and no body. Brew time 2:15.

**Best result**

Click 22, 15 g / 250 mL, bloom 30 mL / 30 s, then three pours of ~70 mL at 30 s intervals. Total brew 2:55. The blueberry from the cupping is here but lighter — the V60 doesn't concentrate flavour the way cupping does. Very clean, floral high notes, mild sweetness, no bitterness.

**Notes**

This coffee tastes different at different temperatures. At ~85 °C it's more citrus; as it drops toward 70 °C the fruit becomes more dried-fig. Worth letting it cool for two or three minutes before drinking.

Will try a lower temp (90 °C) tomorrow to see if that suppresses the acidity without losing the top notes.
