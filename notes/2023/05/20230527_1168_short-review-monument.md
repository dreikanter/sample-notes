# Short review — Monument Valley (village, not the game)

Visited the Navajo Nation park last summer (writing this up late). Notes for anyone asking.

The Mittens and Merrick Butte are the most photographed subjects, but the photographs don't resolve the question of scale. Standing in the valley, the buttes are at a distance that makes them seem almost like props — too composed. It takes time to recalibrate. By mid-morning we were starting to get it.

The Navajo-led jeep tour is genuinely worth taking. Our guide was a woman in her forties whose family has lived in the valley for generations. She pointed out features that aren't on any of the standard maps — petroglyphs at an unreachable cliff face, a route that crosses a particular dune at a specific angle to make the colours shift. The park's geology section on their official site mentions [the mesa formation process](https://navajonationparks.org/tribal-parks/monument-valley/) in outline, but the lived knowledge she carried about this particular ground was a different kind of information.

The light at dusk made everything that came before look like a rehearsal. The red in the sandstone intensified past what seems photogenic into something else entirely.

Go early to beat tour buses if you're doing the loop drive unguided. Bring enough water. The road is rough in the southern section.
