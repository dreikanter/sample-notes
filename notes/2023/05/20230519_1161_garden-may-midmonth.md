# Garden update — mid May

**Radishes (second sowing)**
Germinated day 3 in the terracotta pot. The window box sowing is 48 hours behind — slightly less warmth on that side. Still good germination, maybe 80%. Both batches thinned to 5 cm spacing.

**Basil**
Two weeks under the borrowed grow light: transformed. The seedlings are short, stocky, well-branched, and have a strong aroma when touched. Moving outside next weekend (last frost date here is typically mid-May, and the forecast looks clear).

**Garlic**
Scapes forming on four of the six plants — curling green spirals about 10 cm long. Cut them all today. Sautéed with olive oil and pasta — mild, sweet garlic flavour. Will leave the garlic in the ground until the leaves begin to yellow, probably late June.

**French beans**
Transplanted the module-grown beans into two large containers (5 plants per container). Using bamboo canes in a wigwam formation — the beans will climb. Watered in well.

**Borage**
Seeds arrived from Chiltern Seeds ([[20230429_1140]]). Sowed six direct into a pot. The flowers will attract pollinators and I want to try the flowers in ice cubes and as a garnish. Germination expected in 7–14 days.

**General notes**
Temperatures settled at 14–20 °C during the day. Not yet needing to water daily. Aphid pressure low so far — the marigolds I planted nearby may be helping.
