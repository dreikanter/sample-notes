# Short restaurant review — Oklava, Shoreditch

Dinner with four people, mid-week booking (easier than weekends). Turkish-Cypriot cooking in a small, warm room.

**What we had**

Octopus with fennel seed and pickled cucumber — the best thing we ordered. The octopus was yielding without being mushy, and the fennel/cucumber combination cut the richness cleanly.

Lamb börek: good pastry, slightly underseasoned filling. The outside was the correct amount of shatteringly crisp.

Sigara börek with spinach and feta: better than expected from what could be dull. They'd put something in the filling that created a faint anise note. Couldn't identify it.

Whole sea bass with chermoula: a slight miss. The fish was well-cooked but the chermoula was timid — needed more punch.

Sütlaç (rice pudding): very good. Silky, slightly browned, rosewater restrained rather than overwhelming.

**Verdict**

Worth returning to. The cooking has a particular flavour sensibility — bright acidity, herbs, nothing too heavy — that works well as a group-eating format. The wine list has two or three interesting Georgian and Greek bottles.

Booking notes: [Oklava website](https://www.oklava.co.uk). Walk-ins possible at the bar on quieter nights.

Related: the sigara börek made me want to try making börek at home. Adding to the backlog.
