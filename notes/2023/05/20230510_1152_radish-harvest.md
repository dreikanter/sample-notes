# Radish harvest — first pull

Pulled six Cherry Belle radishes from the balcony pot today. Sown 16 April, so 24 days from sow to harvest. That's about right for this variety.

**Results**

Roots: 2–3 cm diameter, round, smooth skin, good red colour. Three were perfect. Two had split, which I think is from uneven watering — they got very wet last Thursday and then I missed a day. One was pithy, probably from going a day or two too long.

Taste: clean radish bite, not too hot, mild sweetness. The contrast with shop-bought is noticeable — these have texture and the flavour is alive rather than flat.

**Lessons**

- Harvest promptly. Radishes degrade fast in the ground — even a few extra days changes the texture.
- Even moisture is critical. I'm going to set a daily reminder for the morning water check.
- The pot holds about 20 plants at 5 cm spacing. For continuous supply, sow a new batch every 14–16 days.

**Second sowing**

Sowed a new batch in the same pot after pulling (added a handful of fresh compost). Also sowed a row in the window box on the south-facing rail — slightly shadier position, curious if that slows the rate or affects the flavour.
