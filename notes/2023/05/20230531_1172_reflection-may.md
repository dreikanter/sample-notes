# Reflection — end of May

Better month than April in most respects. The blog post exists. The httpx migration got done. The phone calls got made.

**Garden**

The balcony is looking like a real growing space for the first time. Basil outside and thriving. Garlic scapes were a treat. The French beans are climbing the wigwam canes. The borage finally germinated — three of six seeds, which is fine.

**Reading**

Calvino done. Half of Graeber done. Started re-reading some old Sebald (not *Austerlitz* — *The Rings of Saturn*, which I find more accessible on return visits). Sebald and Calvino have more in common than they appear to: both writing texts that resemble novels but behave more like extended meditations on memory.

**Work**

The search redesign is moving. The sprint is stable. I'm slightly worried that I'm doing more specification work and less building, but this is probably a phase.

**The habit that's working**

Morning phone-free time. It's been consistent enough now that it feels like a real habit rather than an ongoing experiment. The quality of the first hour of the day has genuinely improved.

**What I want more of in June**

Being outside. The weather has been good and I've not been taking advantage of it. Also: cooking more adventurously — the kimchi ([[20230529_1170]]) was a good example of what's possible when I actually try something unfamiliar.
