# Wine notes — natural wine tasting

Small tasting at Petra's flat — six of us, seven bottles, two hours. Rough notes.

**Radikon Ribolla Gialla 2018 (Friuli)**
Orange wine, amber in colour. Tannins from the skin contact give it a texture more like red wine. Dried apricot, walnut, oxidative note that divides the room — half of us loved it. Long finish. Not for people who need their white wine to taste like white wine.

**Gut Oggau Mechthild 2020 (Austria)**
Grüner Veltliner biodynamic. More familiar territory — green herbs, white pepper, grapefruit. Clean and precise. Converts fence-sitters because it shows that natural doesn't mean funky.

**Julien Labet Savagnin Ouillé 2019 (Jura)**
The ouillé (topped up, no oxidation) version. Still has that Jura character — walnut, yellow curry, honey — but lighter than a vin jaune. Very interesting with the cheeses we had at the end.

**Frank Cornelissen Munjebel Bianco 2021 (Sicily)**
The controversial one. Skin-contact Carricante from Etna. Volcanic minerality under the orange wine profile. Very expressive, slightly cloudy. One person called it "cheese water" and didn't mean it as a compliment.

**Verdict**

Best pairing of the evening: the Savagnin with the comté. Natural wine has a wider texture range than conventional wine and that creates more pairing possibilities.
