# Sourdough log

Second loaf this week. Still adjusting the hydration.

- Flour: 80% bread, 20% whole wheat
- Hydration: 72%
- Bulk: 4h at 24°C, three sets of folds
- Shape, cold retard overnight
- Bake: Dutch oven, 245°C lid on for 22 min, 215°C lid off for 18 min

Crumb was more open than last time but the bottom crust went too dark.
Try dropping the oven to 230°C for the lid-off phase next round.

Reference: <https://www.theperfectloaf.com/>
