# Journal — Sunday walk, May

Long walk today — nearly three hours, out to the reservoir and back by the longer route through the woods.

The reservoir path was busier than expected for a Sunday morning, then quiet once past the second gate. Met two dogs with no apparent owner (they eventually found him, far back along the path, on the phone). The water level is visibly lower than last summer — a pale mineral band around the edge marks the high waterline.

The woods section was the best part. The canopy is dense enough now that the light comes through in columns. There were three different kinds of birdsong that I couldn't identify — would like to get better at this. The Merlin app helps but I resist using the phone when I'm trying to actually be somewhere.

Thinking about the Graeber book ([[20230518_1160]]) while walking. Specifically the section on the relationship between debt and violence — how creditor/debtor relations always have a threat structure underneath them even when they're entirely cordial on the surface. This seems to generalise to a lot of relationships that ostensibly aren't about money. The grammar of obligation is everywhere.

Arrived back with two hours before the afternoon expired. Made tea, read for an hour, did nothing productive. This is harder than it sounds.

**Route**

Out via the canal towpath (40 min), reservoir loop (60 min), woods section (40 min), back via the main road (30 min). About 12 km total.
