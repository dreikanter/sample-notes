# Japanese curry — from scratch

Not katsu specifically — just the curry sauce, which works with pork cutlets, vegetables, or over udon. Based loosely on [J. Kenji López-Alt's methodology](https://www.seriouseats.com/the-best-japanese-curry-recipe).

**Serves 4**

**Curry roux**

- 4 tbsp butter
- 4 tbsp flour
- 2 tbsp curry powder (S&B if possible)
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp cayenne

Melt butter over low heat. Add flour, cook stirring for 2 minutes until nutty. Add spices, cook 1 minute. Remove from heat, cool to room temperature. (Can be made ahead, keeps 2 weeks refrigerated.)

**Curry sauce**

- 1 large onion, thinly sliced
- 2 carrots, 2 cm chunks
- 2 medium waxy potatoes, 2 cm chunks
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 700 mL chicken or vegetable stock
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp honey
- The roux above

Cook onion in oil over medium heat for 25–30 minutes, stirring, until deep golden brown. This is not optional — the sweetness and depth come from here. Add carrots, potatoes, stock. Simmer 15 minutes. Add roux in pieces, whisking as you go. Add soy, Worcestershire, honey. Simmer 10 minutes.

Sauce should be thick and glossy. Serve over rice.
