# Miso soup variations

Basic recipe is known, documenting the variations that actually work.

**Base (for two)**

500 mL dashi (kombu + katsuobushi, or just kombu for vegan), brought to 70 °C. Do not boil. Whisk in 2–3 tbsp white or mixed miso. Add toppings and serve immediately.

**Variations that work**

*Tofu + wakame (classic)*: Silken tofu cut into 1.5 cm cubes, rehydrated wakame. Add the tofu gently so it doesn't break. Finish with spring onion.

*Mushroom (shiitake + enoki)*: Rehydrate dried shiitake in 500 mL water — this becomes the dashi. Slice and use both. Enoki added at the last minute. The shiitake liquid is very intense; you need less miso.

*Clam (shijimi miso soup)*: This requires shijimi (corbicula clams) or littlenecks if unavailable. Steam clams open in plain water, use the liquid as the dashi. Classic hangover food in Japan — reportedly due to the ornithine content. Red miso works better here than white.

*Potato + onion (hearty winter version)*: Small chunks of waxy potato par-boiled first, then simmered in the dashi until tender. Adds a starchier body. Good in cold weather.

**Things not to do**

- Never boil after adding miso — kills the probiotics and flattens the flavour
- Don't use the same miso for everything; different dishes call for different intensities
- Don't add miso directly to boiling stock — ladle some liquid into the miso and whisk before adding to the pot
