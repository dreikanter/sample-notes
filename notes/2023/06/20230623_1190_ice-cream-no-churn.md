# No-churn ice cream — technique notes

Made four batches this summer. The no-churn method (whipped cream + sweetened condensed milk) is not as smooth as churned, but it's significantly easier and produces a serviceable result.

**Basic ratio**

- 300 mL double cream, whipped to stiff peaks
- 200 mL sweetened condensed milk
- Pinch of salt

Fold condensed milk into cream gently, add flavourings, freeze in a lidded container for minimum 6 hours.

**Batches made**

*Vanilla (batch 1):* 1 tsp vanilla extract + 1 tbsp vodka (alcohol prevents complete freezing, keeps texture scoopable). Good baseline. Slightly icy compared to commercial, but acceptable.

*Strawberry (batch 2):* 200 g fresh strawberries roasted at 180 °C with 1 tbsp sugar until jammy, cooled, folded in. The roasting concentrates the flavour significantly. This was the best batch.

*Coffee cardamom (batch 3):* 3 tbsp instant espresso powder dissolved in 1 tbsp hot water, plus 1 tsp ground cardamom. Adult flavour, less sweet. Goes well with the dark chocolate I pour over at serving.

*Miso caramel (batch 4):* Caramel made from 100 g sugar + 2 tbsp white miso + 50 mL cream, cooled and folded in ribbons. Complex, umami, not very sweet. Divides people. I loved it.

**Texture notes**

The texture improves significantly after 12 hours vs 6 hours. Also improves if you take it out of the freezer 5 minutes before serving.
