# Garlic harvest — first crop

Harvested the garlic today, about six weeks after the scapes. The leaves were 60–70% yellowed — slightly past ideal but not over.

**Results**

Six bulbs total. Size is smaller than shop-bought — the largest is about 5 cm diameter, smallest 3 cm. Individual cloves are well-formed, no signs of rot or disease. Skin is papery and dry. Smell is intensely garlicky even before cutting — good sign.

The two "slow" bulbs (the ones I'd nearly given up on in April) produced the two largest bulbs. I have no explanation for this.

**Curing**

Laid the bulbs in a single layer on a wooden rack in a warm, dry, ventilated spot (south-facing room, window open). Will cure for 3–4 weeks before trimming the roots and storing. During curing the flavour intensifies and the moisture redistributes.

**Lessons for next year**

- Plant more. Six bulbs is not enough. Going for 12–15 next autumn.
- Try a hardneck variety alongside the softneck — hardnecks produce scapes (good to eat) and are said to have more complex flavour.
- Plant earlier — the RHS recommends October/November, I planted November and probably could have gone earlier.

**Container freed up**

The large container that held the garlic is now empty. Repotted one of the tomato plants into it — it's deeper and wider than the original 30 cm pot.
