# Edinburgh trip notes

Long weekend, flew Friday, back Monday. Conference for the first two days, then a day off.

**Conference (Fri/Sat)**

Two talks worth noting: one on event sourcing in distributed systems that directly applies to a decision we made in Q1 (will write an ADR based on it — see [[20230424_1134]]). One on accessibility testing pipelines that I want to share with the team.

The venue was a converted Victorian building near the Old Town. The acoustics in the main hall made all the presentations slightly reverberant, which was fine for slides and distracting during Q&A.

**Sunday — day off**

Arthur's Seat in the morning. I've done this walk three times now and each time the top is a different experience. Sunday was clear, cold for June, the city visible in three directions. The scramble section near the top is rougher than it looks from below.

Lunch at Hemma on Holyrood Road. Scandinavian-influenced menu. The smörgåsbord sharing board was good; the cardamom bun was excellent. Coffee was serious.

Afternoon: the National Museum of Scotland. The Scottish history galleries on the upper floors are well done — they don't avoid the difficult periods. The Lewis chessmen are there (a small selection — the main collection is in London and the Hebrides). Spent an hour in the science galleries with a friend who's a physicist and found them considerably more interesting as a result.
