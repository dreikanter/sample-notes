# Tomato growing — mid-season notes

Three Gardener's Delight cherry tomato plants. About four weeks in the containers now. Notes on what's working.

**Current state**

All three plants healthy. Height: 35–50 cm. Side shoots have been removed to keep as single cordons. First flower trusses visible on two of the three — small yellow flowers in clusters of 5–8.

**Feeding regime**

Switched to weekly tomato feed (Tomorite, diluted to label) once flowers appeared. Before flower formation: no feed. Tomatoes fed too early produce lots of foliage and small fruit.

**Watering**

Daily in hot weather, every other day when cooler. Inconsistent watering is the main cause of blossom end rot (calcium deficiency due to uneven water uptake) and fruit splitting. I set a morning reminder.

**Staking**

Two plants on bamboo canes, one on a tomato cage. The cage is easier to manage — the plant can expand without needing the cane extended as it grows. Will use cages for all next year.

**Pest check**

No aphid outbreak yet, which I attribute partly to the borage nearby. Some leaf curl on the hottest days — this is heat stress, not disease. Checked for blight signs: none. Touch wood.

**Expectation for harvest**

Gardener's Delight is typically 90 days from transplant. These were transplanted early June, so harvest from mid-August. Will probably stretch into September in a good year.
