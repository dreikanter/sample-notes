# Börek — first attempt

Inspired by the Oklava dinner ([[20230530_1171]]). Made sigara börek (cigar-shaped), spinach and feta filling.

**For 12 pieces**

*Filling*
- 200 g frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed very dry
- 150 g feta, crumbled
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 tbsp dried mint
- Black pepper

*Pastry*
- 6 sheets yufka (or filo, thawed)
- 80 g butter, melted, for brushing

**Method**

Mix filling ingredients. Taste for salt — the feta is salty; you may need none.

Cut each yufka sheet into quarters. Brush with butter. Place a heaped tablespoon of filling along one short edge, fold in the sides, roll tightly into a cigar about 10 cm long. Brush the outside. Repeat.

Fry in 1 cm neutral oil at 175 °C for 3–4 minutes per side until deep golden. Drain on paper.

**Results**

The outside: excellent. The frying worked — the yufka crisped and blistered the way it should.

The inside: slightly wet despite the squeezed spinach. Need to squeeze harder and possibly add a tablespoon of breadcrumbs to absorb moisture.

The filling flavour: too mild. The Oklava version had something more complex. I think it was fresh dill, possibly some lemon zest. Adding those next time.

**Next attempt**

Fresh dill, more lemon, and try baking rather than frying to see how different the texture is.
