# Reflection — end of June

June was better than expected. The conference, the engineering work picking up pace, the blog post response, the balcony at its fullest.

**What went well**

The search redesign engineering is in a good place. The architecture decision to split the monolith component into a hook plus display components was correct — the tests are clean and the pieces are individually comprehensible.

The garden has exceeded what I thought was possible on a fourth-floor balcony in the UK. Three tomato plants, French beans climbing, basil actually thriving, the garlic harvested and curing. Eating things I grew — even just the herbs — changes the relationship to cooking.

The reading this month was particularly good. Finished Graeber, deep into Sebald, started Newport. Three very different books but all in productive conversation with each other.

**What didn't**

I didn't start the second blog post (the organisational context switching point from the comment on the first one). This is a pattern I need to break: the follow-up always looks easier before you start and harder after. Going to draft it in the first week of July.

Social energy: fine, but I'm aware I'm in a comfortable routine that risks becoming a rut. Edinburgh shook that slightly; want to maintain some version of that effect.

**Intention for July**

Draft the second blog post. Get the search redesign to a shippable state. Eat the first tomato from the balcony.
