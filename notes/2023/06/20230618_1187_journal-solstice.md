# Journal — around the solstice

Summer solstice was Wednesday. The longest light of the year, which I find more melancholy than the shortest. After the solstice, the days begin to shorten — imperceptibly at first, but it starts then.

The balcony at 21:00 still has colour in the sky. The tomatoes are taller than the railing now. The borage has grown at a pace that surprised me — it's already 40 cm and showing its first buds, which are closed and slightly alien-looking, like small star-shaped pods that haven't decided yet. The French beans are at the top of the wigwam canes, which means I need to pinch the growing tips to encourage lateral growth.

This is the fullest the balcony has ever been. Sitting out in the evening with the plants is pleasurable in a way that I hadn't predicted when I started doing this. There is something calming about being surrounded by things that are doing what they're supposed to do and don't require anything from you except the occasional glass of water.

Ate outside every evening this week. June cooking is mostly improvised from whatever's in the fridge plus herbs from the balcony. Tonight: pasta with fresh basil and a raw sauce of blended tomatoes, olive oil, garlic, and the last of the hot sauce from batch two ([[20230505_1147]]). Light food for warm evenings.
