# Garden update — early June

**French beans**

Growing well. The wigwam structure is working — two of the five plants in the east container have found the canes and begun to climb. Gave first feed this morning: tomato fertiliser diluted to half strength. Will feed every two weeks.

**Garlic**

Leaves have begun to yellow at the tips, which is the start of the harvest window. Going to wait another two weeks — ideally the leaves should be about half-yellowed. Pulled one bulb as a test: under-size, small individual cloves. Too early.

**Borage**

Three seedlings up now, about 8 cm. The leaves are already visibly bristly. Moving to a larger pot this weekend.

**Radishes (third sowing)**

Sowed on 30 May. Germinated day 4. Thinned this morning to 5 cm.

**Tomatoes**

Found three small tomato plants (Gardener's Delight, cherry) at the market. Bought all three. Potted up into 30 cm containers with tomato compost and a handful of grit. Staked. The seller said they'd been hardened off for a week, which I'll assume is true.

**Basil**

Bushy and fragrant. Pinching tips every few days to prevent bolting. This is the best basil I've ever grown — the grow-light start made a significant difference.

**General**

The balcony is at capacity. Running out of containers. Will probably need to triage after the garlic is harvested to make space.
