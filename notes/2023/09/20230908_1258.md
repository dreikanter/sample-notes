# Journal — first week of September

The summer has crested and started its long descent. It's not dramatically different yet — still warm afternoons, still light until 8pm — but something in the quality of it has changed.

Spent Saturday morning walking the river trail before the heat built up. A great blue heron was working the shallows near the old bridge. It stood absolutely still for minutes, then struck with that particular economy of violence that large wading birds have. I watched it swallow a fish that seemed too big for the operation and then stand still again as if considering the decision.

The trail was busy with the September-resolution crowd — people who've decided they need to start running or cycling before summer closes. I was one of them, three years ago. I kept walking. It's pleasant not to have an agenda.

Work has been quieter than August. The retrospective actions are settling in — the design-engineering handoff improved immediately once we added a state inventory to the Figma handoff template. Small change, large effect.

I've been thinking about the Strange Loop talks [[20230907_1257]] and specifically about the observation that programming languages are responses to problems. I wonder if the same is true of my own coding habits — what problems led me to the patterns I default to, and whether those problems still apply.

No useful conclusion. Just something to sit with.

Reading in the evenings: Proust, volume two. Much slower going than volume one. The dinner party sections are extraordinary.
