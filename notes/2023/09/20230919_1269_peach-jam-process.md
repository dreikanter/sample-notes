# Peach jam — small batch from market peaches

The flat of peaches from Saturday's market [[20230916_1266]] needed using. Made two small batches — one classic and one with cardamom and black pepper.

## Classic batch

**Ingredients:**
- 600g ripe peaches, peeled and pitted (about 4–5 medium)
- 400g sugar (67% ratio — slightly lower than some recipes, produces a less set but more fresh-tasting jam)
- 2 tbsp lemon juice

**Method:**
1. Macerate peaches with sugar overnight in the fridge. This draws out the juices and starts the process gently.
2. Transfer to a wide heavy pot. Bring to a boil over medium-high, stirring to dissolve sugar.
3. Cook at a rolling boil, stirring frequently, skimming foam.
4. Test for set on a cold plate — wrinkle test at about 15 minutes.
5. Pour into sterilized jars and seal.

Yield: approximately 500ml.

## Spiced variation

Same process, add 4 cardamom pods (lightly crushed, seeds extracted and ground) and 1/2 tsp coarsely ground black pepper. Both go in with the peaches at step 2. The black pepper is subtle but adds complexity — not identifiable as pepper, just a warmth.

**Result:** Both jams set beautifully. The spiced version is more interesting and is now reserved as a gift. Good on toast with ricotta.

Reference for small-batch preserving techniques: https://www.ballmasonjars.com/blog.html

Water-bath processed both jars for shelf stability: 10 minutes in boiling water.
