# Kitchen equipment review — carbon steel pan

Bought a 26cm carbon steel pan from Matfer Bourgeat two months ago. Reporting on the experience after the seasoning is established.

The pan: https://www.matferbourgeatusa.com/carbon-steel-pans/

## Seasoning process

Followed the stovetop method rather than the oven method. Applied a thin layer of flaxseed oil, heated the pan until it smoked and stopped, let it cool. Repeated six times. The surface went from silver-grey to a patchy brown to, eventually, a more even dark surface.

Truthfully, the seasoning didn't fully click until around the fourth week of actual cooking. The manufacturer's guidance and every blog post I read undersold how long it takes for the seasoning to become properly nonstick. Patience required.

## After two months

The pan is now genuinely nonstick for eggs — I fried eggs this morning with a small amount of butter and they slid cleanly. This is the benchmark people use for carbon steel and cast iron, and it's an accurate one.

It has developed a noticeable dark patina, slightly variegated in color in a way that looks like use rather than neglect.

## Compared to cast iron

Lighter — the Matfer 26cm is about 1.7kg vs. my 28cm cast iron Lodge at 2.6kg. This matters when moving the pan to the oven or pouring out pan drippings.

Heats faster and more evenly (thinner walls, more conductive).

Gets ripping hot quickly — better for searing than the cast iron in my experience.

## What to avoid

Acidic foods (tomatoes, citrus) until the seasoning is very well established — they strip it. Dishwasher. Soaking.

## Verdict

Worth the learning curve. I'll probably buy a smaller one for eggs.
