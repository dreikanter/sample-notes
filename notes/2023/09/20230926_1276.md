# Spinach and arugula germination log

Planted September 2 in the bed cleared after the San Marzanos. Two varieties:
- Bloomsdale spinach
- Astro arugula

Both from saved seed from last spring's planting (first-generation saves, should be fine for open-pollinated varieties).

**Germination log:**

Day 4 (Sept 6): Nothing. Soil temperature ~18°C. Still within normal germination window.

Day 6 (Sept 8): First arugula seedlings — tiny white threads with cotyledons folded. Arugula is fast, this is normal.

Day 8 (Sept 10): Arugula is up in good density. First spinach cotyledons visible in one section.

Day 12 (Sept 14): Full germination for arugula. Spinach uneven — about 60% of the planted area has sprouted. The northeast corner of the bed may have gotten less water; slightly drier soil.

Day 18 (Sept 20): Thinned arugula to 4–5 inches. Fed spinach side of bed with dilute fish emulsion (the uneven germination might be a nitrogen issue). Arugula first harvest leaves looking possible within a week.

Day 24 (Sept 26): First arugula harvest — 80g, cut-and-come-again method. Fresh, slightly peppery, very good. Spinach has caught up — all sections now have strong seedlings.

**Notes:**

Arugula is forgiving. Spinach prefers consistent moisture in early germination more than I give it credit for. Next planting I'll cover with row cover for the first week to retain moisture.
