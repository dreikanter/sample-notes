# Garlic seed received — inspection notes

The order from Southern Exposure arrived today, as promised in the planting plan [[20230827_1246]]. Three varieties.

**Chesnok Red (8 heads, ~20 cloves expected)**

The heads are large and well-formed. Papery white outer skin with a distinctive red-pink blush on the inner scales. Each head has 8–10 cloves arranged in two layers around the central stalk. The cloves are plump and firm — no soft spots or mold. This is the one I'm most excited about.

**German Red (6 heads, ~18 cloves expected)**

True to reputation: large cloves, fewer per head. The outer skin is intact on all heads. One head has a small nick on the outer scale from packing; the cloves inside look fine. This variety tends to have 4–7 large cloves per head, which makes it faster to peel in quantity.

**Inchelium Red (5 heads, ~20 cloves expected)**

Softneck, so no central hard stalk — it bends and curls. These look healthy. Softnecks store longer and the cloves are arranged in concentric rings; I'm counting 3 rings on most heads.

**Plan:**

Storing in a mesh bag in the cool, dry pantry at approximately 13°C until planting. Target plant date: October 20–22.

I'll crack the heads the day before planting to minimize the time the cloves are exposed. Sorting by size — smaller cloves will go in one cluster, larger elsewhere, to plan for the variation in final bulb size.
