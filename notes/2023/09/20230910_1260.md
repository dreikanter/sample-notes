# Butternut squash soup — autumn first batch

Made the first proper autumn soup of the season with the butternut squashes from the garden. This is a loose recipe — more of a method.

**Ingredients for 4 servings:**

- 1 large butternut squash (about 1.2kg), halved and seeded
- 1 medium onion, quartered
- 1 whole head of garlic, top cut off
- 4 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 litre good chicken stock (or vegetable stock)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Pinch of cayenne
- Salt, black pepper
- Juice of half a lemon

**Method:**

1. Preheat oven to 200°C. Place squash cut side up on a baking tray with the onion and garlic. Drizzle everything with oil, season. Roast 45–55 minutes until completely soft and caramelizing at the edges.

2. Squeeze garlic from skins. Scoop squash from skin. Add all of this to a pot with the stock.

3. Blend until smooth. Reheat gently. Season with paprika, cayenne, lemon, salt.

4. Adjust texture with more stock if needed.

**Optional finishes I've tried:**

- Swirl of crème fraîche and toasted pumpkin seeds (classic, good)
- Toasted brown butter and a few sage leaves fried crisp (better, more work)
- Grated Gruyère and a float of good olive oil (I liked this most)

The roasting is essential. You cannot make this well by boiling the squash in stock — it goes thin and flat. The caramelization is the flavor.
