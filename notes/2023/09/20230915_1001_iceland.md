# Notes on Iceland

Iceland is a Nordic island country in the North Atlantic, sitting on the
Mid-Atlantic Ridge where the Eurasian and North American plates meet. The
result is an unusually volcanic landscape with hot springs, geysers, and
frequent eruptions.

A few things worth remembering:

- The population is small (around 380,000) and concentrated in the
  capital region, Reykjavík.
- Roughly 11% of the country is covered by glaciers; Vatnajökull is the
  largest in Europe by volume.
- Almost all electricity and heating comes from geothermal and
  hydroelectric sources.
- The language, Icelandic, has changed relatively little since the
  settlement era, which is why modern readers can still parse the
  medieval sagas with some effort.
