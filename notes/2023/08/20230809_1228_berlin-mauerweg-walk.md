# Berlin Mauerweg — Bornholmer to Nordbahnhof

Walked the Mauerweg section between Bornholmer Strasse and Nordbahnhof this morning. About 4.5km, took two and a half hours with stops.

The Bornholmer Strasse checkpoint was the first one to open on the night of November 9, 1989. There's a plaque and a few interpretation panels, not overdone. Standing there on a Tuesday morning watching cyclists and dog walkers cross what was once the most militarized border in Europe has a specific, hard-to-articulate weight to it.

The path cuts through the area called the Mauerpark neighborhood now. The park itself is built on the former death strip — the wide cleared zone between the two parallel walls. The ground is level and a bit featureless in a way that makes sense once you understand its history.

At Nordbahnhof, the S-Bahn station has a permanent exhibition on the ghost stations — the platforms that West Berlin trains passed through without stopping during the division, sealed off but kept in service just enough to prevent flooding. The photographs of empty, unchanged 1961 platforms are eerie.

Found a Stehcafé near Schwedter Strasse serving excellent filter coffee for €2.20. No seating. Everyone stood at the narrow counter along the window.

Full Mauerweg map and route info: https://www.berlin.de/mauer/en/history/berlin-wall-trail/
