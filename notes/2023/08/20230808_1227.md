# Sourdough crumb troubleshooting

My last two loaves have had a gummy, dense crumb despite a good-looking crust and nice oven spring. Working through the usual suspects.

**Possible causes and what I checked:**

*Underproofing* — Timer said 4 hours bulk, then 1 hour cold final proof. Poke test felt sluggish but springy. Probably not underproofed.

*Overhydration* — I bumped hydration to 78% last month. The dough was extensible but not slack. Might be too high for my flour's protein content. My flour is a local stone-ground AP at 10.5% protein, which is on the lower end. King Arthur bread flour at 12.7% would handle 78% more easily. Reference: https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/learn/guides/sourdough/bake

*Underbaking* — I'm pulling at internal temp 96°C. Standard recommendation is 96–99°C. Going to push to 98°C next bake.

*Steam release* — I'm using a Dutch oven. I remove the lid at 20 minutes and bake an additional 25. Extended this to 30 last week and got better crust but still the gummy interior.

*Starter activity* — Fed at 1:5:5 the evening before, used at peak (roughly 8 hours later in a 70°F kitchen). Float test passed. This seems fine.

**Plan for next bake:**

- Drop to 75% hydration
- Bake to 98°C internal
- Let cool for a full 2 hours before cutting (I've been cutting at 45 minutes — this might be the culprit; gluten structure isn't set)

The cooling point is probably underrated. Bread keeps cooking after it comes out.
