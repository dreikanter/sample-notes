# Fall garlic planting plan

Planning garlic for fall planting (target: October 15–25 window, after first frost forecast but before hard freeze). Notes for the two beds.

## Varieties to plant

**Chesnok Red (hardneck, Rocambole type)**  
Best flavor I've grown — rich, complex, slightly sweet when roasted. Stores 4–6 months. Plant 20 cloves.

**German Red (hardneck, Rocambole type)**  
Larger cloves, easier to peel. A bit more assertive raw. Good for cooking where you need a lot of garlic. Plant 15 cloves.

**Inchelium Red (softneck)**  
For longer storage into spring. Milder flavor but good all-purpose kitchen garlic. Plant 10 cloves.

## Source

Ordering from Southern Exposure Seed Exchange again: https://www.southernexposure.com/categories/garlic/

Ordered the same three varieties last year and they arrived in good condition with large, healthy heads for replanting.

## Bed preparation

- Clear the tomato bed (San Marzanos and one Cherokee Purple) after first killing frost
- Amend with 2 inches of compost, work in with garden fork
- Add bone meal to each planting hole (phosphorus for root development)
- Plant 3–4 inches deep, 6 inches apart, pointed side up
- Mulch with straw after planting to prevent frost heaving

## Schedule

- Sept 30: order garlic seed
- Oct 15: begin clearing beds
- Oct 20–25: plant
- May/June: harvest when lower 2–3 leaves yellow
