# Recipe — Gemista (Greek stuffed tomatoes and peppers)

Made this on Sunday following the method from Diane Kochilas' site. It's a good late-summer dish when tomatoes and peppers are abundant and you want something that can go in the oven while you do other things.

**Vegetables**

- 4 large ripe tomatoes
- 4 green bell peppers (or a mix of colors)

**Stuffing**

- 200g short-grain rice (Arborio works)
- 1 medium onion, grated
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- Handful fresh mint, chopped
- Handful flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 100g pine nuts (toasted) — optional but worth it
- Salt and black pepper

**Method**

1. Preheat oven to 190°C.
2. Slice tops off tomatoes and peppers, hollow out. Reserve tomato pulp and roughly chop.
3. Mix pulp, rice, onion, garlic, herbs, olive oil. Season generously. The rice will absorb liquid from the tomatoes as it bakes.
4. Fill each vegetable about two-thirds full (rice expands considerably).
5. Replace tops, arrange in baking dish. Pour 150ml water and a generous drizzle of olive oil into the base.
6. Bake covered 45 minutes, uncovered 20 more minutes until rice is tender and tops are lightly caramelized.

Source: https://www.dianekochilas.com/recipe/gemista-stuffed-tomatoes-and-peppers/

Serve at room temperature. Good the next day. I added a small amount of dried currants to the stuffing mix which is not traditional but worked well.
