# Restaurant review — Kin Dee, Berlin

Went to Kin Dee on Lützowstrasse on my last evening in Berlin. It's a Thai restaurant run by chef Dalad Kambhu, who holds a Michelin star. Booked two weeks in advance and got the last available slot.

The menu is prix-fixe with a few choices. We opted for the full tasting menu (seven courses).

Highlights:

**Corn and lemongrass soup:** Incredibly light. Aromatic in a way that felt less like perfume and more like walking into a kitchen where the right things are happening. A tiny amount of coconut cream swirled at the end.

**Betel leaf with dried shrimp and shallot:** One bite. Intense, saline, bright from lime, soft and herbal from the leaf. Perfect constructed bite.

**River prawn with green mango:** The prawn was grilled over charcoal and arrived on a mortar of sorts — the shell made a kind of bowl. Green mango salad underneath was aggressive and sour.

**Khao mun gai (poached chicken with rice):** This sounds humble in a tasting menu context and that's the point. The chicken had exceptional texture — still slightly pink at the breast center in a deliberate, perfectly safe way. The broth served alongside had depth I couldn't account for.

Drinks: we did the wine pairing, which leaned toward Austrian and German whites — good choices for the food's acidity and fragrance.

Price: about €90 per person with the pairing.

Reservations: https://kindeeberlin.com/

Would return without hesitation.
