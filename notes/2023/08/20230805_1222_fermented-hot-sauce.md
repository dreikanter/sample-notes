# Fermented hot sauce — first batch

Used the glut of Fresno and cayenne chiles from the garden. Total weight 450g.

## Ingredients

- 400g Fresno chiles, stems removed
- 50g cayenne chiles, stems removed
- 15g kosher salt (3% of total weight with the brine)
- 4 garlic cloves, smashed
- 1 tsp cumin seeds, lightly toasted
- Filtered water to cover

## Process

1. Blended chiles and garlic roughly in the food processor — left some texture.
2. Mixed with salt until evenly distributed.
3. Packed into a 1-quart mason jar, pressed down to release liquid, weighted with a small zip-lock bag filled with brine.
4. Covered with a cloth and rubber band.
5. Left at 70°F room temperature.

## Fermentation log

Day 1 (Aug 5): Nothing visible yet. Smells sharp and green.
Day 3 (Aug 7): Brine clouding up, small bubbles at the surface. Smells funky and complex.
Day 5 (Aug 9): Active bubbling when I press the weight down. Color has shifted from bright red to a deeper, more muted tone.
Day 7 (Aug 11): Taste test — sour, hot, and savory. Ready to blend and bottle.

Reference process from Serious Eats: https://www.seriouseats.com/fermented-fresh-chile-hot-sauce-recipe

Blended final product with a splash of apple cider vinegar to brighten and extend shelf life. Yield was about 300ml.
