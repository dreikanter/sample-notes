# Dani visit — weekend notes

Dani arrived Saturday midday. Eight months since the last proper visit — we'd spoken on the phone in June but that's different.

**Saturday**

Walked along the canal to the lock, then back through the market. I made the Japanese curry ([[20230516_1158]]) for dinner — the version with a pork cutlet. Dani ate three portions. We talked until nearly 1 am about the usual subjects: work, cities we're thinking about, what we want the next two years to look like.

Dani is at a more actively transitional point than me — considering a move, considering a change of field, not sure which happens first or whether they're connected. The kind of conversation that's useful precisely because it doesn't resolve anything but maps the terrain more accurately.

**Sunday**

Slower morning. Coffee and the Munro stories. Walked to the viewpoint that looks north across the city — good clear visibility for late July. Took the bus to the gallery to see an exhibition of works on paper that I'd been meaning to see for two months.

The exhibition was better than expected. Works on paper have a particular intimacy — everything is the scale of the hand, and the evidence of process is visible in a way that canvas work often conceals.

Dani left around 18:00. Post-visit flatness is a real thing: the flat is quiet in a specific way after a full weekend. Cooked simple food, read, went to bed early.

Worth doing more of this. I always underestimate how useful unstructured time with a friend who knows you well is.
