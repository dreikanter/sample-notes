# Sourdough — successful bake after troubleshooting

Following the troubleshooting notes from last week [[20230808_1227]], I made three changes: dropped hydration to 75%, baked to 98°C internal, and waited the full two hours before cutting.

**Result:** The crumb is open and moist without being gummy. The crust has good color and shatters slightly when you press it. The wait was definitely part of the problem — I sliced into the last loaf at 40 minutes and the interior was still setting up.

**Bake log:**

- Autolyse: 45 min, 75% hydration (flour + water only)
- Add levain (20% inoculation) + salt, mix by hand
- Bulk: 4.5 hours at 75°F, 4 sets of stretch and fold in first 2 hours
- Shape: batard, into floured banneton
- Cold retard: 14 hours in fridge
- Bake: preheat Dutch oven to 260°C. Bake at 250°C lid on 20 min, 230°C lid off 25 min
- Internal temp at pull: 98.2°C
- Cool: 2 hours on wire rack, did not cut

**Crumb:** Large, irregular holes in the upper portion, slightly denser at the base — typical batard structure. No gumminess. Good chew.

The long cold proof contributes more to flavor complexity than I'd been giving it credit for. This loaf tastes more sour and complex than recent attempts.

Full documentation of the process I'm following: https://www.theperfectloaf.com/guides/how-to-make-sourdough-bread/

Will repeat exactly and document next bake to confirm consistency.
