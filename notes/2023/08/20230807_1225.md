# Journal — heat wave, August 7

It's 38°C and my apartment is holding steady at around 28°C inside, which is actually better than I expected given that I don't have central air. The combination of blackout curtains on the south-facing windows, a box fan positioned to pull cooler air from the shaded north side, and strict no-cooking after 10am has kept things livable.

Spent most of the morning reading on the couch. Finished the third chapter of the Graeber book and started the fourth, which gets into ancient Mesopotamia and the relationship between temples, debt records, and early currency. The idea that debt is older than money, and that money emerged partly to make debt portable, keeps rattling around in my head.

Made cold brew this morning — 80g coarse-ground Colombian in 700ml cold filtered water, left for 18 hours in the fridge. It's good. Cold brew forgives imprecise water temperatures and that's the whole point today.

The tomatoes need watering but I'm not going out until after 6pm. The Sungolds are probably splitting in the heat but I'd rather lose a few than get heatstroke.

Thinking about what to cook for dinner that doesn't require the oven: probably a big salad with feta and the Sungolds I'll pick at dusk, dressed with the fermented hot sauce from last week [[20230805_1222]]. Add some crusty bread from the freezer, toasted in the dry skillet.

Read a good piece on urban heat islands and how they're affecting summer baselines: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41558-021-01136-6

Grateful for ceiling fans and good books.
