# Tomato harvest notes — early August

The Sungold cherry tomatoes are going absolutely berserk. I harvested about 400g yesterday and the same again today. They're cracking less than last year — I think spacing the drip irrigation to every other day instead of daily has made the fruit tougher-skinned without sacrificing sweetness.

The San Marzanos are not there yet. Blossom end rot hit maybe 15% of the first flush, which I traced back to irregular watering during the heat dome in mid-July. Amended the bed with lime two weeks ago and the second flush looks much cleaner. Will know in another week.

Brandywines are huge and healthy but still completely green. One plant has a cluster of five fruits that are each easily 300g. I've been using the University of Missouri extension guide on tomato ripening cues: https://extension.missouri.edu/publications/g6461

The Cherokee Purples are starting to show that characteristic dark-shoulder coloring. Flavor on this variety is always worth the wait — sweet with a kind of savory undertone that makes raw slicing way more interesting than any red tomato.

Pest report: spotted two tomato hornworms and removed them by hand. No aphids this year, which I'm crediting to the companion basil and the ladybug population that seems to have moved in. Also noticed some slight early blight on the lower leaves of one San Marzano; stripped and bagged those leaves immediately.

Will write up the full season results in October once everything is done.
