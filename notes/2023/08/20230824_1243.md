# Meeting notes — Q3 engineering retrospective

Date: 2023-08-24  
Attendees: full engineering team (12 people)  
Facilitator: me

## Format

Used the 4Ls retrospective format: Liked, Learned, Lacked, Longed for. Ran it as anonymous input via Miro, then grouped and discussed.

## Liked

- The new on-call runbook structure (six mentions) — people felt clearer about what to do and escalation paths were better
- The decision to do shorter sprints (two weeks instead of three) — felt more momentum, less coasting at the end of a long sprint
- Async standups — two people mentioned the written format was more useful than video for context

## Learned

- Container queries (CSS) are actually usable now — came up after I shared the notes [[20230820_1239]]
- How to use the new observability dashboard — Marcus ran a great lunch-and-learn on this
- A lot of people learned about the rate limit investigation from the post-mortem [[20230806_1223]] — the write-up was cited as one of the better ones we've done

## Lacked

- Better design-engineering handoff — several people mentioned getting designs that didn't account for edge states
- Clearer ownership on cross-team dependencies
- More time for technical debt work — currently 0% of sprint capacity is explicitly allocated to it

## Longed for

- A shared space for knowledge that isn't Slack (Slack is where knowledge goes to disappear)
- More frequent pairing sessions — several engineers mentioned wanting to work alongside colleagues more
- Better incident tracking across quarters so we can see trends

## Actions

1. Schedule a design-engineering handoff improvement workshop next sprint
2. Investigate options for internal knowledge base (Notion, Confluence, Obsidian team edition?)
3. Propose allocating 10% of sprint capacity to tech debt starting Q4
