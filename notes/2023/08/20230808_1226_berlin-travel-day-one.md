# Berlin — arrival and first impressions

Landed at BER at 7:15am after a rough overnight flight. Immigration was quick. The S-Bahn from the airport to Mitte took about 30 minutes and cost €3.20 on the 9-euro successor ticket. The new system is called the Deutschlandticket and is €49/month for unlimited regional transit — remarkable value if you live here.

First stop: dropped bags at the apartment (Airbnb in Prenzlauer Berg, fourth floor walkup, quiet courtyard). Then found a bakery two doors down called Zeit für Brot and ate a cardamom knot and a flat white standing at the counter. Recovery begun.

Prenzlauer Berg feels different from my last visit in 2018. It was already gentrified then but there are more international restaurants now, fewer of the old döner shops. Still beautiful in the way that Berlin neighborhoods are beautiful — wide streets, linden trees, ornate facades on the Gründerzeit buildings.

Walked down to Helmholtzplatz in the afternoon, just sitting and watching. A group of teenagers were playing chess at a public table. Some kind of community garden occupied one corner. Berlin's parks and squares have a particular quality of allowing people to use them without being told how.

Took the U2 to Potsdamer Platz in the evening out of curiosity. It still feels like a mall dropped into the center of a city, corporate and slightly airless compared to the neighborhoods.

More tomorrow — planning to walk the Mauerweg section between Bornholmer Strasse and Nordbahnhof.
