# Fermented hot sauce — batch two

First batch (three weeks ago) was too salty and not hot enough. Adjusting the recipe.

**Batch two recipe (makes ~400 mL)**

- 400 g fresh chillies: 250 g cayenne, 100 g jalapeño, 50 g bird's eye
- 4 garlic cloves
- 1 tsp non-iodised salt (2% brine target)
- 250 mL filtered water
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar (added post-ferment)

**Process**

Blend chillies and garlic coarse. Pack into a 500 mL jar. Dissolve salt in water, pour over mash to cover. Weigh down with a zip-lock bag of brine. Cover with muslin. Ferment at room temp (20–22 °C) for 7 days, pressing down daily.

After 7 days: taste for tang. If it needs more acidity, go to 10 days. Blend smooth, strain through a medium sieve, stir in ACV. Bottle and refrigerate.

**Adjustments from batch one**

- Dropped salt from 2.5% to 2% — batch one was almost inedible
- Added bird's eye chillies for more heat without changing volume
- Will add a small carrot (50 g) to soften the raw chilli edge

**Fermentation notes**

Day 2: Good bubble activity, colour deepening. The bird's eyes are visually dominant — whole batch has turned a deep red-orange. Room smells aggressively of fermented capsaicin. Neighbours have not complained. Yet.

Reference: [Serious Eats fermented hot sauce guide](https://www.seriouseats.com/homemade-fermented-hot-sauce-recipe)
