# Garden sowing log — April 29

Updating from [[20230416_1118]].

**Status check**

Radishes sown 16 April: germinated day 4, now showing true leaves. Growth is faster than expected given the temperature. Will thin to 5 cm spacing this weekend — currently overcrowded.

Basil (indoors): germinated day 6. Leggy despite the south windowsill. Going to try moving under a small grow light I borrowed — the natural light isn't enough at this latitude in April.

Salad mix: patchy germination. Maybe 60% take. The glass cover may have caused condensation issues — some of the seeds may have rotted. Will direct sow more this week as a backup.

Garlic (from November): six of six cloves now showing. The two I'd written off are growing, just slow. Scapes haven't formed yet but the leaves are 20–25 cm. Expecting scapes in four to six weeks.

**New this week**

- Sowed French beans in individual modules (3 seeds per module) — want to transplant as a block
- Potted on the mint into a 25 cm terracotta; added grit to the compost mix for drainage
- Ordered a packet of borage seeds from [Chiltern Seeds](https://www.chilternseeds.co.uk) — companion plant and the flowers are edible

**Weather note**

Overnight frost on 25th got down to −1 °C but the fleece over the radishes protected them. Forecast shows clear nights ahead, temps above 5 °C from next week. Will start hardening off the basil then.
