# Sourdough troubleshooting — dense crumb

Third bake in a row with an under-proofed-looking crumb: tight bubbles, gummy patches, no real open structure. Working through possible causes.

**Starter activity:** Fed at 1:5:5 ratio. Peaked at 6 hours at 22 °C. That's slow but not alarmingly so. Float test passed. Not the starter.

**Shaping tension:** Reviewed my technique against [The Bread Code shaping video](https://www.youtube.com/c/TheBreadCode). I think I'm degassing too aggressively during pre-shape. Going to try a looser pre-shape and a shorter bench rest next time.

**Bulk fermentation timing:** Room has been cool this week (19–20 °C). I've been bulk-fermenting for 4 hours, which at that temp is probably not enough. The dough should roughly double with visible bubble activity. Mine is expanding maybe 50% and showing bubbles only at the edges. Going to extend to 5.5–6 hours next bake.

**Final proof:** Retarded overnight in the fridge. Baked cold from the fridge into a preheated Dutch oven at 250 °C. Scored with a wet lame at 30 degrees. This part seems fine.

Hypothesis for next bake: longer bulk at room temp (6 h), gentler pre-shape, shorter bench rest (15 min), then same retard-and-bake process. Will also try a slightly higher hydration (72% instead of 70%) to see if that opens the crumb.
