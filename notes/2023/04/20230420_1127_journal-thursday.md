# Journal — Thursday 20 April

Woke at 6:15 before the alarm. The light this time of year hits the ceiling first — a warm bar that moves across the plaster over about twenty minutes while I'm still in bed deciding whether to get up. Got up.

Made coffee before checking anything. This is the one habit I've managed to maintain consistently: no screens until after the first cup. Probably sounds minor but it used to be that my phone was the first thing I looked at and the first ten minutes of the day were already spent reacting to things.

Work was fine. Three meetings, all of which could have been shorter. The afternoon was clear and I used it properly — got through the backlog of code review that had been sitting since last week. Satisfying in the way that clearing a blocked drain is satisfying: not elegant, but necessary.

Went for a run at 17:30. The route I've been doing takes me past the old print works that's been converted into flats. They've kept the industrial chimney as an ornament, which I find touching rather than kitsch. There's something honest about preserving the evidence of what a place used to do.

Read some Enzensberger before dinner — the essay on the European civil war of the 20th century. Dense but worth it. He's very good at the paragraph that forces you to stop and re-read.
