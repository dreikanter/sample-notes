# Balcony garden — April check-in

The parsley that overwintered under the fleece made it through. It's leggy but putting out new growth from the base. Pinched the tops to encourage branching.

Sowed this week:
- Radishes (Cherry Belle) — direct into the big terracotta pot
- Basil (Genovese) — in seed trays inside, will move out after last frost
- Salad mix (mesclun blend from Chiltern Seeds) — shallow tray, covered with glass

Still waiting on the garlic I planted in November. Four of the six cloves have shoots of 8–10 cm. The other two look dead but I'll leave them another three weeks before giving up.

The mint I thought I'd killed with overwatering has resprouted from the roots. Repotted it into a smaller container with better drainage and moved it to the south-facing corner.

Aphids already visible on the nasturtiums — removed by hand today. Going to try companion planting with marigolds this year. According to [RHS guidance on aphid control](https://www.rhs.org.uk/biodiversity/aphids), encouraging ladybirds is the most effective long-term approach, so I've placed a ladybird house near the railing.

Ambient temps this week: 8 °C low, 17 °C high. Still getting frost warnings for overnight next week — keeping the tender seedlings inside until at least the 24th.
