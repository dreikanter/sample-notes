# Pasta al limone — recipe notes

This is based loosely on the Samin Nosrat version from her [New York Times piece](https://www.nytimes.com/2022/02/18/dining/pasta-al-limone.html) but adjusted after a few test runs.

**For two**

- 200 g spaghetti or linguine
- 1 large unwaxed lemon (zest + juice)
- 60 g unsalted butter, cold, cut into cubes
- 40 g Parmigiano Reggiano, finely grated (plus more to serve)
- 2 tbsp reserved pasta water (minimum)
- Salt, white pepper

**Method**

Cook pasta in generously salted water to al dente. While it cooks, zest the lemon into a large bowl. Add half the lemon juice.

Drain pasta, reserving a good 100 mL of water. Immediately add pasta to the bowl with the lemon. Add butter cubes and toss rapidly — the heat of the pasta melts the butter. Add pasta water 1 tbsp at a time while tossing until you have a creamy, emulsified sauce clinging to the strands. Add Parmigiano in two or three stages, continuing to toss. Taste, add more lemon juice, salt, and white pepper.

**Notes from test runs**

- Cold butter is essential for emulsification. Room-temp butter breaks the sauce.
- Work fast. Once the pasta cools below ~65 °C the sauce goes grainy.
- White pepper over black — the floral quality works better with lemon.
- A small amount of good olive oil at the end adds body.
