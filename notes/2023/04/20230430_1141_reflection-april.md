# Reflection — end of April

April felt longer than it was, which I mean as a compliment. The trip to Berlin stretched the month in a useful way — a week that felt like a separate chapter rather than another block of evenings.

**What went well**

The no-phone-before-coffee habit stuck. I tracked it loosely: 24 out of 30 days. The six failures were all on days when I woke up anxious about something specific — the habit is weak precisely where it would be most useful.

Work was in a productive phase. The pipeline refactor shipped cleanly. I had more focused time than usual because I was more deliberate about protecting the afternoon block.

The bread baking experiments ([[20230417_1120]], [[20230427_1138]]) produced one genuinely good rye loaf.

**What didn't**

Social energy was low. Cancelled two things I shouldn't have. The introvert-recharge logic made sense in the moment but I'm aware it's a rationalisation I reach for too readily.

The blog post didn't get started. It moved forward on the to-do list every week but that's not the same as making progress.

**Intention for May**

Write at least one draft of the blog post, even if rough. Call the two people I've been deferring. Get the basil actually growing.
