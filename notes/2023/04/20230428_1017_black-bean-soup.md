# Black bean soup recipe

This version uses dried chiles rather than chili powder, which gives the broth a more layered flavour without much extra effort.

**Ingredients (serves 4)**

- 500 g dried black beans, soaked overnight
- 2 ancho chiles, stems and seeds removed
- 1 pasilla chile, stems and seeds removed
- 1 large white onion, roughly chopped
- 6 garlic cloves, smashed
- 1 tsp cumin, toasted and ground
- 1.5 L water or light vegetable stock
- Salt, lime, crema, and cilantro to finish

**Method**

Dry-toast the chiles in a heavy pan for about 30 seconds per side until they soften and smell smoky — not until they blacken. Transfer to a bowl, cover with boiling water, and soak for 20 minutes.

Sweat the onion in a little oil over medium heat until translucent, around 10 minutes. Add garlic and cumin, cook another 2 minutes. Drain the beans and add them to the pot with the stock. Drain the chiles, blend them with a cup of the soaking liquid, strain, and add the paste to the pot.

Simmer covered for 90 minutes or until beans are fully tender. Blend about a third of the soup and stir it back in to thicken. Adjust salt.

Serve with lime wedges, a spoon of crema, and fresh cilantro. Keeps well for four days refrigerated; add a splash of water when reheating.

Reference for chile types: [Chile pepper varieties at Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/all-about-dried-chiles).
