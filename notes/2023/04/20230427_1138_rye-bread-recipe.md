# Rye bread — 100% sourdough

Dense, moist, keeps for a week. Very different from the wheat sourdough I usually make — no gluten development, no shaping, no scoring drama.

**For one small loaf (22 cm tin)**

- 350 g wholemeal rye flour
- 250 g rye sourdough starter (100% hydration, made with rye)
- 250 mL warm water
- 8 g salt
- 1 tbsp caraway seeds (optional but traditional)
- Sunflower seeds for the surface

**Method**

Mix everything together — it will be a sticky batter, not a dough. There is no kneading. Pour into a well-oiled tin. Smooth the surface with a wet spoon. Press sunflower seeds into the top. Cover with oiled cling film.

Proof at room temp (20–22 °C) for 8–12 hours. The batter should rise to within 1 cm of the tin rim and show some surface bubbles.

Bake at 220 °C (fan) for 15 minutes, then reduce to 190 °C for 50–60 minutes. Internal temp should reach 96–98 °C.

**Critical:** Wrap in a tea towel and let rest for at least 24 hours before cutting. Cutting warm rye bread gives a gummy, unpleasant texture. The waiting is worth it.
