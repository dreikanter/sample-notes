# Berlin travel notes — day one

Arrived at Tegel — wait, it's BER now. Schönefeld terminal. Still not used to the rebrand after all the jokes about the airport that took a decade too long. Bus to Südkreuz was straightforward: X7, then regional rail.

Staying in Neukölln, Weserstraße. The flat is on the fourth floor, no lift, as seems to be legally mandated in this city. View from the kitchen window is rooftop tanks and a chestnut tree that's about to bloom.

**Afternoon walk:** Down Weserstraße to the canal, then along the towpath toward Treptower Park. The park is enormous and half-empty on a Tuesday. The Soviet war memorial is something. Soldier carrying a rescued child, sword bisecting a swastika underfoot. The scale is totalising in a way that photographs don't convey.

**Dinner:** Hamy Café on Hasenheide — Vietnamese, excellent pho. Long queue but it moved fast. Cash only.

**Bookshop find:** Curious Fox near Hermannplatz. Bilingual secondhand. Found a copy of Hans Magnus Enzensberger's *Europe, Europe* in a Pantheon edition I hadn't seen before.

Useful transport note: the [BVG app](https://www.bvg.de/en) works but the offline map is incomplete for the outer boroughs. Download ÖPNV Karte separately if you plan to go past the Ring.
