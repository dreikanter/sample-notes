# Bread baking log — whole wheat sourdough attempt three

**Date:** 2025-09-08
**Flour:** 70% bread flour, 30% whole wheat (King Arthur)
**Hydration:** 75%
**Starter:** 20% inoculation, 12-hour levain

## Process

Mixed autolyse at 8 a.m.—just flour and water, held for 45 minutes. Added levain and salt, then did four sets of stretch-and-fold at 30-minute intervals. The dough felt extensible but not slack; it held its shape well by the fourth fold.

Bulk ferment ran from 9:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. at approximately 72°F (22°C). I didn't see as much rise as I'd hoped—maybe 40%—but the dough felt airy and had a pleasant tang. Preshaped, rested 20 minutes, final shaped into a batard, then into the banneton.

Cold retard in the refrigerator for 18 hours.

## Bake

Scored with a curved lame at a shallow angle. Dutch oven preheated to 500°F. Baked covered for 20 minutes, uncovered for 22 minutes at 450°F. Internal temperature at pull: 207°F.

## Result

Crust: excellent. Deep mahogany, shatters when tapped. Crumb: open but not gaping—good for sandwiches. Flavor: noticeably more complex than attempt two, with a mild sourness that doesn't overpower the wheat.

Still chasing a more open crumb. The [The Perfect Loaf](https://www.theperfectloaf.com/) community suggests increasing hydration to 78–80% and shortening bulk ferment time slightly.

**Next time:** Try 78% hydration and 5-hour bulk.
