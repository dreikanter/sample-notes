## Notes on September 17 run

Ran the river trail out-and-back. The trail surface is still soft from recent rain, which slowed me slightly but was easier on the knees. Saw three herons and one beaver—unexpected for 7 a.m. on a weekday.

Last kilometer was hard. I pushed through rather than walking, which felt important even though the time difference is probably negligible.

## Thoughts on tracking

Paper works fine for capturing data but I can't visualize trends easily. Intervals.icu has a good fitness/fatigue chart based on the TSS model from [TrainingPeaks methodology](https://www.trainingpeaks.com/learn/articles/the-science-of-the-performance-manager/). May be overkill for my volume but it's free.
