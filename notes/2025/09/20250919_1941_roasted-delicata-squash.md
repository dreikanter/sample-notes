# Roasted delicata squash with miso butter

Made this with the delicata from the farmers market (see [[20250914_1930]]). Came out very well.

## Ingredients

- 2 medium delicata squash
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
- 1 tbsp white miso
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- Black pepper
- Optional: fresh thyme or sage

## Method

Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).

Cut the delicata in half lengthwise and scoop out seeds. No need to peel—the skin is thin and edible, which is the whole point of delicata. Cut each half into half-moon slices about 2 cm thick, or leave as halves for a more dramatic presentation.

Mix butter, miso, and maple syrup until combined. Toss the squash pieces in about half the miso butter, spread on a parchment-lined baking sheet.

Roast 20–25 minutes, flipping once halfway. The edges should be caramelized and the flesh tender.

Remove from oven, dot remaining miso butter over the hot squash and let it melt. Season with black pepper.

## Notes

The miso-butter-maple combination is doing a lot of work here: umami, fat, and sweetness balancing the squash's natural mildness. I've also seen versions with tahini instead of butter.

Good as a side dish or piled onto grain bowls. [Smitten Kitchen has a similar recipe](https://smittenkitchen.com/2013/10/roasted-delicata-squash/) that adds pepitas.

**Next time:** Try with sage browned in the butter before mixing in the miso.
