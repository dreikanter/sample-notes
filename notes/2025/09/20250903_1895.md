# Tomato sauce — end of season large batch

The cherry tomato plants are slowing down and the remaining fruit is all ripening at once. Made a large batch of roasted tomato sauce to put away for winter.

**The quantities**

About 4kg of mixed tomatoes — predominantly Sweet Million cherries, a few late Black Krims [[20250712_1866]], a handful of random ones. Also: 1 large onion roughly chopped, 8 garlic cloves unpeeled, several sprigs of thyme, good olive oil, salt.

**Method**

Everything into two large roasting tins, drizzle of olive oil, salt. Roasted at 180°C for 90 minutes. The tomatoes collapse and the juices concentrate. The garlic becomes sweet and tender inside its papery skin.

Let cool slightly. Squeeze the garlic cloves out of their skins — they slip out soft and caramelized. Pass everything through a food mill (not a blender — the food mill catches skins and seeds but lets the flesh through, giving a cleaner texture). Taste, adjust salt.

**Yield and storage**

The 4kg of fresh tomatoes yielded about 1.2kg of sauce — substantial reduction. Portioned into six 200ml portions and frozen.

**Using it**

This sauce is versatile because it's not seasoned beyond salt — no basil, no chili, no oregano. Add aromatics depending on what you're making. Good as a pasta sauce base, a pizza sauce with oregano added, a braising liquid.

The flavor is noticeably more complex than any commercial tomato product — roasting concentrates and caramelizes in a way that pouring from a can doesn't replicate.
