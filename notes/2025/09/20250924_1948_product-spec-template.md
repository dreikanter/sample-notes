# Product spec template — lightweight version

Based on a format I've iterated on over the last two years of writing specs. Meant to be the minimum useful structure—not a 20-section monolith.

---

## Problem statement

*What user or business problem are we solving? Why now?*

One paragraph maximum. If you can't state the problem clearly in a paragraph, you probably don't understand it well enough yet.

## Users affected

*Who has this problem? How many? How severe?*

Be specific. "Our users" is not specific. Name the segment, quote support tickets if you have them.

## Proposed solution

*What we're building and what we're explicitly not building.*

A brief paragraph on scope followed by a "non-goals" list. Non-goals prevent scope creep and reduce later arguments about what was agreed.

## Success metrics

*How will we know this worked?*

At least one quantitative metric tied to the problem. Examples: support ticket reduction, activation rate for new users, retention improvement. Avoid vanity metrics.

## Open questions

*What do we still need to decide?*

List with owner and target resolution date. Clear accountability here prevents specs from going stale.

## Dependencies

*What needs to happen before or alongside this?*

## Timeline sketch

Not a commitment—a rough expectation for planning purposes.
