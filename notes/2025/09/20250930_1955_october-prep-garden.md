# October garden prep checklist

End of September. The garden needs attention before temperatures drop consistently. Referencing the garlic plan from [[20250920_1943]].

## This week (before Oct 5)

- [ ] Pull spent summer annuals (cosmos, marigolds, zinnias)
- [ ] Cut back the overgrown oregano — take 2/3 of the growth
- [ ] Divide the hostas while soil is still workable
- [ ] Take cuttings of the tender sage before first frost

## Week of October 6

- [ ] Amend beds 2 and 4 with compost (2–3 inches, worked in)
- [ ] Test soil pH (kit is in the garage)
- [ ] Order the straw for mulching — need about a half bale

## Planting week (October 12–15)

- [ ] Plant German Red and Chesnok Red garlic
- [ ] Plant spring bulbs: tulips (25), daffodils (30), crocus (50)
- [ ] Cover garlic bed with 4 inches of straw mulch

## Before end of October

- [ ] Dig up dahlia tubers, store in cool dry location
- [ ] Bring in any containers that won't survive frost
- [ ] Drain and coil the hose before first hard freeze
- [ ] Wrap the fig tree in burlap

## General

The figuring-out process for spring bulb placement: map the beds before planting, mark where bulbs go with small flags or labeled sticks so you don't dig them up later.

Resource: [Old Farmer's Almanac frost dates calculator](https://www.almanac.com/gardening/frostdates) for confirming local timing.
