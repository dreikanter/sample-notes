# Reflections on the summer — what I want to remember

This is the last weekend of what has felt like summer, even if the meteorological calendar disagrees. The swallows are still here but only just. The tomatoes are done. The light is unmistakably autumn now.

I want to write down some things while they're still close.

The first heat of May [[20250531_1837]] — that specific shock of warmth after cold, the city not yet adjusted, the particular quality of surprise.

The fermentation batch [[20250520_1816]], still going, still producing tangy vegetables that have been in rotation for months. What seemed like an experiment has become a kitchen habit.

The solstice evening [[20250622_1855]] — sitting in the garden until the sky finally stopped being luminous and admitted night.

The bittern [[20250829_1891]], after months of hoping. The way it froze with its bill pointed upward into the reeds and simply ceased to exist as a visible thing. That was a gift.

The Rust project finished, the performance work completed, the API docs written and shipped. The work summer was solid.

The Black Krim tomato [[20250712_1866]], warm from the vine, eaten standing up at the kitchen counter. The exact peak of what a tomato can be.

There's something about trying to write down peak moments that changes them slightly — makes them more finished, more past. I don't think I can avoid this. It's the price of writing things down.

The next note I write will be in autumn.
