# Cep risotto — same day as the forage

Made this immediately with the foraged ceps [[20250906_1899]]. There's something compelling about eating a meal a few hours after you found the main ingredient.

**Serves 4**

- 300g ceps, cleaned with a brush (no water — they're sponges), roughly sliced
- 320g Arborio or Carnaroli rice
- 1 medium onion, finely diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 150ml dry white wine
- 1.2L hot chicken or vegetable stock (kept hot throughout)
- 60g good Parmesan, grated
- 40g butter
- Olive oil, salt, pepper
- Fresh parsley

**Method**

Warm a small amount of olive oil in a separate pan, cook the ceps over high heat until they release their liquid and then it evaporates, leaving them slightly golden. Season. Set aside.

In a wide pan: sweat onion in butter and olive oil for 8 minutes until soft. Add garlic, 1 minute. Add rice, stir to coat in the fat, toast for 2 minutes. Add wine, stir until absorbed.

Add hot stock one ladleful at a time, stirring frequently, allowing each addition to absorb before adding the next. This takes 18-20 minutes. Taste for doneness — it should be creamy with the grains just past al dente.

Fold in the ceps, Parmesan, a knob of butter, and parsley. Rest for 2 minutes off heat. Serve immediately.

The result was exceptional — the cep flavor is earthy and deep in a way dried mushrooms don't fully replicate.
