# Garlic planting plan — autumn 2025

Garlic goes in here in mid-October, around the first hard frost. I've been planning this since the garden note from September (see [[20250907_1916]]).

## Variety selection

Ordered two varieties from [Keene Organics](https://www.keeneorganics.com/):
- **German Red** (hardneck, Rocambole type): strong flavor, 8–10 cloves per bulb, doesn't store as long as softneck but the taste is worth it. Ordered 1 lb.
- **Chesnok Red** (hardneck, Purple Stripe): slightly milder, stores well. Ordered 0.5 lb.

Both are hardneck. I live in a climate with cold winters, and hardneck varieties require vernalization (cold period) to differentiate into bulbs—softneck is more suited to warmer climates.

## Bed preparation

Bed 2 and part of Bed 4. Need to:
1. Remove spent plants
2. Work in 2–3 inches of compost
3. Adjust pH if needed (garlic prefers 6.0–7.0; last test was 6.3, good)

## Planting

**Timing:** October 12–15
**Spacing:** 6 inches apart, 2 inches deep, pointed end up
**Row spacing:** 8–10 inches

After planting: cover with 3–4 inches of straw mulch to retain moisture and buffer soil temperature through freeze-thaw cycles.

## Harvest

Late June or early July 2026. The indicator is when about half the leaves have browned. Cure in a warm, shaded, well-ventilated spot for 3–4 weeks before storage.

Expected yield: ~40–50 bulbs. Enough to save some for next year's planting.
