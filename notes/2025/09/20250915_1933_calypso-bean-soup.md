# Calypso bean soup with smoked paprika

Developed this after buying the calypso beans at the market on Saturday (see [[20250914_1930]]). The farmer was right: they held their shape beautifully through a long simmer.

## Ingredients (serves 4)

- 300g dried calypso beans, soaked overnight
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, sliced
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- 400g canned tomatoes
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 1 bay leaf
- Salt, pepper
- Fresh parsley to finish
- Crusty bread to serve

## Method

Drain and rinse the soaked beans. In a large pot, heat olive oil over medium heat. Sauté onion until soft and slightly golden, about 10 minutes. Add garlic, smoked paprika, and cumin; cook 2 minutes.

Add tomatoes, stock, bay leaf, and beans. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer. Cook uncovered for 50–60 minutes until beans are fully tender and the broth has thickened slightly.

Season with salt and pepper. Taste: add more smoked paprika if it needs depth. Remove bay leaf.

Serve with chopped fresh parsley and crusty bread. Even better the next day.

## Notes

- The black-and-white pattern on the calypso beans fades to a mottled grey when cooked—don't expect them to stay pretty
- [Rancho Gordo](https://www.ranchogordo.com/) sells calypso beans online if you can't find them locally

**Result:** Rich and satisfying. The smoked paprika does most of the work.
