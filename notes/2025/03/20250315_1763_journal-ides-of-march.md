# Journal — ides of March

The Ides fell on a Saturday this year, which gave me license to be mildly dramatic about it without work getting in the way.

Took a long walk in the afternoon. The neighborhood has that early spring quality where things are almost green but not quite — a yellow-green that will deepen over the next few weeks. The cherry trees on the avenue are budding but not yet open. Maybe another ten days.

Spent part of the morning reading *The Givenness of Things* (Marilynne Robinson's essays). Her prose is slow and dense in the way of someone who actually trusts the reader to stay. The essay on grace is the one I keep thinking about — her argument is roughly that the category of the sacred shouldn't be abandoned simply because it's been misused. That the impulse to dismiss all transcendence is itself a kind of fundamentalism.

I don't know that I agree but I find the argument more interesting than the reflexive secular dismissal, which I'm more comfortable with.

Made ochazuke for lunch — leftover rice, a small amount of soy and mirin, hot green tea poured over, pickled plum on top. Quick and satisfying. The kind of thing that feels exactly right on a cold mid-March day.

The kimchi from [[20250220_1743]] is now at day 23. Deeply sour and crunchy. Best batch I've made.

Still no progress on the novel. Decided to stop trying to write and just read until May. More honest than the pretense.
