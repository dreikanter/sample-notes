# Wine notes — Burgundy tasting

Attended a small informal tasting at a friend's apartment — six people, four bottles, everyone brought something.

## Wines

**2019 Domaine Sylvain Pataille, Bourgogne Rouge:** This was the opener. Lighter style — high acid, red fruit (cranberry, cherry), very little tannin. Good for early drinking. Lively rather than serious. Solid entry-level Burgundy.

**2018 Domaine Hudelot-Noëllat, Chambolle-Musigny:** A step up. More structure, darker fruit, some earthiness in the mid-palate. The 2018 vintage was warmer than average so there's a riper quality that Burgundy purists sometimes find excessive, but I thought it worked here. Could age another 4-6 years.

**2017 Domaine Mugneret-Gibourg, Nuits-Saint-Georges 1er Cru:** The bottle of the evening. Complex and precise. Forest floor, dried roses, a firm mineral quality. Not obviously pleasurable at first — needed 20 minutes in the glass to open. By the end it was exceptional.

**2020 Domaine Génot-Boulanger, Meursault (white):** Chardonnay, white Burgundy. Brought as a counterpoint. Nutty, rounded, subtle oak. Not quite as impressive as the reds but well-made.

## Notes

The 2017 Mugneret-Gibourg is the benchmark for what good Nuits-Saint-Georges tastes like. Going to look for more of her wines.
