# Concert notes — chamber music series

Attended the third program of the local chamber series on Tuesday evening. Small hall, maybe 200 seats, good acoustics — wood-paneled and reflective without being reverberant.

## Program

**Beethoven, String Quartet Op. 18 No. 4 in C minor:** Opened the night. The quartet (Meridian String Quartet, based locally) played it with more urgency than I expected for an early Beethoven — the first movement had a driven quality that some ensembles smooth out. The slow movement was a little cautious.

**Fauré, Piano Quartet No. 2 in G minor, Op. 45:** The addition of the pianist (a guest, whose name I didn't catch) transformed the sound. Fauré's textures are warmer than the Beethoven — this music lives in the middle of the sonic spectrum. The third movement is one of those pieces that creates a particular suspended feeling, like the music is deciding something. Very fine.

**Brahms, Piano Quintet in F minor, Op. 34:** Added a second violinist for the quintet. The scope is bigger — this is Brahms at his most dense and intense. The performers were technically accomplished; the first and third movements were the best. The second movement felt slightly underpowered.

## Overall

Worth the subscription. The Fauré was the surprise — I wasn't familiar with this quartet and it's now something I'll seek out on recordings.
