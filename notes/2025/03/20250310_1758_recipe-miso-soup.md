# Recipe — proper miso soup

After years of making adequate miso soup, I finally learned to make dashi from scratch. The difference is significant enough that I'm not going back to dashi powder.

## Kombu dashi

- 10g kombu (dried kelp)
- 1 liter cold water

Put kombu in cold water. Bring slowly to 60°C and hold for 20-30 minutes. Do not boil — boiling makes the kombu bitter. Remove kombu. This is kombu dashi; add katsuobushi (bonito flakes) for awase dashi.

## Awase dashi (full)

After removing kombu, bring the stock to a gentle simmer. Add 20g katsuobushi (dried bonito flakes). Remove from heat, steep 5 minutes, then strain through a fine sieve or cheesecloth. 

## The soup

Per 400ml dashi:

- 3 tbsp white or mixed miso (I use Hikari brand)
- 100g silken tofu, cubed
- 2 tbsp wakame, rehydrated
- 2 green onions, thinly sliced

Bring dashi to a low simmer. Dissolve miso in a small ladleful of hot dashi before adding to the pot — prevents lumps. Add tofu and wakame. Do not boil after adding miso (kills the probiotic cultures and dulls the flavor). Serve immediately with green onion.

**Variations:**
- Add a few clams (asari) and no tofu
- Potato and onion (tonjiru style, with pork and more vegetables)
- Mushroom and burdock
