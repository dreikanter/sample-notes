# Spring equinox — March 20

The equinox fell at 9:01am today. I woke up before that, which I rarely do, and stood in the kitchen while the light came in from the east. It's strange to watch the sun rise when you usually don't — the color progression from gray to orange to white is much faster than I expect.

Checked on the seedlings first thing. The tomatoes have their second true leaves now and are starting to look like actual plants rather than sprouts. Still under the grow light 14 hours a day. The peppers are slower — first true leaves visible, no second yet.

In the garden: the spinach I sowed on March 13 (see [[20250313_1761]]) is showing a few small seedlings, barely visible. Germination is definitely happening, which is the main thing.

The snowdrops that appeared in early March (see [[20250306_1755]]) are fully open and there are now more of them — maybe 20 plants in the front bed. The crocuses are up as well, purple and yellow.

I'm told the equinox has been a significant marker in human culture for at least 10,000 years, which makes standing in a kitchen watching dawn feel appropriately archaic. We've been doing this for a long time — orienting ourselves by the sun.

Made toast and ate it standing at the window, watching the birds come to the feeder in the increasing light.
