# Recipe — simple roast chicken

Went back to basics. The most reliable version I've found.

## Ingredients

- One whole chicken, ~1.5kg
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
- 1 lemon, halved
- 1 head garlic, halved horizontally
- 4-5 thyme sprigs
- 1 tbsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

## What matters most

**Dry the chicken:** Pat thoroughly with paper towels. If time allows, salt it uncovered in the fridge for 24 hours. The salt draws out moisture and then the skin dries. This is the difference between crisp skin and rubbery skin.

**High heat:** 230°C throughout. Many recipes lower the heat mid-way through; I've found this produces steamed-tasting skin. High heat the whole time and don't open the oven.

**Don't stuff the cavity:** It inhibits airflow. Put aromatics in loosely — lemon, garlic, thyme — but not packed tight.

**Rest:** 15 minutes minimum under a loose foil tent before carving. The juices redistribute; skipping this step wastes flavor.

## Method

1. Remove chicken from fridge 30 min before cooking. Pat dry. Season all over with salt and pepper.
2. Rub butter under and over the skin.
3. Place lemon (squeezed inside the cavity), garlic, and thyme in the cavity.
4. Put on a wire rack set in a roasting pan. Truss or don't — I don't bother.
5. Roast at 230°C until thigh registers 74°C internally, about 55-65 minutes for a 1.5kg bird.
6. Rest 15 min, then carve.

The pan drippings make an excellent simple sauce with a splash of white wine and chicken stock.
