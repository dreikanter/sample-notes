# Roasted carrot soup with miso and ginger

This came out of wanting to use a full bag of carrots before they went limp. The roasting step makes a bigger difference than you'd expect — the sugars concentrate and you get some char on the edges that reads as smokiness in the finished soup.

**Serves 4. Takes about 55 minutes.**

### Ingredients

- 800 g carrots, scrubbed and cut into 3 cm chunks
- 1 medium onion, quartered
- 4 cloves garlic, unpeeled
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 2 tbsp white miso paste
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- juice of half a lemon
- salt, black pepper
- optional: crème fraîche and toasted sesame seeds to finish

### Method

Heat the oven to 210°C. Toss carrots and onion with oil, season well, spread on a large tray. Nestle the unpeeled garlic among them. Roast 30–35 minutes, turning once, until edges are dark.

Squeeze garlic from skins into a blender. Add the roasted vegetables, stock, miso, and ginger. Blend until smooth — work in batches if the blender is small. Add lemon juice. Taste and adjust salt.

Reheat gently. The miso will turn bitter if it boils hard, so keep it at a simmer.

### Notes

White miso is mild enough not to overpower the carrot. Red miso can work but cut the quantity by a third. The [Serious Eats guide to miso](https://www.seriouseats.com/what-is-miso-paste) has a useful breakdown of the varieties if you want to go further.

The soup thickens noticeably overnight. Thin with a splash of stock when reheating.
