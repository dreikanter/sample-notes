# March reflection — what I'm carrying forward

End of February. Strange month — shorter than it should be and somehow still too long. The cold is still here but there's something different in the light, a yellow quality in the late afternoon that wasn't present in January.

**Work:** The auth layer shipped to internal review. The pipeline post finally got written and published internally. Good to have those done. The next milestone is API v2 beta, which is a two-month sprint starting now.

**Reading:** Finished *Range* (uneven but worthwhile). Still mid-stream on Kleppmann (see [[20250227_1750]]). Started Robinson's *The Givenness of Things*, which is denser and more rewarding than I expected.

**Garden:** Seedlings are coming along (see [[20250226_1749]]). The tomatoes have firmed up under the LED bar. The Anaheim pepper replanting went in three days ago; no signs yet.

**Fermentation:** Kimchi at day 10 is excellent — the right amount of funk, good heat, still a little crunch in the cabbage. Would eat it daily.

**What I want for March:**

- Get outside more. February was too interior.
- Spend a weekend afternoon reading without devices nearby.
- Plant the direct-sow crops once the soil is workable (probably late March).
- Make progress on the novel I keep starting and stopping.

That last item keeps appearing on lists and nothing happens to it. Maybe March is no different. But writing it down is at least honest.
