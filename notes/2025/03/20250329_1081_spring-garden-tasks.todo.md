# Spring garden tasks

Working list for the garden between now and end of May. Some of this carries over from last autumn when I ran out of time.

## Immediate (before mid-April)

- [ ] Turn the compost heap — it's been sitting since October and should be ready on the bottom
- [ ] Clear the raised bed of dead brassica stalks and the root mat underneath
- [ ] Order replacement netting for the fruit cage (the heron got through last summer)
- [ ] Test soil pH in the north bed — the blueberries looked chlorotic last year
- [ ] Plant onion sets: Red Baron and Sturon, 10 cm apart

## Mid-April to May

- [ ] Sow courgettes and squash inside around 20 April
- [ ] Direct sow beetroot once soil hits 10°C consistently
- [ ] Harden off the broad beans that overwintered in the cold frame
- [ ] Mulch the paths with wood chip — the weed pressure through the gravel was a problem last year
- [ ] Fix the lean on the east fence post — it shifted over winter

## Ongoing

- [ ] Water the greenhouse seedlings every other morning
- [ ] Watch for slug damage on the lettuce tray
- [ ] Check the water butt connection — it dripped through December

## Reference

RHS guidance on sowing timing by last frost date: [RHS sowing calendar](https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/sowing-calendar)

Noted last year that direct-sown carrots did better in the south bed than the north, likely drainage. Repeat that placement.

The sourdough cracker recipe I made for the garden lunch last summer used chive flowers from the herb patch — worth growing enough for that again [[20230503_1004]].
