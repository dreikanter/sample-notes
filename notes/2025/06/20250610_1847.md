# Tabletop game notes — current Wingspan campaign

Four sessions into a campaign-style Wingspan playthrough with house rules. Standard Wingspan but we're tracking scores across sessions and have a meta-currency (bonus cards) that carries over between games.

**Session 4 recap**

Went with a prairie/grassland focus — brown "when activated" powers that chain together. Got lucky with an early European Nightjar that gives food each time you activate any bird in grasslands. Combined with three or four other brown-power birds in the same habitat it creates a cascading activation chain.

Final scores: me 87, Keiko 94, Dev 72. Keiko has now won three of four sessions — she consistently builds efficient egg-laying engines in wetlands that I underestimate and then can't catch up with.

**What I've learned**

- Don't spread across all three habitats equally. Specializing in two is more efficient.
- End-of-round goals matter more than I thought. Even one or two points per round from the bonus cards compounds across four rounds.
- Tuck cards (birds that let you tuck other cards for points) are deceptively powerful if you build around them early.
- The card draw economy is tight. Every action you spend drawing is an action you're not placing birds.

**Meta standings after 4 sessions:** Keiko 3 wins, me 1 win, Dev 0 wins.
