# Recipe — roasted aubergine with tahini and pomegranate

This is a summer staple that looks impressive but takes about 35 minutes from start to plate. Serves 2 as a main with bread, 4 as a side.

**Ingredients**

- 2 large aubergines
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- Salt

For the sauce:
- 4 tbsp tahini
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 clove garlic, grated
- 3-4 tbsp cold water
- Salt

To finish:
- Handful of pomegranate seeds
- Small bunch of flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped
- Pinch of sumac
- Toasted pine nuts (optional)

**Method**

Heat oven to 220°C. Cut aubergines lengthwise, score the flesh in a diamond pattern, brush generously with olive oil (aubergines need more than you think). Season with cumin, paprika, salt. Roast cut-side up for 25-30 minutes until the flesh is completely soft and the top is browned and slightly charred at the edges.

While the aubergines roast, whisk tahini with lemon juice and garlic. It will seize up initially — keep adding cold water a tablespoon at a time until it loosens to a pourable consistency. Season with salt.

Plate the aubergines, spoon over the tahini sauce, scatter pomegranate, parsley, and sumac.

**Notes**

The scoring matters — it lets the oil penetrate and allows the flesh to cook through evenly. Don't undersalt.
