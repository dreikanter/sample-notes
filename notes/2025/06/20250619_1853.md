# Notes from a morning of birdwatching

Went to the wetland reserve at Heron's Fen on Thursday morning. Arrived at 6:30am, stayed until 9. First time at this specific reserve though I've walked near it before.

**Confirmed sightings (with confidence)**

- Great crested grebe: two adults, one with a juvenile on its back briefly — classic grebe behavior and a great view through the binoculars
- Sedge warbler: heard and then located in the reeds — the song is a chaotic run of buzzes and whistles
- Marsh harrier: one female hunting over the reed bed, long gliding passes
- Gadwall: a pair on the open water, understated duck that gets overlooked next to the showier mallards
- Grey heron: the usual patient statue on the far bank
- Common tern: two or three, diving for fish in the open channel

**Heard but not seen**

- Cetti's warbler: the explosive call came from thick reedbed, never got eyes on it
- Bittern: possibly. A deep foghorn "boom" that I heard once and couldn't relocate. May have imagined it.

**Conditions**

Still and overcast early, which is actually better than bright sunshine for seeing birds against the sky. Light wind from the west. The reed bed was active — lots of movement in the stems even when I couldn't identify what was causing it.

Planning another visit in July when the waders start passing through.
