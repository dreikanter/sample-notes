# Herb garden — balcony update

The balcony herb situation is more successful this year than last. Last year I tried to grow too many things in pots that were too small, and most of them bolted or got leggy. This year: fewer things, bigger containers, better drainage.

**What's growing**

- Basil: two plants in a 30cm terracotta pot. Growing fast now that it's consistently warm. Pinching the flower buds back regularly to keep it leafy. The smell when you brush against it is one of summer's reliable pleasures.
- Thyme: doing well in a shallow terracotta dish, well-drained, full sun. This is the second year for this plant — it overwintered fine. Looking woody at the base but lots of new growth from the tips.
- Mint: contained in its own pot deliberately, because mint will take over anything it's planted with. Growing vigorously, already provided enough for several rounds of mint tea and a few mojitos.
- Flat-leaf parsley: struggling slightly. It prefers more water than the others but I've been conservative and the lower leaves are yellowing. Going to try weekly deep watering instead of frequent shallow watering.
- Chives: unfussily reliable. They don't do anything dramatic, they're just always there when you need them.

**Failed experiments**

Dill. It bolted within three weeks in the full sun position. Will try a shadier spot next year.

Container herb growing guide: https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/herbs/grow-your-own

The basil is the only one I replace each year. Everything else has potential to be perennial.
