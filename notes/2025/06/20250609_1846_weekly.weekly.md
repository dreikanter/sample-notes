## Observations

The invoice clearance was a good example of how a task that feels large becomes easy once you sit down with it. Three invoices took about 40 minutes total. The resistance before doing it probably cost more energy than the task itself.

Trying to be more deliberate about task switching. When I move from one kind of work to another, I'm starting to do a two-minute cleanup of what I was doing before I switch. Leaves a cleaner handoff for myself when I return.
