# Thai basil chicken (pad kra pao) — home version

This is the dish I make most often when I want something fast, satisfying, and that tastes meaningfully different from the usual rotation. 15 minutes from start to plate if you have everything ready.

**Serves 2**

- 400g chicken thigh, minced or very finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 3-6 bird's eye chilies, depending on preference (don't substitute)
- 1 large handful Thai basil leaves (not Italian basil — the flavor is different)
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 2 eggs (for frying)

**Method**

Mix fish sauce, oyster sauce, dark soy, and sugar in a small bowl. Set aside.

Get a wok or wide pan very hot — hotter than you think. Add oil, then garlic and chili together. Stir-fry for 30 seconds. Add chicken, break it up, and cook over high heat without stirring too much to get some browning. Pour in the sauce. Let it reduce for 30 seconds. Remove from heat. Add Thai basil and toss — it wilts immediately from the residual heat.

Fry the eggs separately in a separate pan in shallow oil — the edges should be crispy.

Serve over rice with a crispy egg on top. The runny yolk breaks into the rice and sauce and is essential.

Reference recipe and variations: https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/pad-kra-pao/
