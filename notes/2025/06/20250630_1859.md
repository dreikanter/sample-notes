# End of June — month notes

The month went quickly, as June always does. Looking back at what happened:

Work was steady rather than eventful. The API documentation project is done and shipped — it ended up being cleaner than I expected, in part because writing the docs forced me to think through the edge cases. Several small bugs emerged from writing clear descriptions of behavior I'd assumed was obvious.

The garden had its best month in two or three years. Tomatoes setting fruit, beans in and germinating, herbs overflowing their pots. We've eaten from the garden almost every day this month — mostly salad and herbs, but that counts. The garlic harvest happened on June 17: twelve heads, smaller than last year due to a dry spring, but usable.

Ran five times, which is the most consistent I've been since February. The evenings were long enough to go out after dinner, which helps enormously with motivation. The route I've settled on is 6km around the park and back — flat, well-lit, not interesting enough to be distracting but not so boring that I dread it.

Reading: Chekhov, bits of a history of fermentation, half of a novel about a lighthouse keeper that I haven't decided whether to finish.

The thing I keep returning to from this month is the solstice observation [[20250622_1855]]. The year's longest breath, held at maximum expansion. I feel like I used June well.
