# Week of November 24

Short week due to the holiday. Four working days, then Thursday off and a slow Friday.

## What got done

- Finished migrating the personal scripts repo to Python 3.12. Tox runs clean except for one deprecation warning in a dependency that I've pinned around for now.
- Had a long call with Marcus about the database sharding proposal. His concern about hotspot keys on the user partition is valid — added a note to revisit consistent hashing.
- Submitted the October expense report (late, as usual).
- Read about 60 pages of *The Dispossessed* [[20251108_1101]] — now at the end and the temporal structure is paying off in ways that weren't obvious earlier.

## What didn't happen

The offsite backup restore test. This keeps sliding. Going to block two hours on the calendar next week with no meeting buffer around it.

Also didn't get to the RFC 9110 reading — I keep overestimating how much reading I can do in a given week.

## Observations

Thanksgiving dinner was simpler than last year — roasted a half-bird instead of a full turkey, which cooked faster and tasted better. The smoked paprika lentils [[20251117_1102]] made a good side dish for the vegetarians.

The week before a long weekend is always fractured in terms of focus. Better to use those days for administrative work rather than pretending deep work will happen.
