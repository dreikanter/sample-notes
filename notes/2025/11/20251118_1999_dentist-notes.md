# Dentist appointment — November 18

Finally went. Six months overdue.

## What happened

Routine cleaning. X-rays (every two years for me). Hygienist spent about 35 minutes. Dentist reviewed the X-rays and spent about 5 minutes with me.

## Results

No cavities — the first time in about three years. The hygienist attributed this to apparently having stopped drinking juice regularly (I just... forgot I used to), which she said is a meaningful change for enamel health.

One "watch area" on the upper left molar. Not a cavity yet; going to check again in six months. She described it as "demineralization at the margin" — essentially early weakening of enamel that might or might not develop further.

**Recommendation:** Use a fluoride rinse twice a week in addition to fluoride toothpaste. Prescription-strength if it's getting worse in six months.

## Notes on the hygienist's technique

She mentioned she was trying a new scaling instrument — piezoelectric ultrasonic scaler. Significantly less uncomfortable than the traditional hand instruments. Finished faster too. Worth asking for by name next time.

## Insurance

Fully covered for the cleaning. X-rays at 80% — my portion was $22.
