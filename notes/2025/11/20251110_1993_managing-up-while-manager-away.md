# Notes on managing up while Thomas is out

Thomas is out until November 21. I'm handling escalations and serving as the point of contact for cross-team questions. Writing down what I'm learning.

## What's working

**Proactive status updates:** I've been sending a brief daily note to Thomas (two sentences max, async) so he can stay oriented without having to ask. He hasn't asked a single clarifying question, which suggests the right level of detail.

**Delegating clearly:** The team knows who to ask for what. I've been explicit about "this is a decision I can make" vs. "this is one Thomas needs to see when he's back." Teams respect clarity about authority levels.

**Not over-deciding:** There's a temptation to make all pending decisions because you have the authority right now. I've caught myself twice about to do this and paused to ask whether the decision was actually time-sensitive or just felt that way.

## What's been hard

A cross-team request came in on Wednesday that required budget approval. I don't have that authority. Sent it to Thomas with a one-paragraph summary; he responded in 6 hours with a decision. But the 6-hour delay frustrated the requesting team. Would have been better to set expectations upfront.

## For Thomas when he's back

Brief written handoff covering:
1. The notification rollout status (now at 25%, going well)
2. C-1 candidate decision (team voted yes; waiting on reference checks)
3. The cross-team budget request resolution
4. Anything that drifted in his absence
