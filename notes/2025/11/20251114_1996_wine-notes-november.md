# Wine notes — November bottles

Tracking bottles opened this month. I'm not a systematic wine person but I've been trying to pay closer attention.

## Bottle 1 — Domaine de Villaine Bourgogne Aligoté 2022

Aubert de Villaine was the co-director of Domaine de la Romanée-Conti; this is his separate estate making wines from what the DRC left out.

Aligoté is the other white grape of Burgundy—less famous than Chardonnay, historically viewed as lesser. This one challenges that. It's lean and tart, with a mineral quality that reminded me of wet limestone. Went with the mushroom risotto (see [[20251107_1992]]) and the pairing was good.

**Price:** ~$28. For Burgundy, a bargain.

## Bottle 2 — Château Musar Rouge 2016, Bekaa Valley, Lebanon

Recommended by someone at the chamber music concert. I'd heard of Musar but never opened a bottle.

This wine is unusual. A blend of Cabernet Sauvignon, Cinsaut, and Carignan, made in a style that seems intentionally oxidative — herbal, gamey, with something almost sherry-like in the nose. It's not for everyone. I found it compelling. It reminded me of descriptions of old-school Southern Rhône, before the style modernized.

Still on the second glass. Not sure yet.

**Price:** ~$40.
