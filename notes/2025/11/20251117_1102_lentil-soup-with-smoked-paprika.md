# Lentil soup with smoked paprika and lemon

Made this on a cold Tuesday when the cupboard was mostly bare. The result was better than expected, so recording it before I forget the ratios.

## Ingredients (serves 4)

- 300g green or brown lentils, rinsed
- 1 large onion, diced roughly
- 4 cloves garlic, sliced
- 2 medium carrots, cut into rounds
- 1 tin (400g) chopped tomatoes
- 1.2L vegetable stock (or water plus a parmesan rind)
- 2 tsp smoked paprika (not sweet — the Spanish kind in the tin)
- 1 tsp cumin, ground
- Juice of 1 lemon, added at the end
- Olive oil, salt, black pepper
- Optional: a handful of spinach stirred in at the last minute

## Method

Soften the onion in a generous pour of olive oil over medium-low heat — 10 minutes until translucent, not browned. Add garlic, carrot, paprika, and cumin; stir for 2 minutes until fragrant. Add lentils, tomatoes, and stock. Bring to a simmer and cook uncovered for 25–30 minutes until lentils are fully soft and some have begun to break down (this thickens the broth naturally).

Taste and adjust salt. Add lemon juice off the heat — it brightens the whole thing considerably. Serve with crusty bread.

## Notes

The parmesan rind trick adds body without making it cheesy; pull it out before serving. Smoked paprika from a tin that's been open for more than 6 months loses most of its potency — buy fresh if the color has faded to orange.

More on lentil varieties and cooking times: https://www.seriouseats.com/lentil-types-guide
