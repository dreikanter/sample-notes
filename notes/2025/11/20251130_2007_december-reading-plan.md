# December reading plan

Last month of the year. The reading pace slows in December—more social commitments, shorter evenings, the will toward lighter material.

## In progress

**Lonesome Dove** (see [[20251124_2003]]): About 600 pages in, probably two weeks remaining. Will definitely finish by year's end. This is the priority.

## Wants to read in December

**A Christmas Carol** — Dickens. This is a tradition I've maintained for four years: read it every December, always faster than expected (~2 hours). Still surprised by how affecting it is.

**Stoner** — John Williams. Everyone who reads this seems to consider it a quiet masterpiece. A novel about an ordinary academic life. About 280 pages; shouldn't take long.

## Probably won't happen

The Warmth of Other Suns is ongoing (see [[20251010_1965]]). I keep picking it up and putting it down not from lack of interest but from the emotional weight. Will carry it into the new year.

## The year in reading

A quick count: I've finished approximately 14 books in 2025. Not the 20 I'd hoped for, but better than 2024 (which was 11). The gap between what I want to read and what I actually read remains large. The solution is probably to read more and plan less, which is advice I've never successfully followed.

Best of the year so far:
1. Lonesome Dove (ongoing)
2. Demon Copperhead
3. Past Lives (film, not book, but it counts in spirit)
