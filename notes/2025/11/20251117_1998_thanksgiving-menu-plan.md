# Thanksgiving menu plan — 2025

Hosting 8 people. One vegetarian, one person avoiding dairy, no other restrictions.

## The bird

14–15 lb. turkey, dry-brined starting Sunday night (see [[20251101_1987]]). Roasting plan: spatchcocked this year. Flatten the bird for even cooking and dramatically shorter roasting time (~90 minutes vs. 3+ hours).

## Menu

**Starters:**
- Crudités with hummus (dairy-free)
- Warm olives with orange and fennel seed
- Cheese plate (acknowledged: not dairy-free, but offset by dairy-free alternatives elsewhere)

**Mains:**
- Dry-brined spatchcocked turkey with pan drippings gravy
- Mushroom Wellington for the vegetarian (ambitious but doable)
- Green beans with toasted almonds and lemon (dairy-free)

**Sides:**
- Mashed potatoes (with butter and cream — not dairy-free, supplemented by)
- Roasted sweet potatoes with maple and cardamom (dairy-free)
- Sautéed kale with garlic (from the garden — probably the last of it)
- Cranberry sauce from scratch (refrigerator for two days, gets better)
- Stuffing with sausage, celery, sage

**Dessert:**
- Apple pie (from the Honeycrisps, frozen after the orchard trip)
- Pumpkin tart

## Timeline

**Sunday:** Brine turkey, make cranberry sauce, prep pie filling
**Wednesday:** Make stuffing, bake pie, prep Wellington components
**Thursday morning:** Spatchcock turkey, bring to room temperature
**Thursday 2 p.m.:** Start roasting
**Thursday 4 p.m.:** Target table time
