# January closing note — the month in brief

The last day of January. Looking back at a month that was fuller than January usually is.

## What happened

The Porto trip [[20250116_1714]] was exactly what I needed — three days of slow attention, good food, a different light. The Douro Valley train journey alone was worth the trip. Coming back was slightly painful in the way that good trips always are.

The running habit re-established itself [[20250111_1709]] — ended the month with twelve runs, up from zero in December. The slow re-start worked: nothing injured, pace improving, most importantly still doing it at the end of the month rather than having burned out in week two.

The Q1 planning [[20250112_1710]] has a shape. The API v2 work moved forward; the documentation debt is being paid down systematically for the first time in a year.

Finished *Pachinko* [[20250114_1712]] and started something lighter. *Housekeeping* [[20250105_1703]] remains the best reading of the new year so far.

The garden [[20250104_1702]] is waiting for February. Snowdrops are up. Garlic is thriving. Seed orders placed.

## The habit check

From [[20241229_1696]]:
- Daily walk: 28/31 days
- Cook from whole ingredients: rough estimate 4 out of 5 dinners
- Consistent sleep: holding at roughly 10:30-11pm, not perfect

## Something I noticed

January, which I usually survive rather than inhabit, was this year inhabited. I'm not sure whether that's because of deliberate intention or because the bar for what counts as inhabiting is lower when you've just had a good December. Both probably.

The [Atlantic essay "The Virtue of Idleness"](https://www.theatlantic.com/magazine/archive/1998/04/the-virtue-of-idleness/377259/) came to mind several times this month. January rewards slowness. Next year, plan for it.
