# Coffee brewing notes

Keeping the variables I care about in one place so I stop re-deriving
them every few weeks.

## V60, single cup

- 15 g coffee, medium-fine grind
- 250 g water at 94°C
- Bloom: 40 g water, swirl, wait 40 s
- Two pours to 250 g, finish around 2:30–3:00

## French press, two cups

- 30 g coffee, coarse grind
- 500 g water at 93°C
- Four-minute steep, one gentle stir at 1:00, decant immediately

For lighter roasts, grind finer and pull the temperature up a degree.
For darker roasts, back off both.

External: [James Hoffmann's V60
technique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1oB1oDrDkHM).
