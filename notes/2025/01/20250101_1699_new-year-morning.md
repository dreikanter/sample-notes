# New Year morning — 1 January 2025

Woke later than planned. Went for a walk at 10am, which is not as austere as going for one at dawn but is better than not going. The streets had the familiar New Year's Day quality — a residue of the night before, a few takeaway boxes, the odd deflated balloon, but also quiet and clean in the morning air.

The river path was more populated than usual — other people with the same idea. Dogs running in the frost-stiffened grass. A family with young children attempting to fly a kite in insufficient wind. One man jogging with the concentrated expression of someone who has decided that this year will be different.

I don't feel particularly different. That's fine and probably honest. Change happens at a pace that doesn't align with calendar events, mostly. But the intention is real and the walk is one small instance of it.

Made a lentil soup from the turkey broth I'd frozen [[20241226_1693]]. Green lentils, onion, garlic, cumin, preserved lemon, coriander. Simple and good. This is what January cooking should be: unpretentious and warming, using things up, starting the month without drama.

The word count from the [new year intentions note [[20241229_1696]]] I keep returning to: "be more present to the things actually happening." The soup. The walk. The cold air on the river. The kite that wouldn't quite fly. These are the things happening.

Read for the evening: started *Housekeeping* by Marilynne Robinson. The [Kirkus review](https://www.kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/a/marilynne-robinson/housekeeping/) compared it to Woolf. After 40 pages I can see why.

Good morning. Good year, hopefully.
