# Portuguese cooking experiments

Inspired by the Portugal trip planning [[20241119_1656]] and [[20241127_1664]], I've been cooking Portuguese-style dishes at home as a kind of preview.

## Caldo Verde (green soup)

The most important Portuguese soup. Simple and perfect.

- 500g Maris Piper or waxy potatoes, diced
- 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves
- Good olive oil (important — this is where the flavour lives)
- 200g kale or cavolo nero, very finely shredded (or use proper Portuguese couve gallega if you can find it)
- 1 chouriço, sliced and browned
- Chicken or vegetable stock, salt, pepper

Soften onion and garlic in olive oil. Add potatoes and stock, simmer until potatoes are very soft — almost falling apart. Blend (fully or partially) until smooth. Add the finely shredded greens and simmer 5 minutes. Serve in bowls with chouriço on top and good bread.

The key: the greens must be cut very fine — almost a chiffonade. Thick pieces are wrong. And the olive oil at the end should be good enough to taste.

## Pastéis de Bacalhau (salt cod cakes)

More involved but worth it. Bought salt cod from a Portuguese deli. Soaked for 48 hours changing the water three times.

- 300g salt cod, soaked
- 400g potato, boiled and mashed
- 1 egg, 1 egg yolk
- Parsley, garlic, white pepper
- No salt — the cod provides it

Flake the cooked cod, combine with mash, egg, herbs. Form into torpedo shapes. Fry in neutral oil until golden. These are exactly what you want: crispy outside, fluffy inside, the slightly funky saltiness of the cod softened by the potato.

The [Leite's Culinaria salt cod cake recipe](https://leitesculinaria.com/2258/recipes-bolinhos-de-bacalhau.html) is a good reference for ratios.
