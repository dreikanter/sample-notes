# Fermentation results — six-week update

Following up on the original batch [[20250520_1816]]. Six weeks in the fridge after a four-day room-temperature ferment.

**Carrots and daikon**

The carrots are excellent. The texture held up well — still crisp enough to have bite. The flavor is clean and tangy, noticeably more complex than fresh-pickled daikon and carrot: there's a depth from the fermentation process that quick pickling doesn't produce. The garlic cloves in the jar have turned a slightly blue-green color, which looks alarming but is a completely harmless reaction between the garlic's enzymes and the acidic brine.

Used them last weekend in a bowl with rice, soft-boiled egg, cucumber, and a drizzle of sesame oil. The tanginess cut through the richness of the egg in exactly the way I hoped.

**What I'd do differently**

The slice thickness was a little inconsistent — some pieces are perfect, a few thicker ones are slightly softer. Will use a mandoline next time for uniformity. Also: the flavor is good but mild. A longer room-temperature ferment (5-6 days instead of 4) would likely produce more pronounced acidity.

**Second batch**

Started a second batch with radishes, fennel, and a small amount of dried chili. The fennel is experimental — not sure how it will behave in fermentation. Four-day room-temperature ferment starting today.
