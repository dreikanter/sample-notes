# Bread baking — back to the basic country loaf

Made bread for the first time since the rye experiment in March (see [[20250307_1756]]). Went back to the baseline country loaf from [[20250201_1720]] to recalibrate.

## What I changed

Dropped hydration from 76% to 73%. The handling difference is significant — much easier to shape without flour everywhere. Will stick to 73% as the standard.

Pushed bulk fermentation to 4.5 hours at 23°C (house is warmer now with spring arriving). The rise was close to 60% rather than the 40-50% I was getting at 76% in winter. More activity.

## The bake

Same Dutch oven, same timing (20 min lid on at 250°C, 25 min lid off). Better ear and bloom than the last few bakes. The scoring was deeper — I sharpened the lame.

## Crumb

Open and glossy. Not gummy at the bottom this time. I think the combination of proper fermentation and the lower hydration made shaping easier and the structure stronger going into the oven.

## Notes

The starter was very active this week (see [[20250423_1794]] for starter notes). Warm weather speeds everything up. Need to calibrate fermentation times to the season — what worked in winter takes less time now.

First loaf I'm genuinely happy with in a few months. The rye experiment was interesting but this is the bread I actually want to eat.
