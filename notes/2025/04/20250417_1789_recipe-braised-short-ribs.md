# Recipe — braised short ribs

Made these for a small dinner party — four people. The long cook time is mostly hands-off, which makes it good for hosting.

## Ingredients

- 1.5kg bone-in short ribs
- 2 medium onions, roughly chopped
- 3 carrots, chopped
- 4 celery stalks, chopped
- 8 garlic cloves, smashed
- 2 cups dry red wine (something you'd drink)
- 2 cups beef stock
- 400g tin crushed tomatoes
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 sprigs rosemary, 4 sprigs thyme, 2 bay leaves

## Method

1. The day before if possible: salt the ribs generously on all sides, refrigerate uncovered overnight. Pat dry before cooking.

2. Brown the ribs in batches in a heavy Dutch oven over high heat — 3-4 minutes per side. The goal is deep mahogany crust, not gray. Remove and set aside.

3. Reduce heat to medium. Add onion, carrot, celery. Cook 8-10 minutes until softened. Add garlic, tomato paste, cook 2 more minutes.

4. Add wine, scrape up the fond, reduce by half (5-7 min). Add stock, tomatoes, and herbs.

5. Return ribs to the pot. The liquid should come about halfway up the ribs. Bring to a simmer, skim any foam.

6. Cover and braise at 160°C for 3-3.5 hours, until the meat pulls from the bone easily but isn't falling apart.

7. Remove ribs. Strain the braising liquid into a saucepan, reduce by a third over high heat, skim fat. Season.

Serve with mashed potato or polenta. Gremolata (lemon zest, parsley, garlic) on top cuts through the richness.
