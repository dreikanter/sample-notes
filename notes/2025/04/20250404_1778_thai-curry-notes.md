# Thai green curry — cooking notes

Made green curry from scratch for the first time (including the paste). Much better than the canned paste version. Time-consuming but worth doing once to understand what's in it.

## Green curry paste (for 4-6 portions)

- 10 fresh green bird's eye chillies (or 6 for less heat)
- 4 stalks lemongrass, tender inner part only, sliced
- 6 kaffir lime leaves, central rib removed, shredded
- 5cm galangal (or ginger — close enough), sliced
- 6 garlic cloves
- 4 shallots
- 1 tsp coriander seeds, toasted
- 1 tsp cumin seeds, toasted
- 1 tsp white pepper
- 1 tsp shrimp paste (or miso for vegan)
- ½ cup fresh coriander with roots if possible
- Zest of 1 kaffir lime (or regular lime if unavailable)

Blitz in food processor, adding a small amount of coconut milk to loosen if needed. The color should be deeply green, almost dark.

## The curry

1. Heat 2 tbsp coconut cream in a wok until it splatters and the oil separates.
2. Add 3 tbsp of the paste (freeze the rest). Fry 2 min until fragrant.
3. Add 400ml coconut milk and 200ml chicken or vegetable stock.
4. Add vegetables: in this version, eggplant cut into chunks, zucchini, baby corn.
5. Add chicken thigh pieces (optional) — simmer 10-12 min.
6. Season with fish sauce (or soy), a small amount of palm or brown sugar, kaffir lime juice.
7. Serve with jasmine rice and fresh Thai basil.
