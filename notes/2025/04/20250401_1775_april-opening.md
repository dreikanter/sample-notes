# April opening — a brief accounting

April 1. No pranks. The month is opening quietly.

Looking back at March: more happened than I credited at the time. The API work advanced. The garden is going. I read three books I actually cared about. Made the roast chicken (see [[20250331_1774]]) twice and it was good both times. The kimchi (see [[20250220_1743]]) lasted almost until end of the month, which surprised me.

What I want from April:

**Work:** Get the API v2 beta to five external users. Ship the first minor version with the bug fixes from the first beta user's reports. The infrastructure migration Terraform work — I want that done or at least staged.

**Garden:** Transplant tomatoes and peppers outside by late April, using cloches for frost protection. Direct-sow beans and cucumbers once we're past last frost (around May 10). Get the trellis built.

**Reading:** *When Breath Becomes Air* — I've been avoiding it for reasons I understand but it's time. Also more Fontaine.

**Personal:** April is when the light really comes back. Use it. Be outside more than in.

**Cooking:** Try a new cuisine or technique each week. March was mostly comfort repetition — roast chicken, pasta, dal. April should have some experiments.

None of this is urgent. It's just a direction.
