# Travel notes — day trip to the coast

Drove to the coast on a Saturday — about 90 minutes. Chose to go on a whim on Friday evening after seeing the forecast was clear and cold.

## What I found

The beach in early April is a different thing from the beach in July. Almost no one else there. The sand is wet and firm because it rained two days ago. The light is horizontal and sharp — the sun low enough that it hits the tops of the waves at an angle that makes everything glitter.

Walked for about two hours along the water's edge. The waves were 4-5 feet, nothing dramatic, but a constant presence. I kept stopping to look at things the tide had left: kelp in long loops, what I thought was a sea star fragment (couldn't confirm), a lot of small coquina shells, a few sand dollars.

The cold was manageable in layers. The wind was steady and kept me from staying too long in any one spot.

Ate lunch from a bag — ham and cheese sandwich, a clementine, some crackers — sitting in the lee of a dune. The meal was excellent in the way that outdoor food often is, regardless of quality.

On the drive back I stopped at a farm stand that was doing early-season asparagus. Bought a bunch. Made it for dinner simply: blanched, olive oil, lemon, flaky salt.

## On the value of going

I'd been indoors more than usual for weeks. This was the right correction. The scale of the ocean and the emptiness of the beach in April did something useful to my sense of proportion.
