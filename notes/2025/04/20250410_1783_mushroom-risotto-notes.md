# Mushroom risotto — cooking notes

Made this on a Thursday evening with the intention of it being simple. It took 45 minutes, which is not simple, but is worth it.

## Ingredients (serves 3)

- 300g Arborio or Carnaroli rice (Carnaroli is more forgiving)
- 250g mixed mushrooms — cremini, oyster, and a handful of dried porcini (rehydrated)
- 1 medium onion, finely diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 150ml dry white wine
- 1.2 liters hot chicken or vegetable stock
- 60g Parmigiano-Reggiano, grated
- 40g cold butter, cubed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, white pepper
- Small bunch of thyme

## Method

Rehydrate the dried porcini in 200ml hot water for 20 minutes. Reserve the liquid (strain through fine sieve — it's full of flavor), chop the porcini.

Sauté fresh mushrooms in a separate pan in batches with high heat — they need to brown, not steam. Season well, add thyme, set aside.

For the risotto: sweat onion in olive oil until soft and translucent (10-12 min, low heat). Add garlic 1 minute. Add rice, toast 2 minutes until edges turn translucent. Add wine, stir until absorbed.

Add the porcini liquid (carefully — stop before the sediment at the bottom). Then add hot stock a ladleful at a time, stirring constantly, allowing each addition to absorb before adding the next. This takes 18-20 minutes.

When rice is al dente, remove from heat. Stir in cold butter (mantecatura) and Parmigiano vigorously — creates the creamy emulsion. Add mushrooms back in. Let sit 2 minutes before serving.
