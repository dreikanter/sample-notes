# Garden transplant planning — April

The seedlings are ready, or nearly so. Tomatoes have their fourth true leaves and have been hardened off in a cold frame for a week. Peppers are smaller but sturdy.

Last frost date historically is around May 10. So transplanting in late April means providing protection. Planning to use:

- **Wall-o-Water cells** for the tomatoes — these can protect down to about -7°C and are reusable. Good investment.
- **Simple cloches** (plastic bottles with bottoms cut off) for the peppers — easier to manage for smaller plants.

## Transplant readiness checklist

- [x] Hardening off started (one week of cold frame exposure, increasing outdoor time)
- [x] Beds prepared — compost worked in, watered
- [ ] Wall-o-water cells filled with water (do this a day before to let them warm up)
- [ ] Stake positions marked for tomatoes (goes in before the plant to avoid root damage)
- [ ] Weather forecast checked — no frost in the 10-day window

## Timing

Targeting April 25-27 for tomatoes. Peppers can follow a week later — they're more cold-sensitive.

## Notes from last year

Planted tomatoes too early last year (April 15) and lost two of four to a late frost even with protection. The Wall-o-Water cells froze solid but the plants survived — just barely. This year waiting until the last week of April.

The beans and cucumbers go in after the frost date — direct sow, no transplanting needed. Targeting May 12-15.
