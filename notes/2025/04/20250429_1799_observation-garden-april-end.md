# Observation — garden, end of April

A week since the tomato transplant (see [[20250420_1791]]). Went out this morning to check.

The Sungold tomatoes have put out new growth — a visible inch of stem above where they were when they went in, and new leaves unfurling. This is the sign that transplant shock has passed and the roots have begun to establish. Brandywine is slightly behind but showing the same signs.

The Wall-o-Water cells are doing their job. Night temperatures dropped to 4°C on Wednesday and the plants are fine. The water-filled ribs act as thermal mass, staying warm through the night.

Shishito peppers are alive but not growing much yet — normal for early in the season. Peppers don't really accelerate until nighttime temperatures are consistently above 15°C.

In bed 2: the salad mix is now thick enough to thin. Did that this morning, pulling seedlings to leave about 3-4 inches between plants. Ate the thinnings as a snack while working — the small leaves at this stage are tender and mildly peppery.

The spinach is the most dramatic change. Four weeks from sowing and it's now 8-10cm with proper broad leaves. Will start harvesting outer leaves in another week.

Bed 3 still has no trellis. The beans need to go in soon (targeting May 12). Have about two weeks to get the structure in place. No excuse.

The garden in late April feels different from March — things are actually happening, not just potential happening.
