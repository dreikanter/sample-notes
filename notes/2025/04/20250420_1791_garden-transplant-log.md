# Garden — transplant day log

Transplant day. Overcast but dry, which is ideal — no direct sun stress on newly moved plants.

## What went in

**Bed 1:**
- 3 tomatoes: 2 Sungold (vigorous, good root mass), 1 Brandywine (slightly smaller but healthy)
- 2 Shishito pepper plants at the east end (these stayed in pots longer than planned)
- Basil seeds sown at the base of each tomato — will germinate once it warms more

**Wall-o-water cells** installed per the plan from [[20250412_1785]]. Each cell filled with water two days ago so they've warmed up. The ribs hold the plants in a teepee of water that moderates temperature overnight.

**Anaheim peppers:** The replanted cells from February never germinated reliably. Have two plants total instead of four. Placed them in Bed 1 in the sheltered corner near the fence.

## Bed 2

Direct-sow crops (spinach, arugula, beets) from [[20250313_1761]] are now 4-6cm and looking healthy. Thinned the spinach today — hard to do but necessary. Left seedlings 10cm apart.

The salad mix I sowed two weeks ago is showing good germination. Will thin this week.

## Bed 3

Not ready yet. The trellis for beans needs to be built. Targeting next weekend.

## Weather

Forecast shows no frost in the next 10 days, which is the critical window. If a surprise frost hits before May 10 the Wall-o-Waters should protect.
