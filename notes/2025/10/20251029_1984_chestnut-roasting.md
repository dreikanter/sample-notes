# Roasting chestnuts — method and notes

Found fresh chestnuts at the Italian grocery this week. Last chance of the season, probably—they come in from October through early December in this region.

## Preparation

Score the flat side of each chestnut with an X before roasting. This is non-negotiable: an unscored chestnut will explode in the oven (I've tested this; it's exactly as dramatic as it sounds). The score also helps with peeling.

Soak the scored chestnuts in water for 15–30 minutes before roasting. This steams them from the inside and makes them easier to peel.

## Oven method

400°F (200°C). Spread chestnuts flat-side up on a baking sheet. Roast 20–25 minutes until the shell has pulled back from the score and the flesh inside is soft when squeezed with a kitchen towel.

Peel while hot. The inner papery skin (the pellicle) comes off more easily when the chestnut is warm.

## Result

Sweet, floury, slightly sticky texture. Not at all like any other nut. The flavor has a mild caramel quality when the chestnuts are fresh.

## Uses

- Eaten plain as a snack
- Added to stuffing for turkey or squash
- Mont Blanc (chestnut cream dessert)—expensive to make well but extraordinary
- Mixed into pasta with butter and sage

The [chestnut growers association](https://www.chestnutgrowers.org/) has regional sourcing information if you want to buy in bulk or directly.
