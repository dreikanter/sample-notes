# French apple cake — recipe and notes

Made with the Honeycrisp apples from the orchard trip (see [[20251003_1958]]). The French version is more apple than cake—which is exactly what I want.

## Ingredients

- 3 large apples (about 600g), peeled, cored, diced small
- 3/4 cup (95g) all-purpose flour
- 3/4 tsp baking powder
- Pinch of salt
- 2/3 cup (130g) sugar, plus 1 tbsp for top
- 2 large eggs
- 3 tbsp dark rum (or apple brandy — much better)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 8 tbsp (113g) unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled

## Method

Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Butter a 9-inch round cake pan.

Whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt. In a large bowl, whisk eggs, sugar, rum, and vanilla. Add the flour mixture, then the melted butter, and stir until smooth. Fold in the diced apples — the batter will be almost more apple than batter, which is correct.

Pour into the prepared pan. Sprinkle top with the 1 tbsp sugar.

Bake 55–65 minutes until deeply golden on top and a tester comes out clean. Let cool in the pan 15 minutes before unmolding.

## Notes

- Apple brandy (Calvados) is genuinely better than rum here
- The cake is best the day it's made; the next day it's fine but the top softens
- Dicing the apples small (1 cm) is important — large pieces don't integrate as well

Reference: [David Lebovitz's version](https://www.davidlebovitz.com/french-apple-cake-recipe/) is the one I started from.
