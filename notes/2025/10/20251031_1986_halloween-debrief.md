# Halloween debrief — October 31

As predicted: the apartment complex group arrived at 7:12 p.m. The bee baby, now toddling, was a crab. An improvement.

**Candy distributed:** approximately 73 pieces. Had 15 left over, which I will now be eating. The lesson is not learned.

## Costume observations

Impressive variety this year. Counted at least three Spider-Men (different iterations), one person in an extremely good Totoro suit, a group of six who came as a traffic light and various road signs, and one adult accompanying their kid in a full 18th-century footman costume, who appeared to be taking it entirely seriously.

The three teenagers who showed up at 8:30 p.m. without costumes but saying "trick or treat" very quietly—candy given. The transaction is the transaction.

## Pumpkins

The traditional jack-o-lantern held up well. The geometric one collapsed slightly—the thin walls between the triangles didn't have enough support. Next year: simpler pattern or thicker walls.

## Weather

Cold but dry. Upper 40s°F as forecast. A lot of kids in costumes with coats barely fitting over them.

## Notes for next year

- Buy more candy (add 25 pieces to the original calculation)
- Consider having a secondary bowl for the 8+ p.m. latecomers
- The porch light left on too long last year—this year turned it off at 8:30, which was the right call

Reference: [History of Halloween traditions](https://www.history.com/topics/halloween/history-of-halloween) is a decent overview if you end up explaining it to someone.
