# Halloween preparations — notes

We get a lot of trick-or-treaters. Last year: approximately 80 kids, which required three resupply trips to the bowl from the backup bag.

## Candy strategy

Bought two large bags early this year to avoid the end-of-October inventory panic:
- Mixed bag with Reese's, Kit Kats, and Snickers (80 pieces)
- Bag of assorted gummies for variety (60 pieces)

The lesson from last year: don't buy anything you'd be happy eating yourself if there's leftovers, because then you eat the leftovers.

## Pumpkins

Two medium pumpkins purchased from the market. Plan to carve this weekend (Oct 25–26).

Design ideas: One traditional jack-o-lantern face (the kid next door requested this specifically). One with a geometric pattern—I've been looking at templates from [Pumpkin Masters](https://www.pumpkinmasters.com/carving-guides/) for patterns that are actually achievable.

## Weather forecast for Oct 31

Looking moderate—upper 40s°F (8–9°C) in the evening. Kids will be in coats over costumes. The window for turning on the porch light is 6–8 p.m. typically.

## Notes on last year

The kids from the apartment complex two blocks over arrive in a large group around 7:15—prep accordingly. The baby in the bee costume is now walking and will be even better this year.

The house across the street does elaborate decorations; we do minimal decorations but good candy. This is the correct division of labor.
