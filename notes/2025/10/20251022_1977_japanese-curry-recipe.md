# Japanese curry — from-scratch method

I've been using packaged curry roux (Golden Curry) for years. Tried making it from scratch and there's a meaningful difference.

## Ingredients (serves 4)

**For the curry powder:**
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1.5 tsp coriander
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 0.5 tsp garam masala
- 0.5 tsp cardamom
- 0.25 tsp cayenne

**For the curry:**
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp ginger, grated
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- The curry powder above
- 800ml chicken or vegetable stock
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 2 medium potatoes, diced
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 300g chicken thigh (or tofu), diced

## Method

Caramelize the onion slowly over medium-low heat in butter—20 minutes minimum. Don't rush this step. Add garlic and ginger, cook 2 minutes. Add flour and curry powder, stir constantly for 3 minutes to cook the flour.

Gradually whisk in stock, scraping the bottom. Add soy sauce, honey, potatoes, carrots, and protein. Simmer 25–30 minutes until vegetables are tender and sauce has thickened.

Serve over Japanese short-grain rice.

## The key difference from packaged

The caramelized onion base and the from-scratch roux (fat + flour) give a depth the packaged version can't match. Also adjustable heat level.

Reference: [Just One Cookbook](https://www.justonecookbook.com/japanese-curry/) for more variations and the history of curry in Japan.
