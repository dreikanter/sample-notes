# Roasted pumpkin soup with toasted pepitas

Not a canned pumpkin recipe. Used a small sugar pumpkin (about 1.4 kg) from the farmers market.

## Ingredients (serves 4)

- 1 small sugar pumpkin (~1.4 kg), halved and seeded
- 1 large onion, quartered
- 4 garlic cloves, unpeeled
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 750ml vegetable stock
- 1 can (400ml) coconut milk
- 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp coriander, pinch cayenne
- Salt, pepper
- Pepitas and good olive oil to serve

## Method

Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Place pumpkin halves cut-side down on a baking sheet with the onion quarters and garlic. Drizzle with olive oil, roast 40–45 minutes until completely tender and slightly caramelized at the edges.

Scoop pumpkin flesh from skin. Squeeze garlic from skins. Add all roasted vegetables to a pot with stock, coconut milk, and spices. Blend until smooth—an immersion blender works; a countertop blender gives a silkier result.

Bring to a simmer, season well. The coconut milk should be present but not dominant.

Toast pepitas in a dry pan until they pop and color slightly. Salt immediately.

Serve with a swirl of olive oil, a spoonful of pepitas, and optional chili flakes.

## Notes

Roasting the pumpkin and onion rather than sautéing produces a deeper, slightly smoky base that makes the soup taste more complex. Worth the extra 40 minutes.

Reference: [Yotam Ottolenghi's approach to winter squash soups](https://ottolenghi.co.uk/recipes/) for variations with tahini.
