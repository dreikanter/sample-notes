# Applesauce — method and notes

Made with Honeycrisp from the orchard trip (see [[20251003_1958]]). About 2 kg of apples.

## Method

Didn't peel. Honeycrisp skins are thin enough that running through a food mill after cooking eliminates them cleanly, and the skins add pectin and color.

**Process:**
1. Core and quarter the apples — no need to peel
2. Put in a large pot with 3 tbsp water and a cinnamon stick
3. Cook over medium-low, covered, for about 25 minutes until completely soft
4. Pass through the fine disk of the food mill

The food mill does the work of separating skin and seeds simultaneously. If you don't have a food mill, peel the apples first and mash with a potato masher for chunky sauce, or blend for smooth.

**Result:** About 1.4 liters of sauce. Pale pink color from the skins—not the beige of store-bought. Sweet but tart. I added no sugar because Honeycrisp is already quite sweet.

## Storage

- Refrigerator: up to 10 days in a sealed jar
- Freezer: 6 months; freeze in 250ml portions for easy use

## Variations

- Add a tablespoon of fresh ginger with the cinnamon
- Spike with a tablespoon of bourbon after cooking (adults-only applesauce)
- Add cardamom and a touch of orange zest for something more complex
