# Garlic planted — October 7

Got the garlic in earlier than planned (see [[20250920_1943]]). The forecast showed a warm dry window closing by the weekend, so moved it up to Tuesday.

## What happened

Prepped beds 2 and 4 Monday evening: pulled the last of the spent plants, worked in compost, raked level. Checked pH in bed 2 with test kit: 6.4, good for alliums.

Planted Tuesday morning, about 90 minutes total.

**Bed 2 (German Red, hardneck Rocambole):**
- 4 rows, 6" spacing, 2" deep
- 42 cloves planted from the ordered bulbs
- Broke bulbs the night before and sorted; discarded 3 undersized cloves

**Bed 4, partial (Chesnok Red, Purple Stripe):**
- 3 rows, same spacing
- 26 cloves planted

**Total: 68 cloves planted.**

After planting: covered with straw mulch, approximately 4 inches. Watered in well.

## Observations

The German Red cloves were noticeably larger and more pungent—even broken into cloves, the aroma was strong. The Chesnok Red is milder and the bulbs are more tightly wrapped, making them slightly harder to separate cleanly.

The soil in bed 2 was in better shape than expected after the compost addition: loose, dark, and moisture-retentive despite the dry August.

## Next step

Check for shoots in spring, likely March. No further intervention needed until then except for pulling back mulch if there's a late frost after early emergence.

Harvest target: late June 2026. See the plan at [[20250920_1943]].
