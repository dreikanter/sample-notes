# Spiced brown butter cookies

Made a batch as gifts and another to keep. Developed from a shortbread base with browned butter and winter spices.

## Ingredients (makes ~40 cookies)

- 225g (2 sticks) unsalted butter
- 150g granulated sugar
- 50g powdered sugar, sifted
- 1 large egg yolk
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 280g all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- ½ tsp cardamom
- ¼ tsp ginger
- ¼ tsp black pepper (unexpected but correct)
- ½ tsp salt
- Flaky salt for finishing

## Method

**Brown the butter:** Melt butter in a light-colored pan over medium heat, stirring frequently. It will foam, then the foam will subside and the milk solids will turn brown and smell nutty—about 8 minutes. Pour into a bowl and refrigerate until solid (about 2 hours) or freeze for 45 minutes.

Beat the firmed brown butter with both sugars until combined. Add egg yolk and vanilla. Mix in flour, spices, and salt until a dough forms.

Roll into two logs, about 4 cm diameter, wrap in parchment, refrigerate 2 hours minimum.

Slice into 8mm rounds, place on parchment-lined baking sheet. Sprinkle with flaky salt.

Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12–14 minutes until edges are just golden.

## Notes

The black pepper is the secret ingredient—it rounds the spice blend in a way that's detectable but not identifiable. Multiple people asked "what's in these" without landing on pepper.

Reference: [Bravetart on shortbread technique](https://www.seriouseats.com/extra-buttery-shortbread-cookie-recipe) for the foundational method.
