# Sourdough hydration notes

Bake log for December 29. Third attempt at 80% hydration after two gummy failures.

**Formula**

- 450g bread flour
- 360g water (80%)
- 100g active starter (100% hydration)
- 9g salt

**Process**

Fed starter the night before, used at peak (domed, doubled, passes float test). Autolyse 45 minutes — flour and water only, no starter, no salt. Then added starter, incorporated by hand, rested 30 minutes, then added salt dissolved in a tablespoon of reserved water.

Bulk fermentation: 4.5 hours at roughly 76°F kitchen. Four sets of stretch-and-folds, 30 minutes apart, then left alone. Dough was extensible but not slack at the end — this is the sign I was watching for, the thing that was missing in the previous two attempts.

Shaped, placed in banneton, cold retard 14 hours in the fridge. Baked in Dutch oven: 500°F covered 20 minutes, 450°F uncovered 25 minutes.

**Result**

Open crumb, good ear, sour but not aggressively so. The crust crackled when I tapped it. First successful 80% loaf.

The key variable was the starter vigor — previous attempts used starter that had peaked and started to fall. Timing matters more than I acknowledged.
