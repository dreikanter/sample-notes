# Year-end reading log

A rough accounting of books I finished in 2025, in no particular order. I track these in a plain text file and compile at year end.

**Fiction**

- *James* by Percival Everett — Huck Finn retold from Jim's perspective, and what a perspective it is. The linguistic code-switching is clever and the moral weight is serious. Best novel I read this year.
- *Prophet Song* by Paul Lynch — Booker Prize winner. Dystopian Ireland through one family's slow catastrophe. Exhausting and necessary.
- *The Women* by Kristin Hannah — Crowd-pleaser, well-researched Vietnam-era nurses, occasionally over-explains its themes.

**Nonfiction**

- *Nexus* by Yuval Noah Harari — Sweeping history of information networks. Less sharp than *Sapiens* but more urgent.
- *On Grief and Grieving* by Kübler-Ross & Kessler — Re-read. Still the most honest thing written on the subject.
- *Four Thousand Weeks* by Oliver Burkeman — Time management book that argues against time management. Genuinely changed how I plan my weeks.

**Technical**

- *A Philosophy of Software Design* by John Ousterhout — Short, strong opinions, most of them correct. See also [[20260103_2030]] for my notes on chapter 4.

Total: 23 books finished. Goal was 24. Close enough.
