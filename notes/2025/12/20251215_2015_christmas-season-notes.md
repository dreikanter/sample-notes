# Christmas season notes — mid-December

Orders placed. Cookies made and distributed. Cards written but not mailed yet. The usual December compression of things that feel urgent and things that are actually important.

## What December has been

The coffee advent calendar continues (see [[20251201_2008]]). Day 15 was a Burundi natural process, heavy and fruity like the Ethiopian on day 2 but with something fermented underneath—I would have said "winey" and checked to see if that was accurate; it is apparently a standard descriptor. Learning to articulate what I taste in coffee is slower than I expected.

The notes CLI has a working `search` command now (see [[20251212_2014]]). The output formatting still needs work but I've been using the tool daily for actual notes and it's already changing how I take them—knowing I can find things changes what I write.

Lonesome Dove is finished (see [[20251209_2012]]). Currently reading A Christmas Carol for the fourth consecutive December, which remains one of the more efficient uses of two hours I know of.

## Christmas Eve plan

Small dinner, just us and two friends who don't have family nearby. The menu is deliberately manageable: beef tenderloin (which sounds expensive but is actually easy), roasted vegetables, bread I'll bake Christmas morning.

The tree is up. We have the kind of ornaments accumulated over a long time—some nice, some absurd, all meaningful.

## One thing I want to notice

Every December I'm in a hurry to get through it and into January, which I then spend not being in as much of a hurry about anything. The thing is, December is actually here right now, and it's good. The light through the tree. The smell of the cookies. The cold walk home from anywhere.
