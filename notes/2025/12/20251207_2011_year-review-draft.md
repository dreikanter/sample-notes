# Year in review — draft notes for 2025

Mid-December draft. The year isn't over; will revise after the 31st.

## Work

Shipped the notification preferences project—six months of design, two months of development, one month of careful rollout. Learned things about feature flag patterns that may generalize. The blog post is written and waiting for Thomas to approve.

Hired one engineer (C-1 came through reference checks, starting January 12). The second role is still open; carried into 2026.

Two significant incidents; neither was catastrophic; both had good post-mortems. The deployment reliability project improved our response time but we haven't eliminated the root causes yet.

## Reading

14 books, maybe 15 if Lonesome Dove finishes before January. Best three: Lonesome Dove, Demon Copperhead, The Dispossessed.

## Garden

The garlic went in. The fig survived two hard frosts under its burlap. The sourdough baking improved measurably across four attempts.

## Running

Roughly 580 km for the year. Down from a goal of 700, up from last year's 520. Moving in the right direction.
