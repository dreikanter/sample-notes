# Year-end reflections, 2025

Rough pass before the proper write-up.

## What worked

- Weekly reviews, more or less consistent after April. See the
  earliest one that stuck: [[20231211_1006]].
- Switching to a paper planner for the short-term list and keeping
  this notes store for anything with a half-life longer than a week.
- Cooking more from the [sourdough log][s] and less from delivery.

## What didn't

- The reading list grew faster than I closed it out. Karamazov
  ([[20250508_1011]]) is still in progress.
- Running tapered off in the autumn and never restarted.

## For next year

- Pick one language learning track and stick to it for the full year
  instead of bouncing.
- Fewer, longer trips.
