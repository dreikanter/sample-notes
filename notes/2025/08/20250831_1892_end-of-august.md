# End of August — month review

August was good. The kind of month where things that had been in progress concluded or advanced, and new things started without taking over.

**Work**

The performance investigation [[20250827_1890]] was the most satisfying work project — a concrete problem with a measurable fix. The rate limiting PR merged cleanly, the API docs are done. Two solid deliverables.

**Learning**

The Kleppmann transactions chapter [[20250805_1879]] has stayed with me. I find myself thinking about isolation levels when I write database-touching code now, which is the test of whether reading something was actually useful.

The Rust project [[20250819_1886]] is still in progress. The file watcher works end-to-end, filtering is done, I'm polishing the output format. Might have something shareable next month.

**Nature and outdoors**

The bittern [[20250829_1891]] was the highlight of the year so far for birdwatching. Also: wader variety at Heron's Fen has been exceptional — probably 12 species over the month.

The garden is winding down gracefully. The tomatoes are slowing, the basil will be harvested in the next week before it starts to decline, the beans have had their last picking. First signs of autumn beginning: a few leaves yellowing on the ornamental crab apple, cooler nights.

**Reading**

Ministry for the Future, finished. Started a book about salt as historical commodity (Kurlansky's *Salt*) — lightweight and enjoyable.

**The solstice note [[20250622_1855]] was right** — summer had its maximum and then began its exhale. Still, a full August.
