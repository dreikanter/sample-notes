# Pesto for freezing — proportions and method

This is the recipe I use when the basil is producing more than I can use fresh. Makes enough for about 20 ice cube portions, each enough for two servings of pasta.

**Ingredients**

- 200g fresh basil leaves (large stems removed)
- 100g good Parmesan, finely grated
- 80g pine nuts, lightly toasted
- 2 cloves garlic
- 200ml extra virgin olive oil (approximately — add to consistency)
- ½ tsp salt

**Method**

Toast the pine nuts in a dry pan over medium heat, shaking frequently, until golden. Watch them — they go from pale to burned quickly. Cool completely.

In a food processor: pulse garlic with salt first to get it finely minced. Add pine nuts, pulse until coarsely ground. Add basil, pulse. Add Parmesan, pulse. With the processor running, pour in the oil until you reach your preferred consistency — I like it thick enough to hold on a spoon but loose enough to stir through pasta without clumping.

Taste for salt. Parmesan is salty; the amount needed varies.

**For freezing**

Pour into ice cube trays. The key step: lay a small piece of cling film directly on the surface of each cube before freezing. This limits oxidation and keeps the color green. Once frozen, pop out and store in zip-lock bags. Use within six months.

When using from frozen: drop a cube into hot pasta directly, add a splash of pasta water, stir.
