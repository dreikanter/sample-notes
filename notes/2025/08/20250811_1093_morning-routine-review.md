# Morning routine review

Set up a structured morning block in February after reading about implementation intentions (the research, not the productivity-blog version). Six months in, here's what held and what didn't.

## What held

**7:00 AM start, no screen for the first 30 minutes.** This one stuck completely. I use the time for coffee and either reading or sitting outside. The no-screen constraint is the load-bearing piece — without it, the 30 minutes evaporates into news and messages.

**Exercise before 8:30 AM.** Held about 4 days out of 5. The 1-day failure rate is usually travel or a very early call. Not concerned about this.

**Weekly review on Sunday morning.** Surprisingly durable. The format I settled on: 15 minutes with a notebook, three questions: what worked, what I want to carry into next week, anything to close out.

## What didn't hold

**Journaling every day.** Dropped to 3x a week by March, then to roughly when-I-feel-like-it by May. Not going to force it back.

**Cold water finish to shower.** Lasted three weeks. Abandoned without regret.

## What I'd change

The exercise slot could move to 7:30 instead of flexible-before-8:30. The flexibility is a small daily decision tax.
