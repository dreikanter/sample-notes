# May opening — notes

May 1. The garden is in. The API is nearly out. The trees are fully leafed. Winter feels genuinely past.

Took a walk this morning through the neighborhood specifically to notice what was in bloom. Lilacs are starting — still tight buds but the fragrance is already there on the warmest exposures. The apple trees in the community orchard are in full bloom: white flowers, bees everywhere. Wisteria on one fence I walk past is climbing aggressively.

The quality of light in May is different from April in ways I can feel but find hard to articulate. Longer shadows in the morning, a greenness to midday light coming through full leaves that wasn't present when the canopy was bare. The temperature is warm enough to sit outside comfortably by mid-afternoon.

**Work:** Public API launch is targeting May 15. The onboarding redesign is ready for final review. Error messages improved. Still one open question on the sandbox environment — we're doing a lightweight version rather than a full deployment, which I think is the right trade-off for launch.

**Garden:** The next two weeks are for beans and cucumbers. Trellis was finally built on Saturday — took 90 minutes, bamboo poles and twine, simple.

**Reading:** Looking forward to *The Art of PostgreSQL* being my May technical book. For pleasure reading, trying to find something that isn't about grief or terminal illness. March and April skewed heavy.
