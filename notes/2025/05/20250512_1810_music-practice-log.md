# Music practice log — May

Returned to the piano this week after a three-month gap. The gap happens every year around winter — something about the cold and indoor focus makes me pick it up; something about spring makes me set it down.

## What I'm working on

**Bach, Well-Tempered Clavier Book I, Prelude in C major:** The one everyone learns, but I've never actually played it cleanly at tempo. Working through it hands-separately at 60% speed, then hands together. The repeated arpeggios are deceptive — easy to play sloppily, hard to play evenly.

**Satie, Gymnopédie No. 1:** The tempo is the challenge. The indication is "Lent et douloureux" (slow and sorrowful). My natural instinct is to rush, which completely destroys the piece. Practicing with a metronome at 50 bpm, then turning it off and playing from feel, then comparing.

**Sight-reading:** Spending 15 minutes per session on something unfamiliar. Currently working through simplified arrangements of Chopin preludes — not the real thing but good for reading rhythmically complex material.

## Session structure

30-45 minutes per session, 4x/week:
- 5 min: warm-up scales and Hanon exercises
- 15 min: Bach
- 10 min: Satie
- 10-15 min: sight-reading

## Notes

The Bach is coming back faster than expected. The muscle memory is still partially there from when I worked on it two years ago. The Satie is slower to recover because it requires a quality of attention rather than technique.
