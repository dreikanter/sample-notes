# Re-reading Dostoevsky

Started *The Brothers Karamazov* again, this time the Pevear and
Volokhonsky translation. The opening is slower than I remembered and
the narrator's voice is odder — chattier, more uncertain about his
own story.

The three brothers as three stances: the sensual Dmitri, the
intellectual Ivan, the faithful Alyosha. A bit too clean to take
literally, but useful as scaffolding for a first read.

Going to take this one slowly. Maybe fifty pages a week.
