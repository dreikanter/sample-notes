# Meeting notes — product roadmap sync, May 26

Attendees: four from product, two from engineering, one from design. Call ran 55 minutes.

There's a divergence somewhere between the Figma file and the implementation ticket. Action item: design to share the final Figma link, engineering to create a ticket for the alignment fix before the next sprint planning.
