# Journal — the day before the day before launch

May 9. Six days until public launch. The internal dry run is tomorrow.

The checklist (see [[20250507_1806]]) is mostly done. The docs review came back today with three real issues: one broken link, one section where the error handling was described incorrectly, one endpoint where the documented parameter name differed from the actual implementation. All three fixed in under an hour, which is either reassuring (small issues) or a sign we got lucky (could have been worse).

The status page is up. Support channel announced to the beta users.

How I actually feel: the anxiety is lower than expected. This might be because we did the work, or because I've miscalibrated the stakes, or because I'm saving the anxiety for next week when there's something to be anxious about. Not sure which.

The beta users have been mostly positive. Two of the five have said they're planning to use v2 in production immediately at launch. One is staying on v1 until June (their team doesn't have bandwidth for the migration before then, which is fine — that's why we set the deprecation timeline where we set it).

Priya is more nervous than me, which is also reassuring — her worry is professionally calibrated and mine might not be.

Made a simple dinner: the spring pasta (see [[20250503_1803]]) with the first peas from the garden. Not the garden peas — those won't be ready until July — but farmer's market ones. Good.

Tomorrow: dry run. Then the weekend. Then Tuesday through Thursday, monitoring and prep. Then Thursday night, the last check. Then Friday morning.
