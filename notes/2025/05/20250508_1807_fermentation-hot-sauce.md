# Fermentation — lacto-fermented hot sauce

Made a hot sauce batch after the previous kimchi batch worked out well. Lacto-fermentation adds depth that cooked hot sauces don't have.

## Ingredients

- 400g fresh red chillies (mixed — some mild, some hot; proportion to taste)
- 6 garlic cloves
- 1 small carrot (adds body and mild sweetness)
- 2% salt brine (20g salt per liter of water)
- Optional: half a red onion, a few sprigs of oregano

## Method

**Fermentation phase:**

Wash everything. Rough chop chillies (wear gloves for anything hotter than jalapeño). Pack into a clean jar with garlic, carrot, onion.

Make brine at 2% salt by weight. Pour over vegetables until everything is submerged. Use a ziplock bag filled with brine as a weight to keep vegetables under the surface.

Cover with a cloth secured with a rubber band. Leave at room temperature (20-24°C) for 5-10 days. Taste daily starting day 3. Bubbling is good — that's CO2 from fermentation.

**Finishing:**

When the flavor is tangy and bright (around day 7 for me in current temperatures), blend everything together with some of the brine. Pass through a fine sieve if you want a smoother sauce. Taste for salt.

Bottle and refrigerate. The fermentation stops in the fridge; it will keep for months.

## Notes

This batch with mixed chillies came out mild enough to use as a table sauce but with a good depth of flavor from the fermentation. The carrot is subtle but rounds out the acidity.

For hotter sauce: use exclusively bird's eye or scotch bonnet chillies, reduce or eliminate the carrot.
