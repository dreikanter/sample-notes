# Garden — first proper spinach harvest

Cut the first real harvest of spinach today — around 300g of outer leaves from the plants in bed 2. I'd been taking small handfuls for salads since May 10 (see [[20250510_1809]]), but today was the first proper cut.

The leaves are in good condition: dark green, flat, no pest damage. The weather has been cool and wet enough that there's been no sign of bolting yet. Bolting becomes a risk when daytime temperatures consistently exceed 24°C, which we haven't hit yet.

**What I made:**

Spinach and white bean soup. Simple:

- Sweat onion and garlic in olive oil.
- Add 800ml of the vegetable stock from the freezer (see [[20250325_1770]]).
- Add a drained tin of cannellini beans.
- Simmer 10 minutes.
- Wilt the fresh spinach in for the last 2 minutes.
- Season with salt, pepper, lemon juice.
- Finish with good olive oil and Parmigiano.

The fresh spinach has a sweetness and depth that bagged supermarket spinach doesn't approach. This is the argument for growing your own.
