# Album review — Blue Rev by Alvvays

Alvvays make music that sounds like being pleasantly overwhelmed — dense, melodic, guitar-driven, with Molly Rankin's voice floating somewhere above the noise. *Blue Rev* is their third album and it consolidates what they've been building toward on the previous two.

The shoegaze influence is more overt here than on *Antisocialites*. The guitars are louder, more distorted, occasionally enormous. But the songs underneath are still pop songs with clear hooks — "Belinda Says" is the obvious standout, three minutes of propulsive melody that's been in my head on and off since I first heard it.

The sequencing is good. The album starts fast and noisy and then slows in the middle third before building back up. It doesn't overstay its welcome at 36 minutes.

If there's a complaint: some of the middle tracks blur together. The production is so consistent that it starts to feel like a long mood rather than individual songs. "Many Mirrors" and "Tile by Tile" are both fine songs but I struggle to distinguish them in memory.

That said, the highs are real highs. "Very Online Guy" is funnier than it has any right to be. The closing track ("Bored in Bristol") ends the album on a note that's almost touching.

Full review at Pitchfork: https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/alvvays-blue-rev/

Rating: 4/5. Best listened to loud, in one sitting.
