# Lentil soup with smoked paprika — recipe notes

Made this on a cold Thursday evening and it was exactly right for the weather. Serves 4 generously.

**Ingredients**

- 300g green or brown lentils, rinsed
- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 medium carrots, diced
- 2 stalks celery, diced
- 1 can (400g) crushed tomatoes
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1.2L vegetable or chicken stock
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper, lemon juice to finish
- Fresh parsley or cilantro

**Method**

Sweat the onion in olive oil over medium heat for 8 minutes. Add carrot and celery, cook 5 more minutes. Add garlic and all the dry spices, stir for 1 minute until fragrant. Add tomatoes, cook 3 minutes to concentrate. Add lentils and stock, bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer for 25 minutes until lentils are completely soft.

Adjust consistency — I like it thick but if you want it soupier, add water. Finish with a generous squeeze of lemon, taste for salt, and serve with crusty bread.

**Notes**

The smoked paprika is doing most of the heavy lifting here flavor-wise. Don't skip it or substitute sweet paprika — the smokiness is the point. A swirl of yogurt on top works well. Keeps in the fridge for 4 days and the flavor improves.
