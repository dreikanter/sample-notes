# Recipe — spring vegetable pasta

Made this with the first asparagus from the coast trip store (see [[20250407_1780]]) and peas from the market. One of those dishes where the ingredients do the work — no elaborate technique.

## Ingredients (serves 2)

- 200g linguine or tagliatelle
- 1 bunch asparagus (thin spears work best), cut into 3cm pieces, tips kept whole
- 1 cup fresh or frozen peas
- 2 shallots, thinly sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced
- 60ml white wine
- 60ml heavy cream (optional — can omit for a lighter dish)
- 30g Parmigiano-Reggiano
- Handful of fresh mint leaves, torn
- Lemon zest and juice (half lemon)
- Olive oil, salt, pepper

## Method

1. Blanch asparagus pieces in heavily salted boiling water for 90 seconds, add peas for 30 more seconds, drain. Reserve the water and use it to cook the pasta.

2. In a wide pan, warm olive oil over medium. Add shallots, cook until soft (5 min). Add garlic, 2 min more. Add asparagus and peas back to the pan.

3. Add wine, let it reduce by half. Add cream if using. Season.

4. Cook pasta in the reserved vegetable water until al dente. Transfer directly to the pan with tongs, carrying pasta water with it.

5. Toss over medium heat until saucy — add more pasta water as needed.

6. Off heat: add Parmigiano, lemon zest, juice, mint. Toss again. Taste for salt.

Serve immediately with extra cheese.

**Notes:** Mint is not optional. It lifts everything and connects the green vegetables.
