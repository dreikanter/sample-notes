# Journal — eve of launch

May 14. Tomorrow morning the API goes public.

The checklist from [[20250507_1806]] is complete. The dry run on Friday went cleanly — no blocking issues, one minor timing problem with the announcement email template that Marcus fixed in ten minutes. The monitoring is up and configured with the SLOs from [[20250513_1811]]. The runbook is written and distributed.

At 6pm today we did a final team call — 20 minutes, nothing to discuss really, just a calibration check before tomorrow. Priya has that focused calm she gets before things she's prepared for. Marcus was already thinking about what we do with user feedback next week. Selin has the monitoring dashboards on a second screen.

I've been on-call for things like this before, but not often for work I built substantially from scratch. There's a particular feeling to it — not quite pride (too complicated for that word) and not quite anxiety (too prepared for that). Something like standing at the edge of something before stepping off.

Made the braised short ribs again tonight (see [[20250417_1789]]). Good decision — it's a long, hands-off cook that fills the apartment with warmth and smell. Had it with mashed potato and a green salad and a glass of the Bourgogne rouge from the tasting back in March.

Tomorrow: wake up early, check the monitoring dashboards, be at the laptop at 7:45am. The announcement goes at 8:00am PT.

It's ready.
