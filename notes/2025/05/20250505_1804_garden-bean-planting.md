# Garden — bean and cucumber planting

May 5. Close enough to the frost date to feel safe. Soil temperature at 4 inches: 15°C, which is warm enough for beans and cucumbers to germinate reliably.

## What I planted

**Bed 3:**

*Pole beans (Kentucky Wonder):* Planted 6 inches apart in a double row on either side of the trellis. Pressed seeds 1.5 inches deep. Covered with soil, firmed down. No inoculant this time — used it last year and didn't notice a difference, but the soil should have legacy rhizobium populations now.

*Cucumbers (Marketmore):* 4 hills at the south end of bed 3, 3 seeds per hill. Will thin to 1-2 plants per hill once established.

**Companion notes:**

Left a gap at the center of the trellis where I'm planning to add climbing nasturtium — these are said to confuse aphids and also look good. Seeds go in next week.

## The trellis

Built it two weeks ago but better late than not at all. 8-foot bamboo poles at 18-inch intervals, cross-braced with 6-foot poles, connected with jute twine in a diamond pattern. Sturdy enough to support a full bean harvest. Same design as three years ago.

## Timing comparison

Last year I planted beans on May 2 and cucumbers May 8. This year both at once — simpler and the timing is fine.

## Expectations

Beans: germinate in 7-10 days. First harvest approximately 60 days from planting.
Cucumbers: germinate in 5-7 days. First fruit approximately 55 days.

Target first harvest: early July.
