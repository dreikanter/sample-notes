# Garden — last frost date passed

May 10 — the historical last frost date for this area. A milestone rather than a guarantee, but it means the Wall-o-Water cells can come off the tomatoes soon. The forecast shows minimum temperatures staying above 8°C for the next two weeks.

## Current state

**Tomatoes:** The Sungolds have outgrown the Wall-o-Water cells. The plants are now 35-40cm with flower buds forming on the first truss. Removed one cell today, will remove the other two over the next few days to let them adjust. The Brandywine is smaller but has the same flower bud development.

**Peppers:** Still slow, but this is normal. They won't accelerate until temperatures are consistently warm. Both Shishito plants are setting new leaves.

**Bed 2:** The spinach is ready for sustained harvest. Cut about 200g of outer leaves today and made a simple salad: spinach, shaved radish from the market, soft-boiled egg, a mustard vinaigrette. First garden produce of the year — this is always a good moment.

**Bed 3:** Beans germinated! First sprouts visible on four of six positions. Cucumbers: one hill showing first seed leaves, the other three not yet.

## Coming up

Remove wall-o-waters from all tomatoes this week. Side-dress all beds with compost. Set up drip irrigation before summer — this year I'm doing it properly rather than hand-watering everything.
