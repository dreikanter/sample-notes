# Sourdough bake log — February 1

Attempted the high-hydration country loaf again after a two-week break. Starter had been in the fridge and I refreshed it twice over two days before using.

**Formula:**
- Bread flour: 400g
- Whole wheat flour: 50g
- Water: 345g (76% hydration)
- Salt: 9g
- Levain: 90g (20% of flour weight, 100% hydration)

**Timeline:**
- 8:00am: mixed autolyse (flour + water)
- 8:30am: added levain and salt, slap and fold until incorporated
- 9:00am–1:00pm: bulk fermentation at 24°C, four sets of stretch and fold at 30-minute intervals, then left alone
- 1:00pm: pre-shape, 30 min bench rest
- 1:30pm: final shape, into banneton, into fridge
- Next morning (7:30am): baked from cold at 250°C in a Dutch oven, 20 min lid on, 25 min lid off

**Result:**
Good ear, decent bloom. Crumb was slightly gummy near the bottom — likely under-fermented. The crust was well-colored. Flavor was good, mild sourness.

Next time I'll push bulk to 4.5 hours or wait for a 50% rise, whichever comes first. Also considering dropping hydration to 73% to make shaping easier.

Good reference for troubleshooting crumb structure: [https://www.theperfectloaf.com/guides/](https://www.theperfectloaf.com/guides/)
