# Seedling update — February 15

Started the first seeds of the season today. Per the garden plan from [[20250203_1722]], peppers need the longest indoor time, so I'm starting them now even though it's late.

## What I started today

- **Peppers:** 6 cells of Shishito, 4 cells of Anaheim. Used seed-starting mix, not regular potting soil — better drainage, lower nutrient load (young seedlings don't need much fertilizer).

- **Tomatoes:** 4 cells of Sungold, 4 cells of Brandywine. These go out in late April, which gives them 10 weeks indoor — slightly longer than needed but won't hurt.

## Setup

Using a seedling heat mat set to 24°C. Bottom heat accelerates germination significantly for peppers especially — they're slow without it. No grow light yet; the south-facing window gets about 5 hours of direct light per day in February which is borderline. Will set up the LED bar if they start to etiolate.

## Germination expectations

- Tomatoes: 5-10 days
- Peppers: 10-21 days (wide range; shishito tends faster than larger peppers)

## Notes on last year's germination

Last year I got poor germination on peppers because I started them in cold soil without bottom heat and lost three weeks to bad germination rates. Heat mat was the fix.

Good reference for seed starting schedules: [https://extension.unh.edu/resource/starting-seeds-indoors](https://extension.unh.edu/resource/starting-seeds-indoors)
