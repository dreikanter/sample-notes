# Garden plan — spring 2025

Finally sat down and sketched out the raised bed layout. Three beds, each 4ft × 8ft. South-facing fence line gets the most sun.

## Bed 1 (full sun, south)

- Tomatoes × 3 (one cherry, two slicing — likely Sungold and Brandywine)
- Basil interplanted at the base of each tomato
- One zucchini at the east end — will sprawl but manageable

## Bed 2 (partial shade, middle)

- Salad mix in succession plantings every 3 weeks starting in April
- One row of beets (Detroit Dark Red, reliable in this climate)
- Spinach in the shadier north end

## Bed 3 (mixed sun, west)

- Pole beans on trellis at north edge
- Cucumbers (Marketmore)
- One or two pepper plants if I start them indoors by mid-February, which is already late

## Direct sow schedule

- Beets, spinach, salad mix: as soon as soil is workable (~late March here)
- Beans, cucumbers: after last frost (around May 10 historically)
- Tomatoes, peppers: start indoors in February, transplant in late April with protection

## Notes

Last year the zucchini overwhelmed the bed by July. This time I'm cutting it to one plant and being ruthless with lateral growth.

Good soil amendment timing reference from the extension service: [https://extension.umn.edu/vegetable-gardening](https://extension.umn.edu/vegetable-gardening)
