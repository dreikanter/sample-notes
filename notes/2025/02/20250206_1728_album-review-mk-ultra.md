# Album review — Mk.gee, Two Star & the Dream Police

Listened through twice this week. Short album, about 35 minutes. Mk.gee (Michael Gordon) has been building a following in guitar-oriented indie circles for a few years, and this feels like a genuine arrival rather than a breakthrough in the hype-cycle sense.

The sound is hard to place cleanly. There's a fuzzed-out guitar palette that nods toward shoegaze but the production is too crisp and digital for that label to fit. Some tracks feel closer to early R&B production — the bass is warm, the drums are flat in the mix, vocals float. "Candy" was the lead single and it's a good entry point, but the album rewards full listens.

Standout track for me is "Are You Looking Up" — it does something interesting in the bridge where the guitar drops out entirely and you're left with just voice and a faint shimmer of reverb. The return feels earned.

The lyrics are elliptical without being precious. Themes of longing, disorientation, impermanence, but stated obliquely enough that they don't announce themselves.

Weak point: the album loses focus in the middle stretch. Tracks five through seven blur together in a way that feels unintentional rather than atmospheric.

Overall: one of the more interesting guitar records I've heard in a while. Not transformative but honest and considered.
