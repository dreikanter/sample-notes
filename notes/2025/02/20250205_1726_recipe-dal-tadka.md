# Recipe — dal tadka

Reliable weeknight dal. Requires a pressure cooker or Instant Pot for the lentils, otherwise add 20-30 minutes.

## Ingredients (serves 4)

**Dal:**
- 200g red lentils (masoor dal), rinsed
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1-inch piece ginger, grated
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1.5 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- Salt to taste
- 600ml water

**Tadka (temper):**
- 3 tbsp ghee or neutral oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 3 dried red chillies
- 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
- Pinch of asafoetida (hing)

## Method

1. Cook lentils with water, onion, tomatoes, ginger, garlic, turmeric, and salt under pressure for 8 minutes. Release naturally, stir well. Add coriander powder.

2. For tadka: heat ghee in a small pan until shimmering. Add cumin seeds and let them splutter. Add garlic slices and fry until golden at the edges. Add chillies, asafoetida, and chilli powder. Stir for 30 seconds.

3. Pour tadka over the dal immediately. Stir through or leave the sizzle on top — matter of preference.

Serve with rice or roti, a wedge of lime, and raw onion.

**Notes:** The second day is better. Dal thickens overnight; thin with water when reheating.
