# Travel notes — Portland, OR weekend

Spent the weekend in Portland. February is not the obvious time to go — it rained both days — but the city is less crowded than in summer and the restaurants were easy to get into.

## Food

**Tusk** (dinner, Friday): Vegetable-forward menu, Oregon wine list. Had the braised celery root, which I would not normally order but it was excellent. Also the anchovy toast as a starter. Long room, noisy, good buzz.

**St. Honoré Boulangerie** (both mornings): Proper croissants. The butter flavor is pronounced without being heavy. Came back Saturday and had the kouign-amann. Line was out the door by 8:30am both days.

**Luce** (lunch, Saturday): Italian, casual. The pasta is made in-house and the ribollita was thick and correct. Got a table without waiting at 12:30, which surprised me.

## What I wandered into

Powell's Books on Burnside took two hours and I bought five books, which was the expected outcome. The room arrangement is labyrinthine in a way that is half infuriating and half charming.

The Japanese Garden is worth a visit even in winter and rain — maybe especially then. The fog was sitting low on the hills behind the garden and the bare maples looked good against it.

## Notes for next time

- Rent a bike if weather allows
- The east side neighborhoods (Alberta, Mississippi) are worth a full afternoon
- Book Tusk further in advance; they were full Saturday
