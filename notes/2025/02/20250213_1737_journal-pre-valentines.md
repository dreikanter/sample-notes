# Journal — February 13

Tomorrow is Valentine's Day and the grocery store was chaotic this evening. The flower section had been picked over — mostly tulips left, which are fine, but the roses were down to expensive arrangements and a few sad singles. I bought tulips, a deep burgundy variety, and they look good in the blue ceramic jug on the counter.

Made dinner at home rather than fighting for a restaurant table. Pasta al limone (third iteration this month, getting closer — see [[20250212_1735]]), a salad with shaved fennel and blood orange, and a simple dessert: dark chocolate pieces with flaky salt and hazelnuts.

The blood oranges are in season and extraordinary right now. Found them at the farmers market on Saturday — from a farm in the Central Valley. The flesh is a deep ruby with no intermediate orange, and the flavor is more tart and complex than navel oranges. Worth seeking out while they're available (usually through March, maybe into April in good years).

Listened to a playlist of late-night piano jazz while cooking. The apartment felt genuinely warm, both literally (finally fixed the radiator issue from last week) and in the atmospheric sense that's harder to engineer.

Some nights the ordinary elements align correctly. This was one.
