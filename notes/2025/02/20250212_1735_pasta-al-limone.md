# Pasta al limone — cooking notes

Made this three times this month trying to nail the emulsion. The technique matters more than the ingredient list.

## Ingredients (serves 2)

- 200g spaghetti or linguine
- 1 lemon (zest and juice — about 3 tbsp juice)
- 60g unsalted butter, cold, cut into small cubes
- 30g Parmigiano-Reggiano, finely grated
- 2 tbsp reserved pasta water (have more ready)
- Black pepper, generous

## Method

The key is heat management. You're building an emulsion of butter, lemon juice, and starchy pasta water, and it will break if you rush it.

1. Salt the pasta water heavily. Cook pasta 2 minutes less than package says (it finishes in the pan).

2. While pasta cooks, heat a wide pan over medium. Add cold butter and let it melt slowly — you don't want it to brown.

3. Add lemon zest. Stir briefly.

4. Reserve a big cup of pasta water before draining. Add the pasta to the pan directly from the pot, carrying some water with it.

5. Toss vigorously over medium-low heat. Add pasta water a splash at a time. The sauce should coat the pasta and look glossy, not wet.

6. Remove from heat. Add lemon juice and Parmigiano. Toss rapidly. The residual heat will melt the cheese. If it seizes, add more pasta water.

7. Black pepper, taste for salt, serve immediately.

**What goes wrong:** Too high heat breaks the emulsion into greasy pools. Too little pasta water makes it clump. Serve fast — it tightens as it sits.
