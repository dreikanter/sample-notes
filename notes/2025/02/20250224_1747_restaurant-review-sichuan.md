# Restaurant review — Chengdu Memory

Went to Chengdu Memory on a Wednesday evening with two friends. Neither of them had eaten much Sichuan food before, which made it a good test for whether the restaurant could handle unfamiliar diners and whether the food was approachable while still being authentic.

## What we ordered

- **Mapo tofu:** Excellent. The ma la (numbing-spicy) balance was correct — enough Sichuan peppercorn to produce the characteristic tingle on the lips, enough chili for heat but not pain. The tofu was silken and the ground pork wasn't skimped on. Better than most places I've tried.

- **Dan dan noodles:** Very good. The sesame paste base was rich, the chili oil present but not overwhelming. Slightly sweet compared to versions I've had in other cities, but intentionally so.

- **Husband and wife beef (fu qi fei pian):** Thin slices of beef and tendon in a red oil. Excellent texture contrast between the meat and tendon. Needed more sesame for my taste.

- **Dry-fried green beans:** Solid. Not remarkable.

## Notes on the space

Noisy and small. The tables are close together and the ventilation could be better. Bring the kind of appetite that doesn't mind communal proximity.

## Overall

One of the better Sichuan options I've found locally. Worth returning to for mapo tofu alone. Will try the hot pot next time.
