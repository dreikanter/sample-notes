# Journal — slow Monday, February 10

Nothing notable today, which is in itself somewhat notable. The week started without any fires. Inbox was calm. Meetings were one short check-in and a 30-minute design review that ended early.

Used the unexpected space to work through a backlog of small tasks: updated three API integration tests that had been failing intermittently (race condition on the test fixtures, fixed with a proper async barrier), reviewed two PRs that had been waiting since Thursday, and finally wrote the internal post about the pipeline profiling work from last month (see [[20250201_1719]] for the weekly where this came up).

In the afternoon I walked a longer route to the grocery store, which took me past the old cinema that's been converted into an events venue. There was a sign advertising a jazz night on the weekend. I've been meaning to go to more live music but rarely follow through. Maybe this time.

Made a simple dinner — beans and greens, olive oil, garlic, good bread. Ate slowly without reading or looking at a screen, which I don't do often enough. The beans were from a can, Rancho Gordo's cooked tepary, which hold their texture in a way that makes canned beans almost worthwhile.

Not every day has an arc. Some days are just time passing in a reasonable direction. This was one.
