# Seed germination update — February 26

Checking in on the seedlings from [[20250215_1739]].

**Tomatoes (Sungold):** All 4 cells germinated. The seedlings are about 2cm tall with first true leaves just appearing. They look slightly leggy — need more light. Setting up the LED bar this evening.

**Tomatoes (Brandywine):** 3 of 4 germinated. One cell still no sign. Will give it another week before replanting.

**Peppers (Shishito):** 5 of 6 cells have sprouted. Slow but expected. The heat mat made a difference — germination was much more consistent than last year.

**Peppers (Anaheim):** 2 of 4 germinated. Disappointing. The seeds were from 2023, which may have reduced viability. Will replant the two empty cells with fresh seed from the packet I just ordered.

## Light setup

Went with a 24-inch LED bar mounted 4 inches above the top of the trays. Running it 14 hours on, 10 hours off. The light timer was already set from last year.

## Notes

The leggy tomatoes confirm what I've read about seedlings under window light in February: even south-facing windows aren't sufficient at this latitude. The LED bar should firm them up within a week.

Will thin to one seedling per cell once the true leaves are established. Difficult to do when they all look so vulnerable.
