# Observation — city fog on February 5

Woke up to a heavy fog this morning, the kind that erases the far end of the street entirely. By 8am the buildings two blocks over were just outlines, gray on gray. The traffic signals looked like small bruises in the air.

The fog lingered until nearly noon, which is unusual here. Usually it burns off by 9 or 10 in winter. I walked to the coffee shop instead of cycling because the visibility wasn't good enough to trust drivers at intersections.

Walking through fog like this produces a particular effect on ambient sound. The city was quieter than usual — not silent, but muffled, as if everything were behind glass. You could hear individual footsteps on the sidewalk from half a block away, but traffic noise was damped. I've read that fog absorbs high-frequency sound better than low-frequency, which would explain why the bass rumble of trucks still came through while voices and birdsong felt distant.

Came across a delivery truck idling in the crosswalk and couldn't see around it at all. The driver was eating something and listening to something loud. He didn't look up.

The coffee shop was warm and bright by contrast. The windows had fogged over from the inside. I stayed longer than intended.

Good day for indoor work. Didn't accomplish much structurally but had the feeling of being exactly in the right place.
