# Fermentation log — kimchi batch 3

Third attempt at kimchi. The first two were edible but too salty and under-fermented (I was impatient). This batch I'm doing properly.

## What I made

Traditional baechu-kimchi (napa cabbage). No shortcuts on the gochugaru — bought the real thing from a Korean grocery, not the generic chili flakes from the supermarket. The color and aroma are completely different.

## Process notes

**Day 0 (Feb 20):**
- Quartered one large napa cabbage (~1.5kg after trimming)
- Salted between layers with coarse salt (2% of cabbage weight = 30g)
- Pressed under a plate with a weight for 2 hours until limp
- Rinsed three times, squeezed dry

**Paste:**
- 4 tbsp gochugaru
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 2 tbsp fish sauce (omit for vegan version)
- 1 tsp sugar
- 3 green onions, cut into 1-inch pieces

Mixed paste, coated cabbage thoroughly by hand (wear gloves), packed tightly into a wide-mouth jar.

**Day 1:** Left at room temperature. Already starting to smell right.
**Day 3:** Pressed down to release gas, tasted — still raw and bright. Moved to fridge.

Will taste again at day 7 and day 14. Last batch got good at day 10.
